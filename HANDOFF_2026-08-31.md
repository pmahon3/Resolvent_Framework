# TASK B — the countable-carrier question

**Phase 4 work. Well-posed, and cheaper to attack than anything else on the board.**

`witness_iff_kernel_of_countable_singletons`: on a countable carrier containing
all its singletons, a witness exists **iff** there is a finitely coherent pattern
with empty kernel. So:

> Is there a countable σ-class containing all its singletons, carrying a finitely
> coherent pattern with empty kernel?

* **Yes** ⇒ Ψ holds on a *countable* carrier, and the whole Ulam/ω₁ apparatus was
  unnecessary.
* **No** ⇒ shovel #4 gets a genuine exact slice: **Φ holds on every countable
  carrier containing its singletons.**

## B.1 The size axis it came from

Four rungs, all proved on the standard axioms:

| carrier | what happens |
|---|---|
| finite | no witness at all — finite additivity **is** σ-additivity (`no_witness_of_finite`) |
| countable + a partition | every σ-state is true on exactly one part (`existsUnique_part_of_countable_partition`) |
| countable **+ all singletons** | every σ-state is carrier-Dirac; clause (ii) is **vacuous** (`isDiracOn_of_countable_singletons`) |
| ω₁ | clause (ii) has content |

**The inversion.** `thm:rigidity` spends an Ulam matrix and uncountability to
conclude `IsDiracOn`. On countable carriers that conclusion is *free*. So ω₁ is
not buying rigidity — rigidity is cheap when Ω is countable. What ω₁ buys is the
other half: coherence with empty kernel at all.

**The escape route between rows 3 and 4 is withholding singletons**, which is
exactly non-intersection-closure. Cross-check that holds: Navara–Pták's published
Example has a countable carrier with a *non-Dirac* σ-state, so by row 3 it must
omit a singleton — and it does, a singleton there being an intersection of two
generators the class does not contain.

## B.2 Where to look

`ConcreteOMLPatterns.lean` carries the whole axis. Adjacent, and proved:
`interClosed_iff_maxBlock_unique` — a carrier is intersection-closed **iff** it
has a unique maximal block.

Do not reach for `descentClass` (`ConcreteDescent.lean`): it has 2^ℕ blocks, so it
is not a countable-carrier candidate.

# TASK A — bridge the two big DAG components

Still open, still unmet. Attempt it only after B, or if B stalls.

## A.1 The obstruction (understand this before proposing anything)

The 86/82 split is genuine: **no σ-essential module imports the query-system core,
and none of the core imports σ-essential.** Verified mechanically — each lane's
transitive import reach contains nothing outside itself. There is no existing
dependency to expose; a bridge must be new mathematics.

A `QuerySystem` posits a single space `Ω` of coherent families. Every observable
is a function on that one space, so `Cyl i A` are subsets of one set and `sigmaQ`
is a **Boolean** σ-algebra. The σ-essential lane's subject is a carrier that is a
proper σ-class — a Dynkin system that is *not* intersection-closed
(`carrier_not_interClosed`), because that is the only place a witness can live
(`boolean_no_witness`).

So any bridge of the form "take the query system's events as the σ-essential
carrier" is dead, and dead trivially. **A real bridge has to break the assumption
that produces the Boolean algebra.**

## A.2 The banned route, and the standard it sets

`Quarantine.lean` (2026-08-25) did close the split and was removed the same day.
It proved: a query's outcome space carries a `MeasurableSpace`; σ-algebras are
intersection-closed; therefore `boolean_no_witness` forbids a witness on one. The
*edge* came from `delayQuery_no_witness`, a pure instantiation of the general
theorem at a delay object. Removing the module put the component count straight
back — proof that nothing but the naming held it.

**The standard: an edge must be a theorem in which the two lanes *constrain each
other*.** A theorem whose statement merely mentions both sides is not a bridge. If
you are instantiating a general result at an object from the other lane, stop.

## A.3 The candidate edge (short — most of it exists)

Directedness is exactly what makes the cylinders closed under intersection:
`Cyl i A ∩ Cyl j B` is again a cylinder at any common refinement `k`, by
coherence; with no such `k` there is no reason it is a cylinder at all.
`UpperDirected` is a *predicate*, not a structure field, so non-directed query
systems are already legitimate objects — nothing needs redefining.

> If `S` is upper-directed, the σ-class generated by `CylGen` is
> intersection-closed, hence carries no σ-essential witness.

Three parts, two already in place:

* `QuerySystem.isPiSystem_CylGen` — **proved**, `QuerySystem.lean:300`
* `S.sigmaQ = MeasurableSpace.generateFrom S.CylGen` — by `rfl`, `QuerySystem.lean:1571`
* Mathlib `MeasurableSpace.DynkinSystem.generateFrom_eq (hs : IsPiSystem s)` —
  `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/PiSystem.lean:671`

What remains is the composition and the hop to `boolean_no_witness`.

**Read the contrapositive — it is the payload:** a witness on a cylinder-generated
carrier *requires the query system to be non-directed*. That makes failure of
directedness — the subject of chapters 1–3, and what `not_seqUpperDirected` and
the Andersen–Jessen counterexample are about — a *necessary condition* for the
σ-essential phenomenon.

**The bar, unchanged (§A.5): if the proof reduces to "σ-algebras are
intersection-closed", it is the quarantine again.** The reason to think it is not:
the hypothesis discharged is `UpperDirected`, the query-system lane's central
predicate, not an object named from the σ-essential lane — which is precisely what
amendment 4(b) faulted the quarantine for.

## A.4 The hard half (open)

> Does some non-directed query system's cylinder σ-class actually **fail** to be
> intersection-closed — and can it carry a witness?

Necessary is not sufficient: non-directedness removes the reason the σ-class is
Boolean, it does not supply a witness. First moves:

- Both non-trivial query systems in the repo are **directed**, so neither can be
  the example — `delayQuerySystem.upperDirected` is proved, and Andersen–Jessen is
  indexed by `ℕ` with `≤`. A non-directed one must be built.
- Smallest interesting shape: two queries with no common refinement (a genuine
  pair of incompatible contexts). Is the σ-class their cylinders generate
  intersection-closed? If it is for *every* such small system, that is a result,
  and a negative one worth recording.
- `ConcreteOMLBlocks.lean` / `ConcreteOMLPatterns.lean` carry the block/pattern
  layer; a query-system-generated OMP would land there.

## A.5 Types and bars — declare before auditing

(`notes/programme/contribution_evaluation.md`)

- **A.3 alone**: most likely Type 6, or a small Type 1. Its value is the
  contrapositive; its bar is that the π–λ step does real work rather than dressing
  up `boolean_no_witness`.
- **A.4**, if it produces a witness: Type 1, and large. If it proves no cylinder
  σ-class can fail intersection-closure: **Type 5**, and a good one — it would
  close the observational route to Φ permanently.

**A negative answer is a real result here.** Do not treat "no bridge found" as a
failure to work around. The split being genuinely unbridgeable is worth stating.

## A.6 Literature — before naming anything

The rule is *name things last*, and this area is crowded. Already recorded, do not
rediscover:

- `papers/reconstruction/notes/PRIOR_ART_VERDICT.md` — **Abramsky–Brandenburger
  2011** (NJP 13 113036, arXiv:1102.0264) owns the definitional core: presheaf
  compatibility = empirical adequacy, global section = realisability, contextuality
  = the obstruction to a global section. Their §5 relates no-signalling to
  extendability by a *signed* measure.
- **Vorob'ev 1962** owns the exact iff for when every locally consistent family
  extends: regularity of the complex, equivalently α-acyclicity. **BFMY 1983** is
  the database mirror.

Why it matters: "local consistency without a global section" is the shared shape of
*both* lanes — compatible marginals that do not extend (AJ), and a finitely
coherent pattern with no σ-additive extension (the witness). **That unification is
known.** A bridge whose content is "both lanes are instances of sheaf
contextuality" is Type 6 at best and probably published. A.3 is different in kind:
it is about *when the carrier is Boolean*, a measure-theoretic π–λ fact, not a
presheaf-theoretic one.

Before claiming anything about A.4, check the quantum-logic literature on when a
family of Boolean σ-algebras pastes into a non-Boolean OMP (Kalmbach,
Pták–Pulmannová, Navara). The lane already cites Binder–Navara and Navara–Pták, so
the neighbourhood is known and the pasting construction is standard. The question
is whether *observational* pasting gives anything abstract pasting does not.

## 9. Discipline (the parts that have bitten this repo)

- **The standing hazard: statements that compile and prove nothing.** Four in one
  week, each caught by reading a source rather than by a gate — a vacuous
  existentially-bound lemma; a "concreteness" predicate true of every
  `DynkinSystem`; a `full` field needing an undischargeable hypothesis; a docstring
  gloss that was not the source's definition. Before adding a node: *does the
  statement say what the paper says?* A clean axiom receipt does not answer that.
- **Check inhabitation.** An implication with an unsatisfiable hypothesis proves
  nothing. The σ-essential open core sat over an unsatisfiable predicate for weeks,
  making `TargetA_sharp` — a named conjecture — provably false. Introduce a
  predicate, exhibit something satisfying it.
- **Search the shelf before the web.** Both papers the previous handoff logged as
  unread leads were already in `notes/literature_review/literature/` with recorded
  verdicts. Now a rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Edges follow the Lean, never the prose.** Add a `\uses` only when the proof
  actually consumes the thing.
- **Node text is the mathematics, not narration.** If trimming a node's rhetoric,
  indexing, or commentary leaves nothing, the node goes.
- Never push without being asked.

# Claim-status ledger: latticehood propagation

*Recorded 2026-08-04. These entries propagate the verdict in
[`latticehood_prior_art.md`](latticehood_prior_art.md); they do not add a
literature search or a mathematical argument.*

## Entry LP-STATUS-1 — the three-way latticehood family

**Former claim.** Across pasting, regularity, and sharpness, latticehood forces
liftability and hence tameness; the family is the corpus's single completing
conjecture.

**Former status.** **OPEN CONJECTURE / PROGRAMME HEURISTIC.** No source called
it a theorem, but the frontier map, shovel plan, taxonomy registry, and spine
all advertised it as the theory-completing target.

**Decisive check.** `L(H)` for `dim H >= 3` is a complete orthomodular lattice
and has no dispersion-free state by Kochen--Specker. Therefore latticehood
cannot imply the sharpness arrow. In addition, the corpus defines liftability
only for regularity, through `Phi`; the three arrows are not instances of one
typed predicate.

**New status.** **REFUTED UNDER THE LITERAL READING — CLOSED AS A CORPUS
CONJECTURE.**

**Evidence grade.** **CLASSICAL COUNTERCHECK + DEFINITION AUDIT.** No new
mathematics. The relevant classical theorem and exact corpus definitions are
documented in the prior-art audit.

**Disposition.** Retain the three-transition filtration as organising
architecture only. Do not describe latticehood as their common boundary and do
not reopen the family without first defining a new common predicate that avoids
the Hilbert-lattice countercheck.

## Entry LP-STATUS-2 — OML finite-trace sigma-liftability

**Claim.** Every concrete, sigma-complete, non-Boolean, essentially irreducible
orthomodular lattice satisfies the finite-trace predicate `Phi`.

**Former status.** **OPEN — LOAD-BEARING REGULARITY CASE OF THE FORMER FAMILY.**

**New status.** **OPEN — WHOLE SURVIVING THEORY-FRONT TARGET.** The mathematical
status did not change; its programme role did. Skeletons A/B, the three-front
convergence, and the T4 relay programme remain evidence and reductions for
this exact question.

**Evidence grade.** **PRECISE OPEN QUESTION WITH HAND/EXECUTABLE PARTIAL
RESULTS.** No proof or counterexample is claimed. The finite-relay record is
exhaustive only in its named bounded grammars.

**Disposition.** State the question directly as `latticehood => Phi`, without
claiming that its solution unifies pasting or sharpness.

## Entry LP-TERM-1 — `VDR` versus the filtration endpoint

**Former claim/notation.** The paper displayed
`S superset S_sigma superset S_df` while defining standalone VDR by existence
of a dispersion-free state, without an explicit sigma-additivity clause.

**Former status.** **AMBIGUOUS ON INFINITE CARRIERS.**

**New status.** **CORRECTED — TYPED SPLIT.** Standalone
`VDR(A) <=> S_df(A) is nonempty`; the filtration endpoint is
`S_df^sigma(A) := S_df(A) intersect S_sigma(A)`.

**Evidence grade.** **DEFINITIONAL HYGIENE.** This is a notation correction,
not a theorem.

**Disposition.** Use `VDR_sigma` or `S_df^sigma` whenever sigma-additivity is
required. The source-specific `EA`/`PR` pairs are likewise fenced in
[`coherence_terminology.md`](coherence_terminology.md).

**NON-EXISTENCE** failure. It is not a counterexample to complete OML

**Original outcome.** **OBSERVATION MATCHED; OLD CLAIM NOT ADJUDICATED.** The

**Pre-registered prediction.** No Boolean algebra, OMP, or OML admits faithful
embeddings of both puncture legs agreeing on the abstract clopen interface and

**Proposed falsifier named in advance.** An explicit ambient algebra and two
injective embeddings agreeing on `Clop(C)`, without identifying the nonzero
singleton with zero, under which the common family has one ambient meet.

**Falsifier validity audit.** **VALID FOR ORDINARY BOOLEAN EMBEDDINGS; INVALID
FOR THE CLAIMED SITE-REGULAR EQUIVALENT.** A Boolean embedding preserves finite
operations, not existing countable meets. Hence the registered falsifier tests
repair by arbitrary enlargement. It does not test whether the incident site
inclusions are regular. Those two tests are not equivalent.

**Observation.** The Stone-ultrafilter amalgam constructed in
[`site_regularity_intrinsic_and_state_coupling.md`](site_regularity_intrinsic_and_state_coupling.md)

\[
  \operatorname{Borel}(C\setminus\{i\})
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \operatorname{Borel}(C\setminus\{j\})
\]

over `Clop(C)` inside a complete powerset Boolean algebra. The common sequence
has ambient meet equal to the embedded `j`-leg singleton over `i`, while the
embedded `i`-leg zero remains zero.

**Outcome.** **ARTIFACT; FALSIFIER FIRED** for the registered arbitrary-ambient
test. The proposed non-existence/disagreement species distinction is
**REFUTED** at that scope.

**Separate regularity verdict.** **IMPOSSIBLE.** In every common realization,
the intersection site has meet zero for the sequence, while the `j`-leg has
meet the nonzero singleton. No realization of these fixed legs can make that

equivalent to “every site is regular.”

**Evidence grade.** Explicit hand construction plus an order-theoretic

## Entry ULAM-B-1 — completion/state coupling test

**Claim under test.** Completing the Ulam witness changes the status or
statability of `PsiOML`.

**Family-identity prediction.** The missing-GLB family is the same family, or
is derived from the same family, as the state-extension obstruction.

**Observation.** Write

\[
 A=M_1\times\{0,1\},\qquad
 B=M_1\times\{0,2\},\qquad
 C=M_1\times\{1,2\}.
\]

The missing meet is forced by the pair `A,B` and equals
`D_0=A\cap B=M_1\times\{0\}`. The state-side value-one family is exactly
`A,B,C`; thus the structural pair is a two-member subfamily of the state-side
triple. The Ulam row/cell families used for sigma-state rigidity are different.

**Fixed-candidate completion outcome.** **COHERENCE DESTROYED.** Adjoining
`D_0` already prevents any finitely additive two-valued state from assigning
one to all of `A,B,C`. The same local pattern remains statable, and absence of
a sigma-additive extension persists by restriction, but `FinitelyCoherent`
becomes false. Hence `IsSigmaEssentialL` changes from true on `L_1` to false on
every same-base concrete `MeetsExist` extension.

**Scope audit.** **PREMISE FALSE.** `PsiOML` is not a proposition about `L_1`.
It is the closed existential

\[
 \exists\,\Omega,d,\mathcal B,s_0,\quad
   \operatorname{MeetsExist}(d)\ \wedge\

It remains well-formed when one particular carrier is completed. Neither
`L_1` nor its completion realizes the conjunction: the former fails
`MeetsExist`, while the latter fails `IsSigmaEssentialL`. Hence this completion
calculation supplies no witness and does not adjudicate the closed existential.

**Phase B verdict on `PsiOML`.** **CO-LOCATED; OPEN — NOT ADJUDICATED.** The

**Current disposition.** **FIXED-CANDIDATE ROUTE CLOSED; GLOBAL `PsiOML`

**Evidence grade.** Explicit hand proof against read-only Lean definitions; no

## Entry R-SA-1 — regularity / sigma-additivity pullback gate

*Registered by the gate-check prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test (Lemma R).** Pulling a sigma-additive state back along a
sigma-regular OMP embedding preserves sigma-additivity; for an arbitrary OMP
embedding, failure at an orthogonal sequence is exactly positive state mass on
the proposed gap

\[
  g=\left(\bigvee_n\phi(x_n)\vee
    \phi\!\left(\bigvee_n x_n\right)'\right)'.
\]

**Registered adversarial prediction.** The lemma was treated as likely false,
with the first proposed failure point the existence or orthomodular
interpretation of `g` in an OMP, where arbitrary meets need not exist. Failure
of any one of the six stated OMP steps was designated a gate-closing result.

**Outcome.** **SURVIVED — SHARPENED TO AN EXACT IDENTITY.** If

\[
  x=\bigvee_n\phi(x_n),\qquad
  y=\phi\!\left(\bigvee_n x_n\right),
\]

then `x <= y`, the orthogonal join `x ∨ y'` exists, and the OMP
orthomodular law gives `y = x ∨ g` with `x ⊥ g`. Hence every sigma-additive
state `s` on the ambient OMP satisfies

\[
  s(y)=\sum_n s(\phi(x_n))+s(g).
\]

The pullback fails sigma-additivity at this family if and only if `s(g) > 0`.
Sigma-regularity makes `x = y` and `g = 0`, proving the pullback claim.

**Hypothesis audit.** For one family it is enough that its image join exists;
sigma-orthocompleteness of the ambient OMP is the uniform existence
hypothesis. Part (a) needs only preservation of countable orthogonal joins.
Full sigma-regularity is sufficient but stronger. Arbitrary regularity is not
used, and no ambient lattice structure is required.

**Novelty finding.** **FOLKLORE — NOT NOVEL.** The exact gap packaging was not
located as a named lemma in Pták–Pulmannová, Hamhalter, or Dvurečenskij, but the
claim is an immediate combination of standard sigma-homomorphism/state
definitions with the OMP orthogonal-difference law.

**Boolean calibration.** **UNIVERSAL NON-REGULARITY CLAIM REFUTED; ALL-STATES
LIFT VERIFIED.** The clopen-to-Baire inclusion is regular for finite Boolean
algebras, so it is not always non-sigma-regular. Whenever an existing
countable join has a topological gap, however, every finitely additive Boolean
state is the pullback of a sigma-additive Radon/Baire measure and its exact
additivity defect is the measure of that gap. All states lift; only states
charging a fixed gap fail there, and a Dirac measure on any point of a
nonempty gap supplies at least one such state.

**Status.** **CLOSED — VERIFIED AS FOLKLORE, WITH ONE BOOLEAN PREMISE
REFUTED.**

**Evidence grade.** Step-by-step hand proof plus primary-source checks; no
Lean formalization or code changes.

## Entry D-DIL-1 — sigma-additive dilation of arbitrary OMP states

*Registered by the Option 2 scoping prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.** For which OMPs \(P\) is every finitely additive state
\(m\) a pullback \(m=s\circ\phi\) along an OMP embedding
\(\phi:P\hookrightarrow Q\), with \(Q\) sigma-orthocomplete and \(s\)
sigma-additive?

**Registered adversarial prediction.** Treat a known theorem, a corpus-internal
answer, or a strictly stronger open completion problem as a successful
gate-closing outcome. Do not label D open merely because its exact wording was
not found.

**Outcome.** **PARTIALLY ANSWERED; GENERAL CASE OPEN — ADJACENT.** Navara,
Pták, and Rogalewicz, Theorem 2.2, provide a state-specific enlargement with a
unique finitely additive state, but not a sigma-complete carrier or a
sigma-additive state. Pták's 1987 appendix explicitly records the
sigma-completeness limitation of the older constructions, while
Harding–Wang Problem 2 still asks the adjacent state-free OML/OMP
sigma-completion question. (It also ranges over stateless OMPs, so it is not
literally a weaker assertion than D.) No general state-preserving
sigma-complete enlargement theorem or general counterexample was found in the
named monographs or the adjacent extension literature.

**Positive regimes.** D holds for Boolean algebras; for finite OMPs; by the
identity for a sigma-additive state on a sigma-complete carrier; for projection
lattices of von Neumann algebras without a type-\(I_2\) summand via
Bunce–Wright plus a faithful/GNS direct-sum representation; and for the
Dvurečenskij–Neubrunn–Pulmannová incomplete-inner-product-space example.

**Named open checkpoint.** After the finite identity regime, Navara's
sigma-orthocomplete non-Boolean OML \(\mathcal L_{\mathbb N}\), equipped with
the unique extension of a free-ultrafilter state from its
\(\mathcal P(\mathbb N)\) sublogic. The state is not sigma-additive; the
located results neither dilate it nor rule out a dilation. This is a concrete

**Programme status.** **OPEN — ADJACENT — PARKED; NO MONTHS-SCALE

**Evidence grade.** Primary-source literature gate plus hand deductions in

## Entry CP-REG-1 — infinitude versus clopen sigma-regularity

*Registered by the Option 2 scoping prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.**

\[
  \operatorname{Clopen}(S(B))\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Baire}(S(B))
  \text{ is non-sigma-regular}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  B\text{ is infinite}.
\]

**Registered prediction.** The prompt proposed the equivalence via the fact
that a compact \(P\)-space must be finite; the audit was instructed to seek a
counterexample.

**Outcome.** **REFUTED.** Let \(B\) be the finite–cofinite algebra on an
uncountable set \(I\). For any countable family in \(B\), its join exists only
when its set union is finite or cofinite, and in either case that union is the
join. Thus the clopen inclusion preserves every existing countable join and,
by complementation, every existing countable meet, although \(B\) is infinite.

**Error localization.** The compact-\(P\)-space fact is correct: if every
countable union of clopens is clopen, compactness forces the space to be
finite. Sigma-regularity is weaker; it tests only countable families whose
join already exists in \(B\). It does not assert closure under all countable
clopen unions.

**Status.** **CLOSED — CLAIM REFUTED; STRONGER TOPOLOGICAL FACT CONFIRMED.**

**Evidence grade.** Explicit hand counterexample and compact-\(P\)-space proof,
also banked in
[regularity_sigma_additivity_pullback.md](regularity_sigma_additivity_pullback.md).

## Entry Q-MECH-1 — quarantine versus the Boolean lift

*Registered by the Option 2 scoping prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.** Does the quarantine theorem use the same mechanism as
the Boolean state lift, and therefore already answer D for the corpus's own
carriers?

**Registered adversarial prediction.** Shared compact zero-dimensional
hypotheses may conceal either a genuinely shared proof step or a scope error
between Boolean charges and OMP states.

**Outcome.** **SAME BOOLEAN PREMEASURE ENGINE; OMP-SCOPE INFERENCE REFUTED.**
Part (ii) is exactly the finite-disjoint-clopen-cover argument: compactness
makes every charge on \(\operatorname{Clo}(X)\) a premeasure. Part (iii) is
Horn–Tarski finite-additive extension followed by that same step. Part (i)
uses compactness differently, through the finite-intersection property of a
clopen ultrafilter. Zero-dimensionality supplies the clopen Boolean base, and
metrizability identifies its generated sigma-algebra with the Borel
sigma-algebra.

**Exact D regime.** The corpus proves D for charges on Boolean subalgebras
\(B\subseteq\operatorname{Clo}(X)\) when \(X\) is compact,
zero-dimensional, and metrizable: take the ambient Borel sigma-algebra. It
also applies to an OMP state already known to extend to such a Boolean
envelope. It does not prove the required Boolean-envelope extension for an
arbitrary OMP state, so it does not answer general D on non-Boolean carriers.

**Status.** **CLOSED — CORPUS-INTERNAL MECHANISM; D REMAINS ADJACENT.**

**Evidence grade.** Line-by-line proof audit of Theorem thm:quarantine in the
paper of record, with the Boolean/OMP type boundary made explicit.

## Entry BW-SA-1 — blockwise sigma-additivity

*Registered by the wrap-up prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.** Under `MeetsExist`, a state is sigma-additive on the
carrier if and only if its restriction to every `IsMaxBlock` is
sigma-additive. The non-routine checkpoint was equality of the block join and
the carrier join for a countable orthogonal family.

**Join-agreement check.** A countable orthogonal family is pairwise disjoint,
hence pairwise compatible, and `exists_isMaxBlock_superset` puts it in one
maximal compatible family `M`. Its set union belongs to the carrier by
`DynkinSystem.has_iUnion_nat` and to `M` by `IsMaxBlock.iUnion_mem`. In either
the carrier order or the restricted order on `M`, every upper bound contains
each family member and therefore contains their set union. The same union is
thus the least upper bound in both places.

**Outcome.** **VERIFIED — IN FACT `MeetsExist` IS NOT NEEDED FOR THE
REDUCTION.** The join argument applies to arbitrary probability states. The
repository already contains its exact two-valued formal instance,
`isSigmaOn_carrier_iff_maxBlocks`. `MeetsExist` is needed only for the further
interpretation of `IsMaxBlock` as a genuine maximal Boolean sigma-field.

**Filtration consequence.** **NO LOCAL--GLOBAL COHERENCE GAP.** The inclusion
between finitely additive and sigma-additive states can still be strict, but
every failure is witnessed inside a single block; it is not caused by failure
to reconcile blockwise sigma-additivity.

**Status.** **CLOSED — VERIFIED.**

**Evidence grade.** Existing zero-sorry Lean theorem plus the explicit
order-theoretic join check above.

## Entry FC-GATE-1 — is the Phase B loss non-trivial?

*Registered as the mandatory gate by the wrap-up prompt on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.** Adjoining $D_0=A\cap B$ either adds a genuinely
inconsistent finite-additivity constraint to the Ulam core pattern, or merely
changes the carrier parameter in the definition of `FinitelyCoherent`.

**Pre-registered alternatives.** The non-trivial branch requires an explicit
new constraint that no extension of the old local data can satisfy. The
trivial branch fires if the earlier argument uses only the fact that a state
on the old carrier is not automatically a state on the new carrier.

**Observation.** Dynkin closure after adjoining `D_0` also contains
$D_1=A\setminus D_0$ and $D_2=B\setminus D_0$, with disjoint decompositions

\[
 A=D_0\sqcup D_1,\qquad B=D_0\sqcup D_2,\qquad C=D_1\sqcup D_2.
\]

If a two-valued finitely additive extension assigns one to `A,B,C`, then
`mu(D_0)=1` forces `mu(D_1)=mu(D_2)=0` and contradicts `mu(C)=1`, while
`mu(D_0)=0` forces `mu(D_1)=mu(D_2)=1`, contradicting their disjointness.

**Outcome.** **NON-TRIVIAL — GATE PASSED.** Carrier-relativity is real, but it
is not the proof: the added event forces new decompositions whose additivity
equations are inconsistent with the unchanged trace.

**Status.** **CLOSED — NON-TRIVIAL MECHANISM VERIFIED.**

**Evidence grade.** Direct finite case split against the read-only definition
of `FinitelyCoherent`.

## Entry RES-ANTI-1 — antitonicity in resolution

*Registered conditionally by the wrap-up prompt; activated after FC-GATE-1
passed on 2026-08-03.*

**Claim under test.** For a fixed underlying set and unchanged local
event-value data, coherence is antitone under inclusion of `DynkinSystem`
carriers: refinement can only destroy coherence and coarsening can only create
it.

**Falsifier named in advance.** A refinement that creates coherence where the
same local trace was incoherent.

**Falsifier hunt.** **NO GENUINE FALSIFIER; IMPOSSIBLE BY RESTRICTION.** If
`Carrier d` is contained in `Carrier e`, a finitely additive state on `e`
restricts to one on `d`: all old normalization, complement, and disjoint-pair
equations are among the fine equations. A sigma-additive state restricts for
the same reason, since every old disjoint countable family and its set union
are still present. Agreement with the fixed local trace is unchanged. Hence a
fine coherent extension would give a coarse one, contradicting the proposed
falsifier.

The same conclusion holds in the block presentation. Glue a hypothetical
fine `CompatibleBlockStates` family using `glueBlockStates` from
`BoundaryDescent.lean`, restrict the global state, and then restrict it to the
coarse maximal blocks. Recomputation of `IsMaxBlock` cannot reverse the
implication.

**Scope obstruction.** Coarse and fine maximal blocks do not have a canonical
one-to-one map: a coarse block may have several maximal fine extensions, and
distinct coarse blocks can merge after missing intersections are adjoined.
Thus an assertion that changes block indices or local data is not a
counterexample; it is a comparison of different predicates. There is no
broader block-resolution invariant in the present corpus.

**Outcome.** **PROVED AT FIXED-DATA SAME-BASE SCOPE.** Phase B witnesses strict
destruction of finite coherence. On W2's audited set of size `2^{omega_1}`,
the countable/co-countable example witnesses the complementary mechanism:
coarsening removes obligations and admits a nonprincipal sigma-state
unavailable at powerset resolution.

This uses W2 only as a strict state-space example. Nonprincipality itself is
representation-relative and is not part of the antitonicity theorem.

**Canonical-resolution verdict.** `P(Omega)` is the terminal same-base event
resolution and the strictest fixed-trace test; for a finite two-valued pattern
its verdict is exactly nonemptiness of the kernel. It is not a canonical
resolution of an abstract OMP/block atlas: it depends on the concrete base and
merges the atlas into one Boolean block. No representation-invariant canonical
block resolution is present.

**Corpus audit.** **NO THEOREM REQUIRES REPAIR.** Existing coherence theorems
fix `d`, `L`, or the protocol in their statements. Only W2's informal
robustness prose had omitted the resolution scope, and that prose was already
retracted.

**Status.** **CLOSED — CORPUS-INTERNAL RESTRICTION THEOREM; NO NEW PROGRAMME.**

**Evidence grade.** Direct restriction proof, checked against
`FinitelyCoherent`, `IsMaxBlock`, `overlapMeasurableSpace`, and
`BoundaryDescent`; no Lean changes.

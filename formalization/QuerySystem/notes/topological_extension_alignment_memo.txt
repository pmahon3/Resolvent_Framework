================================================================================
CONVENTION TRANSLATION DICTIONARY
================================================================================

The Lean formalization uses the OPPOSITE ordering convention from note.tex.

  note.tex:   le i j means "i is finer than j" (i is MORE informative)
              refinement maps go fine → coarse
              compatible marginals: map (π ij) (ν i) = ν j  (fine pushes to coarse)

  Lean:       le i j means "i is coarser than j" (j is MORE informative)
              refinement maps go coarse → fine  (π hij : Outcome_i → Outcome_j)
              compatible marginals: map (π hij) (ν i) = ν j  (coarse pushes to fine)

  Translation: Lean's le = note.tex's le reversed.
               Lean's LowerDirected = "any two queries have a common coarsening"
                                    = note.tex's "lower-directedness" (same name,
                                      same meaning, opposite arrow direction).

This is a consistent global reversal and causes no mathematical error, but must
be kept in mind when reading Lean proofs against manuscript statements.

FUTURE LEMMA FLAGGED
================================================================================

sigmaQ = Borel(Omega)

The current proof shows sigmaQ ⊆ Borel(Omega).  The reverse inclusion
Borel(Omega) ⊆ sigmaQ holds because the projective-limit topology on Omega
is generated by the evaluation maps (subtype of Pi = generated by projections),
and these projections generate exactly sigmaQ.

Proving this equality would confirm:

  "The observable σ-field is exactly the Borel structure of the
   observational horizon."

This is the correct mathematical form of the philosophical claim that the
observational horizon carries its own natural structure.

Target lemma name: TopologicalQuerySystem.sigmaQ_eq_borel

# Covered Leads

Correct, well-expressed presentations of known results. Useful
as reference and teaching material, not publishable as novel work.

## From this programme's explorations

- [realization_as_structure.md](realization_as_structure.md) —
  The algebra imposes no constraint on which ultrafilters are
  principal. Known from Stone duality (1936); our formulation
  is clear but not new.

- [stone_measure_pragmatics.md](stone_measure_pragmatics.md) —
  μ̂ on St(C) exists unconditionally; adds content at the
  infinitary level but St(C) may be intractable. Known.

- [definetti_stone_factoring.md](definetti_stone_factoring.md) —
  Exchangeable charges are automatically σ-additive (Hausdorff
  moments). The Stone factoring of de Finetti is trivial.
  Dead end for novel results; clean writeup of why.

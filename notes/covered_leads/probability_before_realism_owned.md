## The thesis (what was tested)

"Probability before realism": probability should be formulated FIRST over
observation protocols — a coherent family of local probability tables on
overlapping contexts, agreeing on overlaps — NOT assumed over a Kolmogorov
sample space (Ω,Σ,μ). A global Ω is not primitive; it is a REPRESENTATION
THEOREM earned only when local coherence ⟹ global realizability. Unearned ⟹
coherent observational probability with no global realizing space
(contextuality), or only finitely-additive realization, no σ-additive one.
Slogan: "Observation gives coherence. Space is a hypothesis. σ-additivity is a
regularity upgrade. Value-definiteness is a further realist upgrade."

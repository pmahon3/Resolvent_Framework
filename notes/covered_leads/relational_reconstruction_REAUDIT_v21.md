### Stripped statement
Setting: (X, 𝓑, μ, T) is an invertible measure-preserving system, h is a scalar observable, and
O_∞ = ⋁_{n∈ℤ} T⁻ⁿ σ(h) is the two-sided delay σ-algebra. The seed's skew product is two-sided;
forward delays ⋁_{n≥0} are treated separately below.
- (S1) The law of the observed stationary process (h∘Tⁿ) determines the factor (X/O_∞, T, μ),
  hence all conditional/predictive laws of the observations. No ambient manifold is needed.
- (S2) The factor is isomorphic to the full system iff O_∞ = 𝓑 mod μ, i.e. h is a generator.
- (S3) Example: X = A^ℤ × B^ℤ, T = σ×σ, h = a₀. Here O_∞ is the first-coordinate factor, so the
  B-fibre is invisible and the system is not recovered, even with two-sided delays.
- (S4) Claim: faithfulness is a *checkable property of the series* that replaces Takens' genericity
  assumption. The obstruction to it is "named".

### Pure verdict: KNOWN
- **(S1)** is the standard identification of a stationary process with the factor generated by its
  time-zero observable (Kolmogorov extension; any ergodic-theory text, e.g. Walters *Intro. Ergodic
  Theory* Ch. 1–4, Petersen *Ergodic Theory* Ch. 5) ⟦textbook, from memory; not shelf-verified⟧.
  The specific reading "prediction survives where geometric reconstruction fails" is published in
  the audience's own venues:
  - Schroer–Sauer–Ott–Yorke, *PRL* 80 (1998) 1410: prediction "most of the time" from
    self-intersecting embeddings ⟦cited in BG 2025 intro; not read⟧.
  - Takens, "The reconstruction theorem for endomorphisms", *Bull. Braz. Math. Soc.* 33 (2002):
    a non-injective delay map still determines T^{k-1} ⟦as reported by BGS 2024; not read⟧.
  - **Barański–Gutman–Śpiewak**, *J. Math. Pures Appl.* 186 (2024) 103–149, arXiv:2212.13509.
    Defs 1.2–1.3 and 1.10–1.13 define predictability on the fibres of a non-injective delay map, for
    an arbitrary Borel μ, including non-invertible T. The paper says explicitly that "reconstruction
    implies prediction but not vice versa". ⟦**read level:** definitions and theorem statements
    via an ar5iv WebFetch summary; quote verified in the primary arXiv PDF, intro line 142 of the
    pdftotext output; proofs not read⟧. This is the nearest owner of
    the seed's predictive/geometric separation.
- **(S2)** is the definition of a generator (Rokhlin; Krieger 1970 for existence of finite
  generators) ⟦textbook⟧. Calling it "faithfulness of the canonical factor" renames it.
- **(S3)** is the textbook non-generating factor obtained by projecting off an independent factor.
  The repo's own hub has a stronger form (`notes/open_questions/delay_embedding/collision_vs_generation.md`
  §"Re-audit 2026-10-09" ⟦HAND; textbook citations, not shelf-verified⟧). It records an
  *additional, forward-only* obstruction from the Rokhlin/Kolmogorov–Sinai formula
  h(T,P) = H(P | ⋁_{n≥1}T⁻ⁿP): for invertible T, a one-sided generator forces h(T) = 0, so forward
  delays never generate at positive entropy. This is a separate obstruction. It does not subsume the
  two-sided fibre failure of S3, and S3 does not subsume it.
- **(S4)** is **false as stated, and the seed's own example refutes it.** In S3 the observed
  process is i.i.d. A-valued. Its law is identical whether or not the B-fibre exists, or how large it
  is. No statistic of the series can tell a faithful case from an unfaithful one. "Is O_∞ = 𝓑?" is
  a question about the pair (series, posited system). So it re-imports exactly the posited target
  that the seed says it drops. The genuinely checkable relatives are already owned:
  - empirical generating-partition estimation: Kennel–Buhl, *PRL* 91 (2003) 084102 ⟦abstract⟧;
  - the cost of non-generating (misplaced) partitions: Bollt–Stanford–Lai–Życzkowski, *PRL* 85
    (2000) 3524, and *Physica D* 154 (2001) ⟦abstract⟧;
  - a priori observability tests, which need a model: Montanari et al., arXiv:2301.04108
    ⟦abstract⟧.

- **Type 6 (exposition/translation): FAIL.**

  - (b) The literature is accessible to them in their own journals (SSOY 1998 PRL; BGS 2024 JMPA;

- **Type 4 (vocabulary): FAIL.** The three candidate statements are:
  1. faithfulness as a criterion, which is generation mod μ;
  2. the skew product as the canonical failure, which is a standard non-generating factor. It is
     not even canonical: the entropy obstruction is a second, independent failure mode for forward
     delays;
  3. manifold-free reconstruction, which is the default setting of abstract measure-theoretic
     dynamics, and is also done with a measure-theoretic Takens in BGS 2024.

  No new statement, no non-trivial result.
- **Type 5 (impossibility, undeclared): FAIL.** "The observable algebra does not fix the
  realization" is the existence of proper factors. The sharper version (no forward generator at
  positive entropy) is classical. It closes no active direction.

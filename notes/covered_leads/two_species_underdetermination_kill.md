# Two species of underdetermination — KILLED (philosophy-novelty gate)

**Date:** 2026-06-13. **Outcome:** PARKED at Phase 2 gate. Did not graduate
past seed. Audit standard applied: **philosophical** novelty, not mathematical.

## The pitch (what was tested)

A motivating example for Paper II, asked to *span both regimes* (Boolean +
OML) and clarify a place philosophy of science is genuinely stuck. Claim:
the **underdetermination of theory by evidence** silently lumps two
structurally distinct phenomena —

- **Species (i)** realization-space / Ω underdetermination: same observation
  algebra, different Ω (rest-frame / gauge / coordinate). Rivals kinematically
  equivalent, choice FREE, a value-definite completion EXISTS for each.
  Paradigms: geocentric/heliocentric, gauge freedom, hole argument.
- **Species (ii)** value-definite-completion impossibility: the realist rival
  posits global definite values but NONE can exist (Kochen–Specker; non-
  distributive observation algebra). NOT a free choice — one "rival" names an
  impossible object.

Proposed diagnostic: **distributivity** of the observation algebra sorts a
case into (i) vs (ii). This is the EA/PR/VDR + distributivity machinery of
Paper II applied as a taxonomy of underdetermination.

## Why it died (scout: hostile-referee, philosophy standard)

**The distinction is already drawn — and drawn the OTHER way.** The seed's
motivating premise was "philosophy *silently lumps* (i) and (ii)." The field
does the opposite: it routinely keeps no-go theorems and ordinary
underdetermination in **separate boxes**.

- **Egg & Saatsi 2021**, "Scientific realism and underdetermination in quantum
  theory," *Philosophy Compass* 16(11) e12773 — the field-defining survey on
  exactly this topic — frames QM underdetermination as choice among completions
  that EXIST (Everett/Bohm/GRW), and explicitly flags no-go arguments
  (Bell/Fine) as "arguments *other than* underdetermination." The lumping the
  seed claims to fix is not the field's habit; the separation is.
- **Redhead 1987**, *Incompleteness, Nonlocality and Realism* — already treats
  KS as realism-failing-NECESSARILY (no completion) vs. the contingent case.
  The modal contrast (contingent vs necessary failure) the seed wanted to
  introduce is present.
- **Stanford SEP "Underdetermination"** taxonomy (holist/contrastive,
  transient/permanent) does NOT contain the (i)/(ii) cut — but its absence
  there is not a gap to fill, because the cut lives (as a separation) in the
  quantum-realism literature instead.
- **Laudan & Leplin 1991** "genuine rival vs notational variant" line is
  ORTHOGONAL (both species (i) and (ii) can be "genuine").

**Consequence:** gates 3 (the conflation) and 5 (genuinely-stuck) both fail.
There is no live confusion to dissolve. Philosophers have the distinction;
they lack only the algebraic dress.

## The danger-zone residue (correctly NOT rescued)

Two slivers survive literally, both forbidden by the audit standard:
1. **Unifying gesture** — calling both "species of one genus." Runs *against*
   the standard taxonomy (which exiles no-go from underdetermination) rather
   than filling a gap. Weak Type-2/3, arguably wrong-way.
2. **Distributivity diagnostic** — dressing the known KS-vs-gauge contrast in
   algebra. By the stated standard ("re-describing a known philosophical
   distinction in algebraic vocabulary counts as NON-novel"), this is
   re-description. Same rock as [[distributed_sensor_contextuality]]
   (died: "was just contextuality") and [[mechanistic_feasibility_parked]]
   (σ/algebra layer decorative).

## The only revival witness (absent)

The seed would revive ONLY if there were a concrete case where the
distributivity diagnostic **adjudicates an instance the informal KS-vs-gauge
intuition gets wrong or leaves undecided** — i.e., the algebra does work the
bare philosophical contrast cannot. Nothing in the literature suggests such a
case. Without that witness, this is a known distinction in algebraic dress.
If such a case is ever found, that — not the taxonomy — is the contribution.

## What this leaves standing

- The geocentric/heliocentric ↔ "Ω unconstrained" agreement is REAL but is
  Paper-I Boolean-side content, well-known, adds nothing (user's own read).
- Paper II's EA/PR/VDR split remains the novelty zone on its own terms; it does
  NOT gain a philosophy-of-science flagship example from this direction.
- Bohm-vs-orthodox was pre-killed in the same session (slot-5 fail: literally
  Bub–Clifton, standard in foundations). Hole argument dropped (Boolean-only,
  doesn't span).

Recurring lesson: an OML "philosophy example" dies when the philosophy isn't
actually stuck — the field already owns the content under
KS/contextuality/no-go vocabulary. Look for a *witness case where the algebra
adjudicates and the intuition fails*, not for a taxonomy.

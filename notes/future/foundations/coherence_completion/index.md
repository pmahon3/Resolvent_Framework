# Coherence and Completion

*Front door note - 2026-05-03*

## Purpose

This cluster studies the distinction between local consistency and licensed
global completion.  The guiding question is:

> When does locally coherent data fail to force the intended global object, and
> what admissibility condition closes that horizon?

The countable-additivity companion note is the prototype.  CE supplies the
admissibility condition in Paper I; Strategy D tests how far that prototype can
be pushed through Boolean algebra and Stone duality.

\section*{The target (the whole thing, one sentence)}

\begingroup\centering
\fbox{\parbox{0.9\linewidth}{\centering\bfseries
Is there a concrete $\sigma$-complete OML carrying a $\sigma$-additive contextual
state witnessed by no finite sub-OML?}}\par
\endgroup
\vspace{2pt}

\textbf{Equivalently (the sharp form):} a concrete $\sigma$-complete OML $L$ with a
\emph{restriction-gap} $\{\, s|_F : s \in \Sdfs \,\} \subsetneq \Sdf(F)$ for some finite
$F$ --- i.e.\ global $\sigma$-additive dispersion-free states fail to restrict onto the
local df-state hull. \emph{($=\sigma$-additive lifting failure.)}

\textbf{STATUS: OPEN, PARKED.} Not solved, not proved impossible.

\section*{The one thing to do (everything reduces to this)}

\begin{door}
\centering\bfseries
Construct --- or refute the existence of --- a binding global $\sigma$-additive
2-valued point over a concrete $\sigma$-complete non-Boolean OML.

\section*{How Door 1 must be attacked (the tool is forced)}

\begin{door}
\textbf{By-hand / compositional construction is CLOSED} --- every elementary assembly
hits a horn (the graveyard in ``Settled'' below). The lever must be \textsc{global} and
\textsc{non-constructive}: any pairwise/finite-assembly spec of $A\vee B$ across
non-orthogonal pairs collapses to a closed horn, so the point cannot be \emph{built}
locally --- it must be \emph{summoned} globally.\\[2pt]
$\Rightarrow$ \textbf{The only tool left is FORCING} (or an inner-model argument). The
missing object and the independence proof are plausibly the \emph{same} forcing
construction. \textbf{Where it bites:} only at the \textsc{uncountable-block} /
$\Sdfs$-density level --- on $\mathbb{N}$ the obstruction is a clean ZFC contradiction
($\bigcap A_k=\emptyset$), no independence there.\\[2pt]
\textbf{Honest cost:} months-long, set-theory-heavy (iterated forcing, plausibly a
measurable cardinal or its failure); real chance the answer is ``the object can't be set
up coherently'' rather than a clean theorem.
\end{door}

\begin{door}
\textbf{FORCING SCOUT --- do NOT start the heavy construction cold.} Bounded effort first:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=2.2em,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Pin the precise set-theoretic sentence} whose independence you'd prove
        (``a binding $\sigma$-point exists over [object schema]''). Check it isn't already
        decided.
  \item \textbf{Survey what forcing / large-cardinal tech could apply} to $\sigma$-point
        existence over non-distributive structures (RVM cardinals, Boolean-valued models,

        independence was already fenced as \textsc{not} earned).
\end{enumerate}
Outcome: a \textsc{foothold} (a forcing notion that plausibly acts) or
\textsc{confirmation the wall is load-bearing}. Both worth the low cost; neither commits
the months. \textbf{Recommended before opening a forcing branch.}
\end{door}

\textbf{The persist-vs-pivot call is yours} (problem-selection $=$ human): commit to the
forcing programme now, run the scout first, or confirm the park. No rush.

\section*{The standing guardrails (violating these $=$ the failure mode)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{\remdw{} is STABLE.} $\sigma$-essential cell \textsc{empty} for
        Polish/regular-representable OMLs (Derr--Williamson). Residue $=$
        non-representable case $=$ $\sigma$-LS wall. Restate, never reopen.
  \item \textbf{``Couldn't build it'' $\neq$ ``needs choice'' $\neq$ ``independent of
        ZFC.''} Three claims, increasing strength. We have the first (proved), suspect the
        second (conjecture), have \textsc{not} shown the third ($=$ Door 3, the open
        work). Don't slide up the chain without doing the math. (Nor couldn't-refute
        $\to$ proved.)
  \item \textbf{CARRYING vs SELECTING} (the 2026-06-22 Door-1 discriminator): the
        difficulty is \textsc{not} that blocks can't hold the mass --- states are
        plentiful ($\Sdf$ compact, $\prod_n MO_2$ state-rich;
        ``horizontal-sum-destroys-states'' is a \textsc{killed} premise). It is that
        \textsc{selecting} one globally-coherent $\sigma$-additive point across all
        incompatible block-pairs at once is the non-compact inverse-limit /
        free-ultrafilter existence step. Door 1 is a \emph{selecting} problem.
  \item \textbf{Exit-B inclination held OPEN, not a verdict.} $\sim$23 reversals on
        record.
  \item \textbf{Red-flag rule:} on \textsc{any} positive result, first ask \emph{where
        did the smuggle enter?} --- inner-regularity / a $\pi$-system / the
        $P(A)\leftrightarrow\Sdfs$ switch / a finitary obstruction masquerading as
        $\sigma$-essential.
  \item All novel hand-work flagged $\hand$; user verifies. Survey gets \textsc{no}
        unproven claim.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Settled --- do NOT re-walk (most of the $\sim$23 reversals, now closed)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Deliverable $=$ the restriction-gap} (above). Non-Borelness of $\Sdfs$ is
        only its descriptive shadow. Off-hull is \textsc{always} finitely detected
        (Hahn--Banach in $\mathbb{R}^L$).
  \item \textbf{Duality determined at (i)+(ii)} $=$ Derr--Williamson $\sigma$-Dynkin
        systems, inhabited by $\prod_n MO_2$. A definitional pin, not a theorem; all
        weight on (iii).
  \item \textbf{Predicate fork RESOLVED} (P-weak $\Rightarrow$ P-strong; co-extensive
        $\Leftrightarrow$ $\sigma$-point-realization $=$ Door 1; agree on all
        segregated/Polish objects; diverge only on the missing witness; use P-strong if a
        witness ever appears). A restatement of Door 1, not a new door.
  \item \textbf{Countable-generation $\Rightarrow$ lifting: PARKED} (prior-art OPEN;
        tribe-vs-points structural; cardinality-control axis DISSOLVED by
        Bure\v{s}ov\'a--Pt\'ak). Witness, if any, uncountably generated.
  \item \textbf{Order-determination does \textsc{not} help} (Paper-I Stone-descent
        transfer): $\delta_x$ are order-determining for free yet lifting can fail --- it's
        the settled (i)+(ii) leg, not the descent leg. The OML lacks the
        Stone-\textsc{duality} leg $=$ Door 1.
  \item \textbf{All carrier constructions dead:} faithful $\sigma$-tribe (Floor); 5
        binding modes; band family (S8 dichotomy, Lean-certified);
        loops/Greechie/KS (finite-character). \emph{This is why the lever must be
        global/non-compositional $\to$ forcing.}
  \item \textbf{Strategy D KILLED} (prior-art, Gaifman 1964). Separate from the descent
        arc.
\end{itemize}

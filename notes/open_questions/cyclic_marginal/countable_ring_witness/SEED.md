# Seed: a σ-essential witness on a countable, literally irreducible carrier (2026-10-27)

Branch `countable-ring-witness`. reviewed: false.

## Puzzle first

Four friends sit in a ring and each pair of neighbours reports who is ahead: x₀<x₁, x₁<x₂,
x₂<x₃, x₃<x₀. Values are natural numbers, so there are only countably many possible seatings.
Each pair report is a perfectly good yes/no answer, and every finite batch of questions you can
build from the pair reports (by "not" and "either one") gets consistent answers. Yet no
countably additive world fits: every countable list of "where exactly is x₀?" questions would
have to locate x₀ somewhere, and then the ring would close a strict loop a₀<a₁<a₂<a₃<a₀.

## The claim

Ω = ℕ^L (L ≥ 4), windows {k, k+1} round the ring, carrier D_L = the Dynkin system generated by
window events (closed under complement and countable disjoint union). The staircase window
ultrafilters extend to a two-valued finitely additive state s on D_L with every step {x_k<x_{k+1}}
true, and s has no σ-additive extension (σ-essential). At L = 4 the carrier is literally irreducible
(centre {∅, Ω}), admissible, and generates the full σ-algebra P(ℕ⁴).

Lean (standard axioms only): `WindowGluing.Cyclic.s₀_sigmaEssential`,
`targetA_sharp_countable`, `targetA_sharp_countable_literal`, `D₄_six_conjuncts` (Polish clause
conditional on the cut), `WindowGluing.Ring.sL_sigmaEssential` (all L ≥ 4).

It answers `q:cardinality` of the σ-essential paper ("can a countable Ω carry one?") positively,
for the paper's σ-essential notion with literal irreducibility (not "essential irreducibility",
which is vacuous on countable Ω).

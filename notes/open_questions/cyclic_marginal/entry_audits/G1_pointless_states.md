## 1. `thm.triangle_pointless`

**Stripped statement.**
- Ω = ℕ³ and D₃ = the λ-system (Dynkin system / σ-class) generated by the three coordinate-pair σ-algebras
  σ(x₀,x₁), σ(x₁,x₂), σ(x₂,x₀).
- For every non-principal p, the signed combination s\* = W₀₁₂ + W₁₂₀ − W₁₀₂ of three iterated-product ultrafilters is
  {0,1}-valued on D₃.
- So D₃ carries a finitely additive two-valued state that is not the restriction of any ultrafilter on ℕ³. In
  standard terms it is a non-coherent 0–1 charge on a λ-system.

**Owners found:**

| Owner (exact item) | What it owns | Read level |
|---|---|---|
| De Simone–Navara–Pták, Ex. 2.2 (6-point MO₄ concrete logic; a two-valued state with no extension even to a signed measure on exp X) | Existence of two-valued states on concrete logics that are not restrictions of ultrafilters (finite, so σ = finite) | primary, this session |
| Navara–Pták 1983, Example (Ω = (ℚ∩(0,1))², σ-class A_{f,g} generated by x, y, x+y; m(A)=1 iff A ⊇ {x=1/3}, {y=1/3} or {x+y=1/2}; not concentrated) | **A three-generator "triangle" of observables carrying a σ-additive pointless two-valued state.** This is the closest printed analogue of the triangle. It has one-variable observables on a 2-dimensional world, and the union of the three generated σ-algebras is already a σ-class | primary, this session |
| Navara–Pták 1983, Thm 1 | two-valued m is concentrated on A_{f,g} ⟺ the integral is additive. This is the reading of "pointless" as integral non-additivity | primary, this session |

**Hostile kill attempts:**
- *"It is the Navara–Pták Example with ultrafilters."* No, it is not reducible to that Example.
  - In the Example the σ-class is the union of three σ-algebras, because nonempty preimages meet pairwise, and the
    state is σ-additive.
  - On D₃ every σ-additive two-valued state is a point (Prop. `sigma`). So D₃ is strictly larger than the union, and
    the state must be purely finitely additive.
  - The extension from the flight sub-carrier to all of D₃ is exactly the step no source performs.
  - A referee will still say "same phenomenon". The answer is the σ/finite split together with the lift lemma and
    non-buildability (`thm.triangle_estar_not_built`).
- *"It is a trivial consequence of p⊗p non-commutativity."* No. Finite additivity of s\* is trivial, since it is a
  signed sum of ultrafilters. Two-valuedness on D₃ needs three further steps: Ramsey homogenisation, the
  two-box-identity reduction to the cyclic and anticyclic patterns, and the Dynkin-induction exclusion. None of
  these steps was located in print.
- *"It is a finitely additive marginal-problem theorem."* The located finitely additive marginal theory
  (Hansel–Troallic, per a secondary source) is stated on algebras. On the algebra generated by the windows no 0–1
  extension exists, since ∩C_k = ∅ lies in it. The λ-system version was not found.
- *Contextuality.*
  - The sheaf/contextuality frameworks with infinite outcomes presuppose σ-additivity (`own.cv_sheaves_sigma`).
  - No contextuality paper with ultrafilter-valued local sections was found here or in the 2026-10-23 Stone pass.
- *Model theory.* 3-amalgamation of types (`own.type_amalgamation`) owns the obstruction kinds for complete types. It
  does not own the charge version on a λ-system.

**Pure verdict: UNCLEAR (entry-level cap).**
- No owner of the stated result was found, and the nearest σ-class sources (Navara–Pták 1983, Dravecký–Šipoš,
  Navara 1984) are read in full.
- NOVEL is still blocked, because the unread sources most likely to contain a finitely additive λ-system example
  remain: Bhaskara Rao Ch. 3 (body), Gudder SIAM Rev. 1984 Ex. 3.1, the Pták–Pulmannová book, and Chrapčiaková 1987.
  Hindman–Strauss and Blass are unread for the six-orders/Ramsey step, which is ingredient risk only.

**Status: PARTLY OWNED.**
- Owned: the existence of pointless two-valued states on concrete logics (finite: DNP Ex. 2.2; σ-additive triangle of
  observables: Navara–Pták Example), and all proof ingredients.
- Residual: (a) a purely finitely additive pointless state on the full λ-closure of pair windows on a 3-cycle over
  ℕ, where every σ-additive state is a point; (b) the non-membership of the cyclic family.

**Type 3: FAIL.** No method transfer is demonstrated. The flip condition is unchanged: decide Navara's
two-sided-unbounded regime, or transfer the lift/Dynkin-induction to a second domain.

**Type 4: FAIL.**
- The result is cleanly stateable in standard vocabulary: a non-coherent 0–1 charge on the λ-system generated by
  coordinate σ-algebras.
- The three-statements test therefore fails as vocabulary. Each statement ("pointless ⟺ not a forest", "σ-states are
  points, finitely additive ones need not be", "E\* is not buildable") is expressible without new words.
- This is a Type-1-style theorem candidate, not infrastructure.

**Referee estimate: BORDERLINE** (capped by the UNCLEAR verdict).
- Venue class: short note (Czech/Slovak concrete-logic school; Math. Slovaca, *Order*, IJTP-type).
- Value carried by: factor 1.
- Main risk a referee would raise: "Navara–Pták 1983's Example already gives a pointless two-valued state on a
  three-generator σ-class; the ultrafilter version is the same phenomenon." Rebuttal: σ-additive states on D₃ are
  points.

**WHY.md bearing.** The entry bears on question 1 (*loop?*). Three windows, each correct and pairwise consistent, do
not add up to one world, even an ideal one. Only finitely additive (potential-infinite) answering allows the loop.

## 2. `thm.staircase_no_ideal`

**Stripped statement.**
- Let u_k = p⊗p on each ring window, oriented around the ring. The u_k agree on shared coordinates, since every
  marginal is p.
- No ultrafilter W on Ω (equivalently, no 0–1 charge on the window-generated algebra) projects to all u_k. The reason
  is that W would contain every C_k = {x_k < x_{k+1}}, while ∩C_k = ∅.

**Pure verdict: KNOWN, as a trivial consequence (folklore).**
- The derivation takes three lines: {x<y} ∈ p⊗p (textbook); compatibility follows because both marginals are p; and
  W ⊇ {C_k} contradicts the FIP.
- It is the ultrafilter instance of the classical cyclic-marginal obstruction:
  - Vorob'ev, `own.vorobev_acyclic`;
  - Fritz–Chaves's three coins (shelf);
  - Kharoof–Ipek–Okay for cycles (`own.cycle_vertices_fixed_L`).
- The asymmetry it uses is the p⊗p non-commutativity owned by `own.ultrafilter_products`.
- The project's own hub already records the algebra clause ("Boolean tools cannot reach μ").
- No printed sentence stating exactly this was located. The verdict rests on the triviality rule ("trivial consequence
  of standard techniques: KNOWN"), not on a citation. The entry-level cap restricts only NOVEL.

**Owner:** folklore. Ingredients: Hindman–Strauss and Goldberg (p⊗p), secondary; Vorob'ev, Fritz–Chaves (cyclic
obstruction), read.

**Type 3: FAIL.** Type 4: FAIL; it is stateable verbatim in standard terms.
**Referee estimate: REJECT-LIKELY as a standalone claim.** It is fine as a lemma in a paper whose result is
`thm.countable_witness`.

**Proposed new `status`:**
> proved-hand. ENTRY AUDIT G1 2026-10-27: KNOWN (trivial consequence / folklore): {x<y} ∈ p⊗p + equal marginals +
> FIP failure of ∩C_k = ∅; ultrafilter instance of the cyclic-marginal obstruction (own.vorobev_acyclic,
> own.ultrafilter_products). No novelty claimed here; the content is the extension to the carrier
> (thm.countable_witness, thm.triangle_pointless).

## 3. `thm.ring_conceptual`

**Stripped statement.** This is a hand proof that, for L ≥ 4, the forced functional Λ is a 0–1 finitely additive
state on the whole ring carrier. The steps:
1. Locality: E = E_{k−1} ⊔ E_k.
2. Cut at an untouched window m, so that Λ(E) = W^{(m)}(E) ∈ {0,1}.
3. Λ is linear.

**Correctness check (hand).** The argument is sound given its inputs.
- Step 2 needs a window m ∉ {k−1, k}, which exists for L ≥ 3. At L = 3 step 1 fails (D ≠ G).
- Linearity needs well-definedness. Zero-sum window-function families on a cycle are coboundaries plus constants, a
  standard cycle-cohomology fact, and the agreeing marginals kill those terms.
- So the proof's weight sits entirely on `thm.shape_lemma`, which is hand and checked on finite alphabets only.

**Owners of components:**
- The rigidity half of locality (non-empty window events on disjoint windows meet, so pieces of a disjoint union lie
  on pairwise adjacent windows) is the Dravecký–Šipoš pairwise-meeting device (`own.dravecky_sipos_flight_subcarrier`).
- The slicing/closure half: not located. Its nearest neighbour is the chain descriptions in Navara 1984 Lemmas 1–3
  (read, different regime).
- The cut-the-loop step is path amalgamation (`thm.amalgamation`, folklore).
- The cocycle identity W^{(j)} − W^{(k)} = δ_k − δ_j is a Čech-type cocycle, in the sense of Abramsky–Mansfield–Barbosa
  2011 (shelf). That is a neighbour in form only; no transfer is shown.

**Pure verdict: UNCLEAR (cap).**
- The theorem proved is `thm.countable_witness`, which has no owner after three passes. The cap applies because
  Bhaskara Rao's body, the Hindman–Strauss and Goldberg bodies, and the Pták–Pulmannová book are unread.
- As a proof it is not a Type-1 item. Read hostilely, it is a liability: it shows the ring witness is short once
  locality holds. A referee holding the locality lemma could call the ring theorem routine. This strengthens
  `obs.ring_forced_formula`'s qualification of the earlier "all novelty in `mu_cases`" rebuttal.

**Type 3: FAIL.** The cocycle reading is a candidate bridge to cohomological contextuality, but no technique
transfers.
**Type 4: FAIL.**
**Referee estimate: BORDERLINE** (cap). Value: factor 2, a conceptual proof of a Lean result. Main risk: "routine once
locality is known; locality's rigidity step is Dravecký–Šipoš."

**Proposed new `status`:**
> proved-hand (agrees with Lean; not cross-checked line by line). ENTRY AUDIT G1 2026-10-27: pure UNCLEAR (inherits
> thm.countable_witness; cap: Bhaskara Rao body, Hindman–Strauss/Goldberg bodies, Pták–Pulmannová book unread).
> Correct given thm.shape_lemma; all weight on locality, whose rigidity step is Dravecký–Šipoš's pairwise-meeting
> device (owned) and whose slicing step is unlocated. Hostile reading: makes the ring witness look routine given
> locality. Type 3 FAIL (cocycle = form-neighbour of Abramsky–Mansfield–Barbosa 2011, no transfer), Type 4 FAIL.

## 4. `obs.ring_forced_formula`

**Stripped statement.**
- On rings with L ≥ 4, every state satisfies s(E) = u_{k−1}(E_{k−1}) + u_k(E_k). This is immediate from
  `thm.shape_lemma` and additivity.
- The right side is ≤ 1, because the two adjacent windows form a path, so their ultrafilters amalgamate
  (`thm.amalgamation`) and the sum is W(E_{k−1} ⊔ E_k).

**Pure verdict: UNCLEAR, with residual = `thm.shape_lemma`.**
- Given the lemma, the observation is a two-line corollary, KNOWN as trivial.
- The lemma itself was not found. Its rigidity half is owned (Dravecký–Šipoš); its slicing half is unlocated. Nearest
  neighbours read: Navara 1984 Lemmas 1–3 (bounded generator) and Greechie's loop lemma (`own.greechie_loop`; same
  order-3 exception, on blocks not states).
- Unread, and the likely owner if one exists: the Pták–Pulmannová book (σ-classes generated by several σ-algebras);
  Gudder SIAM Rev. 1984.

**Type 3: FAIL. Type 4: FAIL.** Its value is internal: it correctly weakens the ring-witness novelty rebuttal.
**Referee estimate:** not applicable standalone; at most BORDERLINE (cap).

**Proposed new `status`:**
> proved-hand. ENTRY AUDIT G1 2026-10-27: corollary of thm.shape_lemma + amalgamation (trivial given the lemma);
> pure UNCLEAR with residual = the slicing half of thm.shape_lemma (rigidity half = Dravecký–Šipoš pairwise-meeting,
> owned). Unread likely owner: Pták–Pulmannová book; Gudder SIAM Rev 1984. Stands as the honest qualification of the
> 'all novelty in mu_cases' rebuttal.

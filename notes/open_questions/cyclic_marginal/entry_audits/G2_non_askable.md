## The puzzle, then the question

Three clocks, read two at a time. A question can be built from pair-questions by "not" and by
"either one, never both" (countable disjoint union). On a finite dial, every 0/1 pattern that is a
sum of pair-patterns can be built. On an unbounded dial (ℕ), the question "do the three readings run
in cyclic order?" (E\*) is a sum of pair-patterns, `1_{E*} = [x0<x1]+[x1<x2]+[x2<x0] − 1` off ties,
and still cannot be built. Stated formally: E\* is not in `D3 = DynkinSystem.generate` of the three
pair-window σ-algebras (each the full power set of ℕ², pulled back) on ℕ³.

**Bearing on WHY.md:** these entries name questions that every pair of views answers, but that no
procedure built from the views can ask. That is the "unaskable joint question" (question 2), in its
infinite form.

### The strip test (hostile step 1)

Without the vocabulary, the claim reads: let X = ℕ³ and let 𝒜_{ij} = σ(x_i, x_j), the three
coordinate-pair sub-σ-algebras. Then the cyclic-order set is not in the λ-system λ(𝒜₀₁∪𝒜₁₂∪𝒜₀₂),
although its indicator lies in 𝒜₀₁+𝒜₁₂+𝒜₀₂ (sums of measurable functions). Standard names:
λ-system generated by the union of sub-σ-algebras; "σ-class generated by coordinate observables"
(Gudder/Czech school).

### The key comparison: flight sub-class vs full carrier

The Czech results live on C_{f,g} for f = x1−x0 and g = x2−x1 (the "flight" class, pulled back to
ℤ³ or ℕ³). The class C_{f,g} is contained in D3. Non-membership in the smaller class (owned) does
**not** imply non-membership in D3, because D3 contains the full pair σ-algebras and could build
more. Navara 1984 shows that a richer class can contain K: on ℤ×ℕ₀, one-sided boundedness supplies
a base case. On ℕ³, D3 contains the bounded-below coordinates x_i, yet E\* is still not in it.
So the residual is not a costume of the owned result: it is non-membership in a strictly larger
class that has bounded-below observables available, in a regime where Navara's chain has no base
case built from the generators f and g.

## Entry 1: `thm.triangle_estar_not_built`

**Pure verdict: UNCLEAR (PARTLY OWNED).**
- **Owned part.** The flight-sub-class analogue: E\* and K(a,b) are not in C_{f,g} for the
  two-sided-unbounded difference observables. This follows from D–Š 1980 Ex. 6 (the class equals
  A_f∪A_g∪A_{f+g}; full text read). Also by contraposition of N–P 1983 Thm 1 (3)⇒(1) applied to the
  D–Š state (full text read).
- **Owned method.** The invariant-plus-least-stage ("good sets") induction for Dynkin
  non-membership is owned (Keleti–Preiss 2000, Lemma 12 template; textbook good-sets principle).
  The lift lemma is the instance-specific part.
- **Residual: NOT FOUND.** E\* and its finite-band perturbations are not in the full λ-closure D3
  of the three pair σ-algebras over ℕ, although 1_{E\*} is a sum of window functions.
- **Why UNCLEAR, not NOVEL.** The nearest works (N–P, Navara, D–Š, Chrapčiaková) are now read in
  full and do not own the residual. But Zerbe–Gudder 1985 and Gudder SIAM Rev 1984 Ex 3.1 sit in
  the same lineage and remain unread. The prior audit named the second of these as a gap.
- **Upgrade path.** Read Zerbe–Gudder 1985 and Gudder 1984 §3 past the abstract. If neither owns
  the residual, the verdict becomes NOVEL (residual-scoped).
- **Type 3: FAIL.** No technique moves between fields. The method is Keleti–Preiss's template on a
  new object. It would flip if the lift lemma decided a Czech-school question the D–Š rigidity does
  not already settle, for example K-membership in some intermediate class between C_{f,g} and D3.
- **Type 4: FAIL as vocabulary.** "Buildable" = λ-system generated by coordinate sub-σ-algebras
  (standard name). The entry is a candidate for the **non-trivial-result leg** of the collection's
  Type-4 test, not a vocabulary item.
- **Referee estimate: BORDERLINE** (capped by UNCLEAR). Venue class: short note (Math. Slovaca /
  Colloq. Math. tradition). Value carried by factor 1. Main risk: "the D–Š class already shows
  this; why does the larger class matter?" The answer is that D–Š states do not extend to D3, while
  the finitely additive pointless state does (thm.triangle_pointless).

## Entry 2: `thm.navara_witness_not_buildable_Z`

**Pure verdict: UNCLEAR (PARTLY OWNED); no novelty beyond Entry 1.**
- **Transport lemma (`mem_pre`): KNOWN, textbook.** A map φ that sends each window σ-algebra into
  the corresponding one satisfies φ⁻¹(λ(𝒢)) ⊆ λ(φ⁻¹𝒢). The proof: the sets whose preimage lies in
  the target λ-system form a λ-system ("good sets"; preimage commutes with complements and disjoint
  unions). Folklore; no owner citation needed beyond any measure-theory text's π-λ chapter.
- **Owned analogue.** K(0,0) ∉ C_{f,g} on ℤ² (two-sided unbounded f, g) is immediate from D–Š Ex. 6.
  Contrast Navara 1984's ℤ×ℕ₀ example, where K(a,b) **is** in C_{f,g} (full text read). The entry's
  content is the step from C_{f,g} to D3 over ℤ, inherited from Entry 1 through the restriction
  K(0,0)|_{ℕ³} ∈ InF (anticyclic off ties).
- **Scope caution (correct in the entry).** Non-membership does not decide the sign of Λ on D3.
  N–P Thm 1 ((3)⇒(1)) is stated for σ-additive two-valued states. On D3 every σ-additive two-valued
  state is a point (own.dravecky_sipos_flight_subcarrier), so Thm 1 says nothing about the
  finitely additive Λ. Do not read "K ∉ D3" as "Λ is non-additive".
- **Type 3: FAIL. Type 4: FAIL** (a corollary of Entry 1 plus textbook transport).
- **Referee estimate: REJECT-LIKELY standalone** (a corollary); acceptable as a remark beside Entry 1.

## Entry 3: `obs.c0c1_not_in_D`

**Pure verdict: KNOWN (folklore; a trivial consequence).**
- **Statement.** The ring carrier (triangle-free window graph) consists of the star-sets (in-house
  `thm.shape_lemma`). {x0<x1<x2} = {x0<x1}∩{x1<x2} is an intersection of events from two windows
  that share x1. Its slice at x1 = c is a product {x0<c}×{x2>c}, not a cylinder in one leaf. Hence
  it is not in D_L.
- **Owner of the mechanism.** Rigidity (non-empty events from non-adjacent windows meet, so a union
  of sub-σ-algebras is closed under disjoint unions) is D–Š 1980 Ex. 6 (full text read). The
  prototype, a Dynkin system generated by two crossing sets missing their intersection, is a
  standard exercise (Tübingen Maßtheorie WS 2020/21 Blatt 02 Aufg. 5; snippet level). Once the shape
  lemma holds, the observation is one line.
- **What carries weight.** The observation's value is diagnostic: with thm.basic, it shows exactly
  which intersection pointlessness needs. It is not a contribution. Any novelty sits in
  `thm.shape_lemma` (out of scope here; the lemma deserves its own audit, since λ-systems of
  α-acyclic / Helly families of sub-σ-algebras may be owned in database or Markov-random-field
  literature).
- **Type 3: FAIL. Type 4: FAIL.**
- **Referee estimate: REJECT-LIKELY standalone**; fine as an example in a note.

## 1. thm.countable_witness

**Stripped statement.** Let Ω = ℕ^L with L ≥ 4, and let D_L be the σ-class (Dynkin system)
generated by the pullbacks of 𝒫(ℕ²) along the L cyclic pair projections.
- There is a finitely additive {0,1}-state μ on D_L with μ(C_k) = 1 for every k, where
  C_k = {x_k < x_{k+1}} (indices mod L), although ⋂C_k = ∅.
- Every σ-additive {0,1}-state on D_L is a Dirac (G3). So no σ-additive two-valued state agrees
  with μ on the finite block {C_k, C_kᶜ}.
- That block pattern is therefore σ-essential: finitely coherent, with an empty kernel. Lean:
  `sL_sigmaEssential`, plus `s₀_sigmaEssential` and `mu_cases` at L = 4.

In concrete-logic language: **a countable concrete σ-class whose σ-additive hidden variables are all
concentrated, but which carries a free (finitely additive) hidden variable.**

### 1.1 What is owned (ingredients and shadows)

- **Pták 1987 CMUC, full text.**
  - **Terminology.** A two-valued state concentrated at no point is a **"free hidden variable"**
    (p. 159). This is the programme's "pointless state". Cite it.
  - **Finite free example.** Prop 2(i): X_even(4) has a free hidden variable,
    h{1,2} = h{2,3} = h{1,3} = 1. Prop 2(ii): for even n ≥ 6 every hidden variable on X_even(n) is
    concentrated.
    - This is a **second finite shadow**, beside Navara–Pták 1983's six-point σ-class.
    - It is the same rebuttal: on a finite Ω, finite additivity and σ-additivity coincide, so it
      exhibits no σ split.
  - **Thm 4 (compactness extension).** On a *Boolean* sublogic, a hidden variable extends to any
    larger logic. This is the Boolean baseline in hidden-variable form. It does not reach D_L,
    because the window algebra is non-Boolean once glued.
  - **Thm 5.** Every concrete logic is *isomorphic* to one, represented over Ω′ = Hid(A), in which
    every hidden variable is concentrated.
    - Do **not** read this as "σ-essentiality is representation-relative". The isomorphism preserves
      finite orthogonal joins only, and the image over Hid(D_L) need not be a σ-class. So it does
      not make μ σ-additive anywhere.
    - **Its effect on σ-essentiality is undetermined.** It is a note for `q:cardinality`'s framing,
      not a kill.
- **Hindman–Strauss, body read.**
  - Def 11.1 / Lemma 11.3 own p⊗q = lim_s lim_t (s,t). This is the staircase window ultrafilter.
  - Thm 11.1.1 is a notable cousin. It builds f, g : S → S with f̃(p) = g̃(p) although
    {f = g} = ∅, from q⊗q on S×S minus the diagonal. That is a Boolean, single-ultrafilter
    "agreement without a meeting point", **pairwise only**.
  - §18.1 owns Ramsey from an arbitrary non-principal ultrafilter. That is an ingredient of the
    triangle, not of the ring.
  - Nothing on Dynkin systems, λ-closures, cycles of windows, or states on non-Boolean set systems.
  - This supersedes the "secondary" grade in `own.ultrafilter_products` for H–S.
- **Goldberg 2102.04677** (full, earlier pass): "amalgamation of (U, W)" for pairs only.
- **Bhaskara Rao §3.2** (contents only, plus the DNP 2007 cross-check): the Horn–Tarski criterion.
  μ fails it trivially (kernel empty), so the book owns the *diagnosis*, not the *existence*.
  Any {0,1} charge on the *algebra* generated by the windows would give μ(⋂C_k) = 1. So the window
  ultrafilters have no joint charge. This "Boolean impossibility" half is folklore (cyclic order,
  Vorob'ev type).
- **Navara–Pták 1983, Navara 1984, Dravecký–Šipoš 1980** (full, earlier passes):
  - the finite shadow;
  - σ-additive uniqueness from generators;
  - σ-additive non-point states on a sub-σ-class.

  None of them treats the finitely additive extension to the full λ-closure.
- **Bikchentaev–Navara 2016** (full): symmetric logics, Δ-subadditivity.
  - Ex 4.4 (uncountable Ω, even or co-even finite sets) is the only infinite example, and it
    concerns subadditivity, not free hidden variables or σ.
  - It cites [14]: a non-Boolean concrete logic all of whose states are subadditive. So **no
    general theorem forces free states from non-Booleanness**, and the witness is not a corollary of
    a Boolean-characterisation theorem (Pták–Pulmannová 1994 Thm 1 is about OMLs and needs unital
    subadditive states; D_L is not shown to be a lattice).

### 1.2 Hostile checks run on the statement itself

1. **Is there a trivial countable witness that makes this one routine?**
   - Blow up a finite free example (X_even(4), or Navara–Pták) by ×ℕ, adding 𝒫(ℕ) on the second
     factor. Then σ-additive states m⊗δ_n reproduce the free pattern, so this is not a witness.
   - Products of X_even(6) blocks: σ-additive two-valued states are Diracs (by Pták Prop 2(ii) and
     block σ-additivity). The finitely additive ones built from an ultrafilter over blocks are
     ultrafilter restrictions, so they are coherent with a nonempty finite kernel. Not a witness.
   - ⟦HAND, sketch⟧: no cheaper countable witness was found. The cyclic-order non-commutativity of
     p⊗p is what supplies the empty kernel.
2. **Does the structural lemma (`collision_vs_generation.md`, 2026-10-15) make `mu_cases`
   bookkeeping?**
   - Partly. D_L = {E_{k−1} ⊔ E_k}, so μ is forced and checking that it is {0,1}-valued is finite
     work. A referee holding the lemma may call the existence step easy.
   - It still has **no printed source**. The lemma's own prior art is unchecked: it looks like the
     Greechie loop shape, Kalmbach §4 L9, but is not that.
3. **Is D_L a symmetric logic?** No. C₀ Δ C₂ involves two non-adjacent windows, so it is not of
   the form E_{k−1} ⊔ E_k ⟦HAND, from the lemma⟧. This separates DNP 2015's *construction* from
   D_L. It does **not** separate their *existence question*: a σ-complete symmetric logic on a
   countable set with a free, non-extendable two-valued state would be a cousin witness.

## 2. thm.literal_irreducible_L4

**Stripped statement.** The centre of D_4 is {∅, ℕ⁴}. Together with σ-completeness, concreteness,
non-segregation, non-intersection-closure and (conditional on `DWPolishCut`) non-Polish-
representability, this gives the repo's six-conjunct admissibility bundle on a countable carrier
(`targetA_sharp_countable_literal`, `D₄_six_conjuncts`).

### Pure verdict: **KNOWN (routine), not a contribution on its own**

- **The trivial centre** follows in a few lines from the rectangle identity:
  - a central E meets each fibre {x_i = a} inside D;
  - separated pairs in the ring then force E to be independent of each coordinate.

  An expert would call it a verification. No literature item states it, and none needs to.
- **The six-conjunct bundle** is a repo-internal definition (`Admissible` plus `Irreducible`). It
  is not a field notion, so there is nothing to own. Its value is as a **receipt** that the
  countable witness meets `q:cardinality` in the sharp form.
- **The costume fix is correctly recorded.** `EssentiallyIrreducible` is vacuous on countable Ω,
  and the literal form replaces it.
- **Caveats:**
  - **L = 4 only.** For L > 4 the irreducibility conjunct is still the vacuous one.
  - **The Polish clause stays conditional** on `DWPolishCut`. It is not assessed and is not a
    refutation of the cut (as recorded in `collision_vs_generation.md`).

**Type 3: FAIL. Type 4: FAIL.** It is a receipt for `thm.countable_witness`, scope L = 4.

**How this bears on WHY.md (loop / unaskable joint question / hidden idealisation / circle or
composition).**
- **Loop.** The witness is the cleanest case of the loop question: on a countable world, four local
  "x_k < x_{k+1}" answers each hold, and no world makes them all true.
- **Hidden idealisation.** The only way to make the four answers cohere is the idealisation that
  σ-additivity forbids.
- **Prior art.** Pták's 1987 "free hidden variable" shows the Prague school named the phenomenon
  forty years ago, but only finitely, where it has no σ edge. The countable step is where it bites.

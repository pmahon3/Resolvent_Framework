# Resolution: Ovchinnikov 1999, Ovchinnikov–Sultanbekov 1998, De Simone–Navara–Pták 2015

**Lead (reframing, not a reversal).** Hidden-failure kernel (b), "D = V₀₁", is **exactly Ovchinnikov's
*closedness*** of the finite concrete logic D (his Galois correspondence, O99 p.1959, from Ovchinnikov
1994a). Hidden failure is exactly **non-positivity** in his sense. He proves positive ⇒ closed. So (b) is
not an independent bridge lemma. Together with Sullivant, it is *equivalent* to "no hidden failure" at
compressed sizes. Ovchinnikov does **not** prove (b) for any pair-generated product logic, and has no
sufficient criterion for positivity. The kernel survives, renamed.

## Item 1. Hidden-failure kernel (b) and the 3×4×4 example: **PARTLY OWNED**

### 1.1 What O99 / OS98 contain (relevant parts)
- **Definitions (O99 p.1959).**
  - Regular: "every signed measure on E extends to a signed measure on P(Ω)" (Def 2.1).
  - Positive: "every measure on E which extends to a signed measure ... also extends to a measure"
    (Def 2.2).
  - Equivalence: "E ∈ R ∩ P ⇐⇒ S^pure(E) = S^point(E)". So "every state is a mixture of points"
    is R ∩ P.
- **Galois correspondence (O99 p.1959).**
  - S° = {x : μ(x)=0 ∀μ∈S} and T° = {μ ∈ M : μ(x)=0 ∀x∈T}, where M is the signed measures of
    total mass 0.
  - Closed means T°° = T.
  - Thm 2.5: regular ⟺ dim E° + dim V(E) = card Ω.
  - **Thm 2.6: "P ⊂ N"**, i.e. positive ⇒ closed. It is generalised by Thm 4.6 (positive ⇒ locally
    positive, p.1961) and Remark 4.7 (locally positive ⇒ closed, p.1961).
- **Δ(Λ) (O99 §3, pp.1960–1962).**
  - Δ(Λ) is the least concrete logic on a finite Λ ⊂ ℝ² making π₁, π₂ and π₁+π₂ measurable. It is the
    finite version of the Navara–Pták 1983 σ-class of x, y, x+y that is already in the taxonomy.
  - Thm 3.3 (p.1960): Δ(n×m) is regular, via the Zerbe–Gudder additivity of integrals (Lemma 3.5).
  - Remark 3.6 (p.1961): "S^{0,1}(Δ(Λ)) = S^point(Δ(Λ))", i.e. all two-valued states are points
    (Navara–Pták 1983).
  - p.1962: Δ({0,1,2,3}²) "is not positive", via a strange set with no positive polar.
  - p.1962: Sultanbekov's remark that the same holds "whenever n ≥ 4 and m ≥ 4" (no proof given;
    also OS98 p.150).
- **Open problems (O99 p.1965).**
  - 6.1: is V(E) = Lin S^{0,1}(E)?
  - 6.2: is every Δ(Λ) regular / closed / filled?
  - **6.3: "Is every locally positive concrete logic on a finite set positive?"** There is no
    sufficient positivity criterion anywhere in O99 or OS98.
- **OS98 (pp.147–152).**
  - A survey: Prather logics, Gudder–Marchand logics, the same duality, local positivity and
    filledness.
  - Thm 2.9 is the same regularity theorem.
  - Thm 3.3 characterises symmetric logics (Nim).
  - Same open problems (5.1–5.6).
  - Nothing beyond O99 on extension.
- Neither paper has product carriers, pair or marginal events, polytopes or compressedness.

### 1.2 Translation of the kernel ⟦HAND, auditor; short, re-check⟧
Let D be the concrete logic on X₀×X₁×X₂ generated by the three pair partitions. Let W be the span
of the pair-cell indicators.
1. **span D = W.** Pair events lie in D. Also D ⊆ V₀₁ ⊆ W, because complement and disjoint union
   preserve both span membership and the 0/1 property.
2. **D° = W^⊥ and D°° = V₀₁.**
   - Ω ∈ D, so D° = M ∩ W^⊥ = W^⊥.
   - In finite dimension, x ∈ D°° ⟺ 1_x ∈ (W^⊥)^⊥ = W.
   - So **(b) "D = V₀₁" ⟺ D is closed (O99 p.1959).**
3. **D is regular.**
   - The linear relations among pair indicators are generated by the marginal-consistency relations
     (dimension count r₀r₁ + r₁r₂ + r₂r₀ − (r₀+r₁+r₂−1)).
   - Each such relation equates two disjoint-union decompositions of the same set {x_i = a}, so every
     signed measure on D respects it.
   - Induction along complement and disjoint-union constructions then shows that every signed measure
     on D is the restriction of a linear functional on W. Hence dim V(D) = dim W and Thm 2.5's equality
     holds.
   - Consequence: "every carrier state is a mixture" ⟺ **D is positive**, and a hidden failure is a
     witness of non-positivity.
4. **General equivalence for any finite concrete logic E** (Farkas plus O99 Thm 2.6). Let V₊ be the
   pointwise-nonnegative vectors in span E. Then:
   > **E positive ⟺ E closed and every extreme ray of V₊ is spanned by a 0/1 vector.**
   - (⇒) Positive gives V₊ = cone(1_E), so the extreme rays are indicators of elements of E;
     closedness is Thm 2.6.
   - (⇐) The extreme rays are 0/1 vectors of the span, hence in E°° = E, so V₊ = cone(1_E).
   - For the triangle, Sullivant Thm 2.4 identifies the second conjunct with *compressed*.
   - So **D positive ⟺ D closed ∧ compressed.** The classification "hidden failure ⟺ not compressed"
     is then *equivalent* to (b) at compressed sizes. The kernel is the whole content beyond Sullivant
     and Ovchinnikov, not one lemma among several.
   - I did not find this equivalence stated in O99 or OS98. It is a two-line consequence of their
     definitions plus Farkas: credit it, do not claim it.

## Item 2. thm.countable_witness vs DNP 2015: **NOT OWNED**

**What DNP15 contains.**
- Def 2 (p.1996): their "state" requires Δ-subadditivity, s(A Δ B) ≤ s(A) + s(B), on symmetric
  logics.
- **Thm 1** (pp.1996–1997): states, two-valued included, extend from a Boolean subalgebra to the
  symmetric logic, by compactness. This is an ingredient-level cousin of Pták 1987 Thm 4.
- **Thm 2** (p.1997): states on copies of E_{2n} extend.
- **Thm 3** (pp.1997–1998): on Ω = {0,…,9}, with D generated by four 4-sets ("Chinese lantern"
  MO₁₅), "there is a two-valued state ... which cannot be extended over the entire exp Ω". This is
  **finite**: another finite shadow (free hidden variable on 10 points), with no σ split.
- **σ-part**, Construction 1 and Thms 4–6 (pp.1998–1999):
  - S ⊗ (Ω, D) with S of uncountable cofinality is a σ-complete symmetric logic (Thm 4).
  - With card S = ℵ₁ and card Ω = ℵ₀, "each σ-additive state ... is carried by a countable set", and
    so it extends to exp (Thm 5, p.1999).
  - Thm 6 is a ¬RM version.
  - These are **positive extension results on uncountable carriers**, the opposite direction to the
    witness. There is no countable σ-class and no free finitely additive two-valued state.
- D_L is not symmetric (G4 §1.2(3)), so the constructions do not meet.

## Item 3. thm.shape_lemma and thm.triangle_pointless residue: **NOT OWNED**

- **Shape lemma.** The only structural facts in these papers are generic for finite logics:
  - every element is a disjoint union of atoms;
  - a family A is the atom set of a logic iff its elements are incomparable and complements of
    disjoint unions are disjoint unions (O99 p.1958).
  - There is no generated-logic normal form, window graph, Helly or star condition.
  - **Remove O99 from G5's unread list.**

## WHY.md, one line
Ovchinnikov's vocabulary splits "local checks certify one world" into two separate failures:
- a question you *could* phrase linearly but cannot build from the views (not closed);
- every buildable question passing while no world exists (not positive).

The triangle result says the second failure happens exactly when the table is not compressed. The
open kernel says the first never happens at compressed sizes.

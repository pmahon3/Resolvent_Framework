## 1. The candidate, stated precisely

- Variables X, Y, Z with finite value sets of sizes r1 ≥ r2 ≥ r3. Pair laws P_XY, P_YZ, P_ZX with agreeing one-variable marginals.
- **Visible failure:** some event built from pair events by complement and disjoint union (the Dynkin system they generate) receives a negative or impossible value under the finitely additive extension, or a support-level check fails.
- **Hidden failure:** supports glue (every allowed pair extends to an allowed triple), the finitely additive extension to the Dynkin system is nonnegative on every such event, yet no joint law P_XYZ reproduces the three pairs.
- In-repo candidate theorem (`definitions_draft.md` §22): hidden failure is possible iff r2 ≥ 4 and r3 ≥ 3, iff the no-three-way-interaction polytope is non-compressed. Witness at 3×4×4: all three pair laws uniform 1/9 on 9 allowed pairs (`intuition_kit.tex` §3).
- Scope: real (not integer) joint laws.

## 4. Where the seam is NOT (owned framings)

- Pairwise consistent but globally inconsistent on a cyclic schema: owned in databases (BFMY, Atserias–Kolaitis), probability (Vorob'ev), contextuality (Abramsky–Brandenburger), graphical models (local vs marginal polytope), and statistics (disclosure: Sullivant, De Loera–Onn).
- Existence of a real joint is an LP (marginal polytope membership); integer versions are NP-complete (De Loera–Onn; Atserias–Kolaitis 2021). Do not present either as new.
- The compressed / non-compressed threshold: owned (Sullivant; Ohsugi–Hibi).
- Logical vs probabilistic contextuality as a hierarchy: owned (Abramsky–Brandenburger).
- "Cyclic merging needs a repair step": practised under other names (cycle-consistency losses, Barak-type projection, Private-PGM).

## 5. Verdict: mixed, mostly (i) for the existence gap, open for the hidden refinement

Pairwise compatibility without a joint is textbook in four fields, and the compressed-polytope size threshold is known to polytope theorists, so neither is new. What was not found, in a thin snippet-level sweep, is the specific statement "event-level and support-level checks all pass, a nonnegative finitely additive extension to the pair-generated Dynkin system exists, yet no joint law" together with the size threshold r2 ≥ 4, r3 ≥ 3 in those terms. Closest neighbours: probabilistic-only contextuality (coarser class, includes the visible binary triangle), De Loera–Onn's r=3 slim tables (integer, different question), and de Finetti coherence in statistical matching (Capotorti–Vantaggi, not read at the needed level). Whether it occurs in practice is unknown. Pipelines that derive all couplings from one shared embedding, latent space, or barycentre (Harmony, LIGER, Seurat CCA space, uniPort, MMOT outputs, Private-PGM) glue by construction, so the question is vacuous there. The question is live only where couplings or tables are estimated separately and then compared round a loop: pairwise OT couplings (SCOT-type, Scanorama-type pairwise stitching), separately released or noisy pairwise tables (disclosure, federated), or statistical matching of three files. Even there, real noisy estimates would most plausibly fail visibly, and I have not shown that the hidden region has non-empty interior in the polytope (unverified, ⟦HAND⟧ to check), so "occurs in practice" is open. Honest distance: this is a lens (can supports and coarse checks certify a merge?), not a statement that any named method is wrong. Absence claims rest on snippets; re-run before relying.

## Addendum (main session, 2026-10-22): the two open checks

- **Binary triangle (a, a, −a), 1/3 < a < 1.** ⟦HAND, checked⟧ With ±1 variables and uniform marginals, a joint exists iff the correlations satisfy the triangle cut-polytope inequalities. One of them, −c₁₂ − c₂₃ + c₁₃ ≥ −1, reads −3a ≥ −1, so it fails for a > 1/3. For a < 1 every pair outcome has positive probability, so supports glue (no logical contextuality): this is Abramsky–Brandenburger probabilistic-only. At 2×2×2 the classification says hiding is impossible (r₃ = 2), so the failure is VISIBLE in the programme's sense. Conclusion: the hidden class is strictly smaller than AB's probabilistic contextuality, not a renaming of it.
- **Hidden region has non-empty interior.** Already settled in-repo: at 3×4×4 the hidden region is full-dimensional, about 29% of R by volume (`definitions_draft.md` §19). [exact/float label as recorded there]

## 1. The question

*Answers first.* Questions and consistent answer patterns (states) are primitive. A world, whether
actual (a point) or ideal (an ultrafilter on the possibilities), is something answers *earn* by
fitting together. Only the space of possibilities Ω is assumed, and it is scaffold.

Our states are **complete**: every askable question gets yes or no. They are also **coherent**,
since the answers add up. So the theory studies one gap:

> **complete + coherent, yet no world.**

In classical logic the completeness theorem closes this gap: every complete consistent theory has
a model. That relies on every "and" being askable, a Boolean board. Glued boards are not Boolean.
The theory asks where, and why, completeness fails for them.

## 2. The puzzle

Penrose's staircase has every flight going up and the stairs closing into a loop. Each flight is
fine; no building is. On the triangle (three landings, three flights) the contradiction can only
show up in one question: *"do the three heights go round the staircase's way?"* That question
cannot be built from flight questions, not even in infinitely many steps. The answers stay
coherent because the only question that would expose them is unaskable.

**On "query" and "measurement" (C1).** In Abramsky–Brandenburger terms a repo query is a *context* (a set of
coordinates read together), not a *measurement* (one coordinate). Lean's `Query` is any measurable outcome type,
so a two-coordinate window is one `Query`. "Measurement" is therefore a loose translation.

**Warning on "pointless" (C10).** Here *pointless* means: no ultrafilter on Ω induces the state, i.e. it has no
two-valued extension to all subsets of Ω. It is **not** pointless topology (locales, frames without points).
On infinite Ω it is **not** Pták's "free" state (free = non-Dirac, which includes ideal points). It is
**weaker** than De Simone–Navara–Pták non-extendability, which rules out every [0,1]-valued or signed extension too.

**On the name "pointless coherence".** The name was chosen before the prior-art pass. The problem it names, the finitely
additive extension problem for two-valued states on concrete logics, has ancestors: Pták's free and concentrated hidden
variables, Bhaskara Rao's extension theorems for charges, and the finite non-extendable logics of De Simone, Navara and
Pták. The stance "answers first" has ancestors too: locales (point-free topology) and de Finetti's previsions. Credit these
wherever the name is used for others.

## 4. Structure theorems

**T1. When coherence earns a world** (pair windows, infinitely many values unless stated).

| Regime | Coherent ⇒ world? | Status |
|---|---|---|
| finitely many values | yes, actual | hand (`prop:sigma`); A=2 enumerated |
| adding over the whole infinity (σ) | yes, actual | hand; Lean on rings |
| finite adding, forest | yes, ideal | hand (`thm:path`) |
| finite adding, any loop | no: pointless states exist | Lean (rings L ≥ 4, triangle) + hand transport |

**So: pointless coherence ⟺ finite adding ∧ infinitely many values ∧ a loop.** The dichotomy
"pointless ⟺ not a forest" is the failed completeness theorem, located exactly.

**T2. Extension is never the obstruction** (so far).
- On triangle-free graphs of finite degree, every consistent window family extends to a unique
  state (hand corollary of locality).
- On the triangle, every tensor-type family extends. Status:
  - Lean for one representative of each of the three twisted classes (`triangle_pointless_state`,
    `triangle_ddp_pointless`, `triangle_dpp_pointless`);
  - hand for the symmetry transport;
  - hand sketch for distinct slot ultrafilters.
- Consequence: **pointless = the window 2-types do not glue into one ultrafilter.**

**T3. What the board contributes: one unaskable question.**
- No set that agrees with the cyclic (or anticyclic) order on some infinite P³ is buildable.
  Status: Lean (`not_inP_of_mem`).
- Mechanism:
  - the *balance rule*: every buildable question balances on every 2×2×2 box;
  - the *lift*: raising the lowest landing above the highest never changes a buildable answer.
- This single exclusion absorbs every twist checked.

**T4. Gauge versus order.**
- Loops made only of gauge windows always glue: a monodromy F with F₊p = p fixes a p-large set
  (Katětov). Status: hand.
- For tensor types: twisted ⟺ the orientations of the order windows, after contracting the gauge
  windows, contain a directed cycle. Status: hand sketch, not banked.
- For tensor types, the twist is an *acyclicity* invariant, not the winding number the note once
  hoped for. **Scope: tensor types only.** NT1 (the update at the end) shows that orientation is not
  the whole invariant in general.

**T5. Motion is not impossibility.**
- Invariance under a group action only detects symmetry breaking. An invariant ideal point exists
  iff the family lives on the diagonal. Status: hand (degeneracy lemma).
- **The twist lives in the loop, not in the action.**

## 5. Thesis (conjecture)

> **Pointless coherence is exactly a cycle of "beyond".**
>
> **REFUTED AS STATED (2026-10-17, hand; see the update at the end).** It holds for tensor types,
> by hand sketch, and fails for sum-type windows.

"Drawn beyond every number" is the potential infinite's own direction. Each local "beyond" is
consistent. A cycle of them is a task that, carried round the loop, would have to finish before it
started. Relabeling (gauge), motion (group actions) and actual-infinite adding (σ) never produce
it.

What would refute the thesis: a twist built from window 2-types that are neither "beyond" nor
"renamed". That is probe NT1 below.

## Update 2026-10-17: NT1 fired (a). The thesis is false as stated ⟦HAND — unverified⟧

**The family is twisted although its orientation is acyclic**: for any p containing a lacunary set, and by the composition law (2026-10-19, hand) for EVERY non-principal p.

**Witness.**
- Take S ∈ p+p and B = {b : S − b ∈ p} ∈ p.
- X = {(a, a+b) : b ∈ B} has value 1 in window 01.
- Y = {(y, y+c) : c ∈ ⋂_{b∈B, b≤y} (S − b)} has value 1 in window 12. The intersection is finite,
  so it lies in p.
- Together X and Y force x₂ − x₀ ∈ S.
- Z = {x₂ − x₀ ∉ S} has value 1 in window 02 unless p+p = (−p+p) + (p+p) in βℤ.

**Why that equation fails.**
- −p+p is non-principal.
- p is right-cancellable when it contains a set with gaps tending to ∞: translated tails of the
  set give disjoint witnesses.
- So p+p is right-cancellable too, and the equation would force −p+p = 0, a contradiction.
- Hence X ∩ Y ∩ Z = ∅, and no ultrafilter glues the windows.

**What it means.**
- "Beyond" is not just a direction. Beyonds *compose*. The difference x₂ − x₀ has to be the
  composite of x₁ − x₀ and x₂ − x₁, computed in the non-commutative semigroup (βℤ, +).
- An independent window asks for a *different* composite, so the loop fails to close even
  though nothing goes round in a circle.
- **Provable narrower statement ⟦HAND⟧: translation-type triangle.** Suppose every window has the
  form law(a, a + d_ij) with d_ij drawn beyond. Then the family is flat ⟺ d₀₂ = d₀₁ + d₁₂ in
  (βℕ, +).
  - ⇐: the natural glue (a, a+b, a+b+c) works.
  - ⇒: the NT1 forcing puts every S ∈ d₀₁ + d₁₂ into d₀₂.
- **Heuristic, not proved:** use the difference map δ(u) = law(x_j − x_i) ∈ βℤ. For a product,
  δ(p⊗q) = −p + q. Twist might then be a failed composition (cocycle) law for δ, with orientation
  as its sign shadow. But a product window's difference law varies with the fibre, and the forcing
  needs the "drawn after" structure. So the general case is OPEN.
- **Owned ingredients to check:** Hindman–Strauss on right cancellation (Ch. 8) and the algebra of
  βℤ.
- **Open:** (ii) does this family still extend to a state on D₃, i.e. does the board absorb a
  non-orientation twist too?

**Priority 2, hand reading: what "beyond" is in a finite world** ⟦HAND; RESCOPED on review⟧
- *A single query.* If both slots of a query have the same law and P(up) = 1 − ε, then

  ℕ, "always up" with equal marginals forces mass to escape to infinity (H3). **This is a fact about
  one query, not about the loop.**
- *The loop.* The cycle inequality Σ P(down) ≥ 1 holds for every mixture of points (classical). It
  never "escapes" (see the box-limit lemma below).
- *WITHDRAWN:* the slogan "finite twists are wraps, infinite twists are escapes", and the claim
  that renaming can twist only in finite worlds. A translation-invariant density charge d on ℕ has
  d + 1 = d, so the "+1 per flight" loop is consistent with charges. On a ring it extends by
  locality and is non-coherent. **The regime split is two-valued vs probabilistic, not finite vs
  infinite.**

## Session 2026-10-18 (cont.): box limits ⟦HAND; corrected on review⟧

**Box-limit lemma (any window graph).** Suppose finite states s_N are carried to the ℕ^V carrier by
E ↦ E ∩ [0,N)^V and each s_N is a mixture of points (a law P_N). Then every ultrafilter limit is
**coherent**: P(E) := lim P_N(E ∩ box) is a charge on all sets that fits it. So box limits of
mixtures of points never produce pointless states, and the loop's unit of "down" never escapes,
being a finite sum of limits.

**Corollary (what is true about wraps).**
- *Pointless idealised states are box limits only of finite POINTLESS states.*
- *Rings.* Rings have finite pointless states, because the finite ring is blind (locality). Example:
  x_{k+1} = x_k + U mod N with U uniform on {1..w} is already non-joint once L(w+1)/(2N) < 1. Its
  box limits are pointless; the limit carries finite pointlessness to infinity, it does not create
  it.
- *Rings, directly (a near-immediate lemma).* Any consistent family of query *charges* on a ring
  extends by locality: s(E) = c₋(A) + c₊(B) ≤ m(S) + m(Sᶜ) = 1.
- *The triangle.* If the finite triangle board equals the joint polytope at EVERY alphabet size
  used along the limit (proven only at A = 3), then the triangle's pointless states are not box
  limits of finite states: pure idealisation. This is scoped to box approximations.

## 2026-10-19: the composition law for forward window types ⟦HAND — unverified⟧

**Definition.** A 2-type u on (x_i, x_j) is *forward* (over x_i) if:
- it is fibred over x_i: T ∈ u ⟺ {a : T_a ∈ u_a} ∈ m_i, where m_i is the x_i-marginal and u_a an
  ultrafilter on x_j; and
- it contains a set on which each value of x_j comes from only finitely many values of x_i.

Examples:
- products p⊗q with x_j drawn after x_i (fibre q; restrict to x_j > x_i);
- graphs of injective maps (fibre δ_{f(a)});
- translations x_j = x_i + d with d ~ w drawn after (fibre a + w).

**Composite.** (u₀₁∘u₁₂) is fibred over x₀ with fibre at a equal to the u₀₁_a-limit over b of
u₁₂_b. Explicitly: S is in the fibre iff {b : S ∈ (u₁₂)_b} ∈ (u₀₁)_a.

**Forcing lemma.** If u₀₁ and u₁₂ are forward, every amalgam U on ℕ³ of u₀₁ and u₁₂ projects to
u₀₁∘u₁₂ on (x₀, x₂).
- *Proof.* Take T in the composite and A = {a : T_a in the composite fibre} ∈ p.
- For a ∈ A, set B_a = {b : T_a ∈ (u₁₂)_b} ∈ (u₀₁)_a.
- X = {(a, b) : a ∈ A, b ∈ B_a}, intersected with the finite-to-one set, lies in u₀₁.
- Set C_y = ⋂ {T_a : (a, y) ∈ X}. This is a finite intersection, so C_y ∈ (u₁₂)_y, and
  Y = {(y, c) : c ∈ C_y} lies in u₁₂.
- So X × Y forces (x₀, x₂) ∈ T.

**Triangle theorem.** For forward u₀₁ and u₁₂, the triangle is flat ⟺ u₀₂ = u₀₁∘u₁₂. The
natural iterated amalgam realises the composite.

**Loops.** Around a loop cut at one window, with all other windows forward along the path, the loop
is flat ⟺ the closing window equals the composite of the others. **The twist is the holonomy of
composition.**

**It unifies three earlier results:**
- *products:* the orientation / directed-cycle criterion;
- *bijections:* flat ⟺ trivial monodromy;
- *sums:* flat ⟺ d₀₂ = d₀₁ + d₁₂.

It also STRENGTHENS NT1 to every non-principal p, not just lacunary ones:
- the forced (x₀, x₂) has fibres a + w, while a product has constant fibres;
- equality would need a + w = a′ + w for two distinct a, a′;
- translation has no fixed point on non-principal ultrafilters (Katětov).

**Additions after review (2026-10-19).**
- The same X/Y construction on sets in ℕ³ shows the *whole* amalgam is unique, not just its
  (x₀, x₂) projection.
- Composites of forward types are forward: they are fibred, and finite-to-one through b. That is
  what makes the loop induction legitimate.
- **Scope of "unifies orientation".** It covers only orientations that admit a forward path
  through every window except the closing one. That includes every acyclic triangle (by a
  topological order) and every directed cycle. It does NOT cover alternating rings such as

  the loop statement.

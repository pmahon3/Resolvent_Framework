# The shape of the theory (2026-10-17)

reviewed: false. Consolidation after the triangle, extension and dynamics probes. Status labels:
**Lean**, **hand**, **conjecture**. The detail lives in `glued_carriers_taxonomy.json` and in
`../delay_embedding/collision_vs_generation.md`. The narrative lives in
`pointless_coherence.tex`.

## 1. The question

*Answers first.* Questions and consistent answer patterns (states) are primitive. A world, whether
actual (a point) or ideal (an ultrafilter on the possibilities), is something answers *earn* by
fitting together. Only the space of possibilities Ω is assumed, and it is scaffold.

Our states are **complete**: every askable question gets yes or no. They are also **coherent**,
since the answers add up. So the theory studies one gap:

> **complete + coherent, yet no world.**

In classical logic the completeness theorem closes this gap: every complete consistent theory has
a model. That relies on every "and" being askable, a Boolean board. Glued boards are not Boolean.
The theory asks where, and why, completeness fails for them.

## 2. The puzzle

Penrose's staircase has every flight going up and the stairs closing into a loop. Each flight is
fine; no building is. On the triangle (three landings, three flights) the contradiction can only
show up in one question: *"do the three heights go round the staircase's way?"* That question
cannot be built from flight questions, not even in infinitely many steps. The answers stay
coherent because the only question that would expose them is unaskable.

## 4. Structure theorems

**T1. When coherence earns a world** (pair windows, infinitely many values unless stated).

| Regime | Coherent ⇒ world? | Status |
|---|---|---|
| finitely many values | yes, actual | hand (`prop:sigma`); A=2 enumerated |
| adding over the whole infinity (σ) | yes, actual | hand; Lean on rings |
| finite adding, forest | yes, ideal | hand (`thm:path`) |
| finite adding, any loop | no: pointless states exist | Lean (rings L ≥ 4, triangle) + hand transport |

**So: pointless coherence ⟺ finite adding ∧ infinitely many values ∧ a loop.** The dichotomy
"pointless ⟺ not a forest" is the failed completeness theorem, located exactly.

**T2. Extension is never the obstruction** (so far).
- On triangle-free graphs of finite degree, every consistent window family extends to a unique
  state (hand corollary of locality).
- On the triangle, every tensor-type family extends. Status:
  - Lean for one representative of each of the three twisted classes (`triangle_pointless_state`,
    `triangle_ddp_pointless`, `triangle_dpp_pointless`);
  - hand for the symmetry transport;
  - hand sketch for distinct slot ultrafilters.
- Consequence: **pointless = the window 2-types do not glue into one ultrafilter.**

**T3. What the board contributes: one unaskable question.**
- No set that agrees with the cyclic (or anticyclic) order on some infinite P³ is buildable.
  Status: Lean (`not_inP_of_mem`).
- Mechanism:
  - the *balance rule*: every buildable question balances on every 2×2×2 box;
  - the *lift*: raising the lowest landing above the highest never changes a buildable answer.
- This single exclusion absorbs every twist checked.

**T4. Gauge versus order.**
- Loops made only of gauge windows always glue: a monodromy F with F₊p = p fixes a p-large set
  (Katětov). Status: hand.
- For tensor types: twisted ⟺ the orientations of the order windows, after contracting the gauge
  windows, contain a directed cycle. Status: hand sketch, not banked.
- So the twist is an *acyclicity* invariant, not the winding number the note once hoped for.

**T5. Motion is not impossibility.**
- Invariance under a group action only detects symmetry breaking. An invariant ideal point exists
  iff the family lives on the diagonal. Status: hand (degeneracy lemma).
- **The twist lives in the loop, not in the action.**

## 5. Thesis (conjecture)

> **Pointless coherence is exactly a cycle of "beyond".**

"Drawn beyond every number" is the potential infinite's own direction. Each local "beyond" is
consistent. A cycle of them is a task that, carried round the loop, would have to finish before it
started. Relabeling (gauge), motion (group actions) and actual-infinite adding (σ) never produce
it.

What would refute the thesis: a twist built from window 2-types that are neither "beyond" nor
"renamed". That is probe NT1 below.

## 6. The philosophical reading

| Source | Its claim | What the theory makes precise |
|---|---|---|
| Aristotle, *Physics* III.6 | the infinite is only potential | finite adding is the potential infinite; demanding the actual (σ) collapses every coherent state to a point (T1) |
| Kant, KrV Antinomy §§7–8 | the totality of conditions is *aufgegeben*, a task, not given | each window is right, and the totality read as an object contradicts them; read as a task (an ideal limit) it is coherent; on a loop the task is circular (T1, §5) |
| Specker 1960 | the seer's three boxes: any two opened are consistent, all three are impossible | the triangle; the one unaskable question (T3) |
| Penrose 1991 | impossible figures as cohomology of a loop | the loop is necessary (T1), but the invariant is acyclicity of "beyond", not a winding class (T4) |
| Gödel / ultrafilter theorem | complete consistent theories have models (Boolean) | fails on glued boards exactly at loops (T1) |

## 7. What is owned (seam map, short)

- **σ-additive pointless states on sub-boards:** Dravecký–Šipoš 1980 Ex. 6; Navara–Pták 1983.
- **Two-of-three sets with a bounded generator:** Navara 1984.
- **Extension ⟺ coherence:** Bhaskara Rao Thm 3.2.10.
- **Ingredients of the proofs:**
  - p⊗p non-commutativity and pair amalgamation: Goldberg 2022, read in full;
  - ultrafilter Ramsey: Hindman–Strauss;
  - forest gluing: folklore;
  - Katětov's lemma.
- **Not found:** the triangle results (T2, T3), the gauge/order invariant (T4), or the assembled
  dichotomy (T1). Coverage is partial. Still unread: Blass's thesis, Puritz, Hindman–Strauss in
  full.

## 8. Open, in order

1. **NT1, the non-tensor probe.** Is "beyond" the only source of twist?
   - Every off-diagonal 2-type decides {x_i < x_j}, so every window has an orientation or is
     gauge. The real test is therefore an *acyclic* orientation that still fails to glue.
   - The triangle family:
     - (x₀,x₁) = (a, a+b), a sum type;
     - (x₁,x₂) = (y, y+c), a sum type;
     - (x₀,x₂) independent (a product), x₂ beyond.
   - All three windows point forward, so the orientation is acyclic. The natural glue
     (a, a+b, a+b+c) makes x₂ − x₀ correlated with x₀, which the product window forbids.
   - **If it is twisted, the thesis fails**: "beyond" composes, and the invariant needs the
     algebra of (βℕ, +). That is Hindman–Strauss territory.
   - Pre-registered as `gate.nontensor_sum_type`.
2. **Wider windows.** How much joint seeing closes the gap? A window that sees the whole triangle
   makes it Boolean. The conjecture is the hypergraph analogue: pointless ⟺ the window
   hypergraph is not acyclic.
3. **No background.** Drop Ω and keep only the questions. Does pointless coherence survive when
   there is no space to lack a point in?

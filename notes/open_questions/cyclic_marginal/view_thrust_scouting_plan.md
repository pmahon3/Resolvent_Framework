# Scouting plan: the "view" thrust (2026-10-20)

reviewed: false. This is a plan, not results.

## The thrust, stated so it can fail

**Mathematical core (clock case, triangle).** Questions about relations only, relata only, or both
combined by "not" and "either one" are all blind to the clock twist. Questions about **what a pair
shows jointly** (the view) expose it. (`definitions_draft.md` §14; exact mod 2 and mod 3; hand
argument over ℤ.)

**Claimed significance (to be tested):**
- The relations/relata partition is incommensurate with where inconsistency becomes detectable.
- The working unit is the joint local view.

**Applied form (to be tested):**
- Pipelines that keep only summaries can be blind to inconsistencies that joint pair records
  would expose. "Summaries" here means relations (contrasts, correlations) or relata (each unit's
  own score).

## Decision rule (pre-registered)

Bring the thrust to bear only if **both** hold:
1. **Not owned outright.** Either the mathematical or philosophical statement is open, or it is
   owned but the applied translation has not been made (Type 3 / Type 6 bar).
2. **An applied foothold exists.** There is a domain where all of these are true:
   - (a) joint pair records exist but are routinely discarded or summarised;
   - (b) inconsistency across a loop of sources matters to practitioners;
   - (c) data or a worked example is accessible to us.

If 1 fails, record the owner and stop. If 2 fails everywhere, the thrust stays theory plus lens.

## Block D: self-checks first (local, exact; no scouting)

1. **Other families.** Run the R / S / J / F test on:
   - order families (Condorcet with scores);
   - a non-gauge probabilistic family;
   - C4 and C5 (rings).

   Does J stay blind while F sees? Pre-register the outcomes: "J blind and F sees" supports the
   thrust; "J sees" weakens it; "F blind" means the case is irrelevant.
2. **The hierarchy.** relations/relata (J) ⊂ pair views (F) ⊂ larger views. Is there a case where
   F is blind but a 3-view sees? The Lean staircase is an instance (F blind; a full view is
   trivially a world). Is the hierarchy strict in general?
3. **The general hand lemma.** Whenever single-variable and single-relation events pairwise meet,
   J is a union class, so it holds every family-wise consistent state. State it and check it, then
   consider Lean.

## Block A: mathematical prior art (one sonnet scout, ~25 searches; shelf first)

- **Contexts as primitive:**
  - Abramsky–Brandenburger measurement covers;
  - Kochen–Specker partial Boolean algebras;
  - Foulis–Randall test spaces;
  - Harding–Heunen–Lindenhovius–Navara.

  Is there a theorem that the algebra generated by single observables (or by functions of them)
  is blind where joint contexts are not?
- **Gudder / Czech school:**
  - summability of observables (Dvurečenskij 1980; Chrapčiaková 1987; Zerbe–Gudder 1985);
  - "logic generated by observables" vs "by contexts".
- **Relational / gauge-invariant observables:**
  - quantum reference frames (Giacomini–Castro-Ruiz–Brukner);
  - relational observables in gauge theory and GR (Rovelli; Dittrich);
  - detecting holonomy with invariant vs non-invariant observables.
- **Finite-quotient detection:** residual finiteness and profinite detection of holonomy or
  cohomology classes.

## Block B: philosophical prior art (one sonnet scout)

- Structural realism (Ladyman, French, Esfeld): relations vs relata, ontic structural realism.
- **Perspectival realism** (Massimi 2022) and relational quantum mechanics (Rovelli). These are the
  most likely owners of "the perspective as the third term". Read them closely.
- The reduction vs complexity synthesis literature (Wimsatt, S. Mitchell, Batterman), so we don't
  reinvent it.

## Block C: applied footholds (one sonnet scout)

- **Network meta-analysis.** Loop inconsistency (Bucher; Lu–Ades). The **arm-based vs
  contrast-based** debate (White; Dias; Hong) maps onto relata (arms) vs relations (contrasts).
  Does anyone use joint (individual-patient, multi-arm) records to detect what contrasts miss?
- **Data fusion / statistical matching:**
  - the conditional independence assumption (D'Orazio et al.);
  - causal data fusion (Bareinboim–Pearl);
  - ecological inference (King).

  Where are joint records unavailable or thrown away?
- **Ecology / environmental:** joint species distribution models from paired vs marginal data;
  coupling multi-scale models (submodels on their own clocks).
- **Psychology:** CbD datasets (on the shelf). Do they keep joint records per context? That is the
  existing practice to learn from.

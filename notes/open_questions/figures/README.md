  non-σ-additive "fake" corners, always reached since S_df is compact) vs
  conv(S_df^σ) (σ-additive corners only); the witness lives in the annulus
  between them, invisible to every finite sub-lattice. Scratch intuition aid, not
  a survey figure: panels 2–3 are SCHEMATIC (real state spaces are
  high-dimensional; only the nesting and the gap are faithful, not the shapes).

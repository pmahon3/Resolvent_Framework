\textbf{Our case, to hold beside the book.} $\Omega=\mathbb N^3$. Three $\sigma$-algebras, one per pair of
coordinates: events depending only on $(x_0,x_1)$, on $(x_1,x_2)$, on $(x_2,x_0)$. $D_3$ = the smallest collection
containing all three and closed under complement and countable disjoint union. A \emph{state} is a finitely
additive $s:D_3\to\{0,1\}$.

\textbf{Words (ours $\to$ likely theirs).} carrier $D_3$ $\to$ concrete ($\sigma$-)logic, $\sigma$-class;\ \
windows $\to$ blocks, Boolean sub-$\sigma$-algebras;\ \ 0/1 state $\to$ two-valued state / measure;\ \
given by a point $\to$ concentrated, carried by a point.

Index words to try: \emph{concrete logic, $\sigma$-class, set-representable, two-valued, concentrated,
pasting, generated, extension, countable, determined, unital / rich / full set of states}.

\textbf{1. Normal form (now a prior-art question: do they own it?).}\\

``every member is a disjoint union of sets, one from each''. We now have (hand, part Lean): every member is three window events $\sqcup$ the pullback of a set on a finite grid; the countable closure adds nothing to the finite one.

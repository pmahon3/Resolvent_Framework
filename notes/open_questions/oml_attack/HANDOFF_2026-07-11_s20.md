## Current normal form

For a coherent cluster face $C_{\mathcal C}\subseteq\Stfa(L)$ and a
countably generated block $M$, let

\[
r_M:C_{\mathcal C}\to U(M)
\]

be restriction to the block's Stone space. T4 for
$(\mathcal C,M)$ is exactly
$r_M(C_{\mathcal C})\cap P_M\ne\varnothing$, where $P_M$ is the
dense set of point ultrafilters; by §14 this is also exactly nonempty
interior of the restriction image and principal-atom reachability.
Maharam (8.2) is unavailable: every
empty-kernel cluster already violates its positivity condition.

## Priority 1 — fine-block principal-atom reachability

Attack the minimal open locus first: a three-element cluster supported
on three blocks and one further countably generated block $M$.

Session-20 sharpening (§14): do not treat local interior as a stronger
interpolation lemma. On a fine block, T4, nonempty interior of the
restriction image, and the proposed basic-open interpolation statement
are equivalent, because every point ultrafilter is isolated by its
signature atom.

Target either:

1. principal-atom reachability: $r_M(C_{\mathcal C})$ contains a
   principal ultrafilter (equivalently, T4/local interior); or
2. a restriction-image construction avoiding every principal atom.

Translate any topological claim into finite algebra before declaring a
win. The exact form is: find an atom $D\in M$ and a global f.a. state
charging both $D$ and the cluster. Taking $A=D$ makes “every block
ultrafilter charging $A$ lifts” tautological, since there is only one.
Record exactly where lattice meets enter; §14 shows that the Stone
equivalence itself uses them only upstream to put the countable
signature intersection in the block.

Exit A: a proved three-block atom-reachability lemma with a credible
induction/generalization route. Exit B: a precise failed implication
whose counterconfiguration names the missing carrier property.

## Priority 2 — co-equal T4 falsification

Search for a nonempty cluster face with
$r_M(C_{\mathcal C})\subseteq U(M)\setminus P_M$. Work backward from
a closed subset of the nonprincipal remainder and ask whether finite
cross-block value constraints can realize it on a concrete σ-class OML.

A free ultrafilter in the image is not enough: failure of T4 requires
the entire restriction image to avoid $P_M$. A failed greedy atom
choice is also not a counterexample.

Exit: either a genuine T4-violating configuration, or a proved
realization obstruction that feeds Priority 1.

## Priority 3 — coarse-block toy

In parallel, seek a concrete σ-class OML with one genuinely coarse
block, one incompatible overlapping block, nontrivial lattice meets,
and a nonprincipal blockwise σ-state. This is an inhabitation probe, not
yet a σ-essential witness.

Exit: an explicit toy with all four properties verified, or a located
failure showing which OML axiom prevents the construction.

## Current normal form

For a coherent cluster face $C_{\mathcal C}$ and countably generated
block $M$,

\[
T4(\mathcal C,M)
\Longleftrightarrow
r_M(C_{\mathcal C})\cap P_M\ne\varnothing
\Longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{int}r_M(C_{\mathcal C})\ne\varnothing.
\]

Equivalently, some global f.a. state charges the cluster and one Boolean
atom of $M$. The last equivalence uses isolation of point ultrafilters by
signature atoms. Local interior/basic-open interpolation is therefore
T4 itself, not a stronger lemma. Maharam (8.2) is unavailable (§12).

## Priority 1 — fibre-to-signature-atom charge

Let $E_1,E_2,E_3$ be a coherent normal-form cluster supported on three
pairwise-incompatible blocks, let $M=\sigma(G_1,G_2,\ldots)$ be a
further countably generated block, and form §15's tree

\[
T_n=\{A\in\operatorname{At}(\operatorname{alg}(G_1,\ldots,G_n)):
\{E_1,E_2,E_3,A\}\text{ is f.a.-coherent}\}.
\]

Each branch has a nonempty compact fibre of cluster-face states with
that finite-generator trace. Crucial correction: the branch does not
determine their restrictions on all of $M$, and even if
$D=\bigcap_nA_n\ne\varnothing$, a f.a. state in the fibre need not charge
$D$. Countable σ-generation does not determine merely f.a. ultrafilters.
Seek either:

1. an OML/cross-block carrier property forcing some branch fibre to
   contain a state charging its nonempty signature atom $D$; or
2. a concrete σ-class OML realization in which every cluster-face state
   restricts nonprincipally to $M$.

Start from finite algebra. Track the exact meet/carrier property used.
Promising proof statements must expose a checkable carrier condition;
merely proving every finite level nonempty, invoking König's lemma,
finding a branch with nonempty set intersection, or restating nonempty
interior is no progress. Finite-level feasibility and nonempty compact
branch fibres are automatic. A single free restriction is not a
counterexample—the entire image must avoid point ultrafilters.

Exit A: a fibre-to-atom charge lemma with a credible extension route.
Exit B: a realizable all-states-remainder T4 violation. Exit C: a
proved realization obstruction naming the missing carrier property.

The simplest falsification attempt is already blocked (§15): assigning
each countable block atom to lie in two cluster sets and be disjoint from
the third makes each pairwise cluster intersection a countable union of
block atoms, hence a carrier element, hence makes the pair compatible.
Any counterexample must use subtler global state constraints.

The weakest proof bridge is also dead (§15): the finite Greechie
pentagon has coherent incompatible value-one pairs with lattice meet
zero (explicit $a_2,a_6$ witness). Do not infer a nonzero cross-block
meet from coherence without a new carrier hypothesis.

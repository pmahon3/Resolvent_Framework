## Banked normal form

For a cluster face $C_{\mathcal C}$ and fine block
$M=\sigma(G_1,G_2,\ldots)$, T4 means that the restriction image contains
a point ultrafilter, equivalently a state charging one signature atom.
Finite generator traces form branches with nonempty compact state
fibres, but do not determine f.a. restrictions on $M$.

For a branch of finite-stage atoms $(A_n)$ and
$D=\bigcap_nA_n$, §16 proves:

- BC: some feasible branch has $D\ne\varnothing$;
- FS: at some stage $\mathcal C\cup\{A_n\setminus D\}$ is
  f.a.-incoherent;
- BC + FS implies T4.

The finite Greechie pentagon kills any automatic inference from
cross-block coherence to nonzero meet.

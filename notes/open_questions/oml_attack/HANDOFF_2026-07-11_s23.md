## Current boundary

T4 asks whether a three-block cluster face reaches a principal
ultrafilter of every further fine block. Local interior is equivalent to
T4. Finite generator branches have compact fibres but do not determine
f.a. restrictions on the generated σ-field.

BC + finite-stage fibre saturation is sufficient, but §17 shows FS is
exactly universal principality of a whole branch fibre and fails even in
$\mathcal P(\mathbb N_0)$ when that fibre already contains a Dirac.
Do not pursue FS from latticehood alone.

## Priority 1 — contextual cyclic propagation

A T4 counterexample may use a countably atomic block
$M=\sigma(D_n)$ and force every cluster-face state to satisfy
$\mu(D_n)=0$ for all $n$. Countably many compatible complements
$H_n=D_n^c$ can support a free ultrafilter, so σ-completeness alone does
not stop this.

Known construction exclusions:

- atomwise three-colouring makes cluster pairs compatible;
- one reused or finitely many compatible value-one carriers contradict
  σ-completeness;
- shared-tail exact-one equations force literal equality of the
  distinguished carrier sets.

Attack either:

1. construct genuinely contextual/cyclic finite exact-one gadgets whose
   global state equations force every $H_n=1$ from one fixed coherent
   three-cluster, without any shared-tail cancellation, and verify the
   countable concrete carrier is a σ-complete OML; or
2. prove that OML latticehood turns every such countable propagation
   into one of the excluded carrier mechanisms or otherwise forces a
   principal restriction.

Exit A: a verified all-remainder OML configuration. Exit B: a structural
lattice obstruction covering a nontrivial class of contextual gadgets.
Exit C: a narrower exact gadget class fully classified, with the next
uncovered class named.

## Guardrails

A single free restriction is not a T4 counterexample. Failure of FS is
not a T4 counterexample. Cross-block value-one coherence does not imply
nonzero meet. Statewise equality of values is not equality of carrier
sets unless the partition equations justify it. Verify latticehood and
σ-closure separately from state equations.

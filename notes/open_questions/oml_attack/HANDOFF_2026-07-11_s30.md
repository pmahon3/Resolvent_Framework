## New necessary screen

For a periodic relay, form the finite automaton of pentagon states under
the inter-cell identifications. Mark a state σ-live if it lies on an
infinite path whose master-target word contains exactly one $1$.

A viable concrete candidate needs:

1. σ-live states order-determine the pentagon in every cell;
2. compatible σ-live paths separate cross-cell non-orders;
3. the full cluster state is outside the σ-live set and admits the free
   all-zero-target path.

Finite proper-trace escape does not imply σ-liveness.

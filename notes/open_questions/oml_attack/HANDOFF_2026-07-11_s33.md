## Guardrails / lessons

- The advisor's period-collapse + rotation-invariance test is the cheap
  known-answer check that caught this. Run it on ANY new automaton.
- Never validate an automaton against itself. The s32 reproduction did
  exactly that and hid the bug for a whole session. Always cross-check
  against an independent from-scratch path enumerator.
- The face census (static) and the girth/master geometry (incidence)
  are trustworthy; the σ-liveness dynamics are the fragile part —
  triangulate them hardest.

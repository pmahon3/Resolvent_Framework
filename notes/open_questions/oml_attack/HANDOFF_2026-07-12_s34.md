# OML lattice attack — session 34 handoff

## Resolution: the late s33 σ-liveness alarm was a convention mismatch

`relay_core.analyze.live` correctly implements the convention stated in
its module and used by the censuses:

- the relay is the one-sided chain of cells `n = 0,1,2,...`;
- cell 0 is unconstrained on the left; and
- a phase-r state is live when it occurs at some position `n ≡ r (mod p)`
  of a rooted infinite path whose whole target word has exactly one 1.

Under this convention, period collapse `[m] = [m,m,m]` phase-by-phase is
not expected: phase 1 or 2 requires a compatible finite prefix from cell
0.  Cyclic rotation also moves the distinguished boundary.  Those tests
belong to the two-sided/translation-invariant convention implemented by
`live_fix.live_any`, which is a different question.

## Independent validation

`s34_liveness_oracle.py` constructs the phase/state graph from scratch,
detects zero-letter infinite continuations by explicit cycle search, and
does a rooted product search on `(vertex, ones_so_far, wanted_seen)`.
It does not call `analyze` or reuse its fixpoints.

Agreement obtained:

- all 4,150 period-1 one-port and two-port maps; and
- 600 seeded random period-1/2/3 relays.

Therefore no liveness fix or census rerun is needed.  The s32
`4050→1000→275→45→0` census and the s33 k≥3/two-cell and three-cell
results stand.  Propagated attack/taxonomy/frontier/shovel warnings were
corrected in s34.

## Headline for E4

- **(a1) Non-extendable states DO exist in print, on concrete
  carriers.** The cluster is De Simone–Navara–Pták:
  - *"Extending states on finite concrete logics"*, IJTP 44 (2005),
    1087–1093 (= arXiv:math-ph/0311012): even-cardinality-subsets
    concrete logic on a finite even set; characterizes exactly when
    states (and signed states) extend to the full power-set field.
    Finite, concrete, OMP; not σ-relevant by itself.
  - *"States on systems of sets that are closed under symmetric
    difference"*, Math. Nachrichten (2015, Wiley): reportedly
    constructs a difference-closed system on which **even a two-valued
    state fails to extend**, and treats a **σ-complete version**.
    ⚠ PAYWALLED — content recovered from secondary snippets only.
    **This is the E4-critical item**: if the σ-complete construction is
    a LATTICE (or adaptable to one), it enters the candidate pipeline;
    if OMP-only or non-lattice, it feeds the "engine must act at
    inclusion 2" evidence line. PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED
    (the repo's own hostile-prior-art lesson applies).
  - Stateless companions (Order 2022 ODL; arXiv:2401.13651;
    Navara–Pták augmentation-property OMP, dml.cz/dmlcz/153529):
    extreme cases (empty/singleton state space), context not witnesses.
  - Pták, *set-representable point-distinguishing OMPs*, IJTP 2023 /
    arXiv:2401.13798: a class where every two-valued state is Dirac —
    the positive direction of the E1 lemma's equivalence, realized.
- **(a2) The FIP ⟺ extendable ⟺ point-limit packaging was NOT found
  published in that form for concrete OMPs/OMLs.** The Boolean-level
  fact is textbook Stone-tradition folklore (Sikorski). Per the seed,
  this changes nothing: no novelty is claimed on the lemma; cite
  Stone/Sikorski folklore + the DNP cluster for the OMP instances.
- **(a3) The screen use (non-extendability ⟹ not a pointwise limit of
  σ-states) was NOT found anywhere.** Genuinely empty across
  quantum-logic, Boolean, operator-algebra sweeps — high scout
  confidence this is a gap, not a search failure. Seed §6(a) kill
  condition (reduction published WITH the screen already run) does
  **NOT** fire.
- **(b) Linearization:** Bunce–Wright Mackey–Gleason (Bull. AMS 26
  (1992) 288–293 = arXiv:math/9204228; JLMS 46 (1992) 269–279; JLMS 49
  (1994) 133–149 vector case; 2025 JBW* follow-up arXiv:2509.03213) is
  strictly a von-Neumann/JBW* projection-lattice result. **No
  concrete/set-representable-OML analogue in print.** The one
  circle-crossing paper is Bunce–Navara–Pták–Wright, *Quantum logics
  with Jauch–Piron states*, Quart. J. Math. Oxford 36 (1985) 261–271
  (already the attack note's "critical citation gap" BNPW 1985).
  **Loomis–Sikorski for σ-OMLs: neither theorem nor explicit no-go
  found** — only Boolean (original), σ-MV (Mundici tradition; Algebra
  Universalis 2008), monotone σ-complete effect algebras
  (Buhagiar–Chetcuti–Dvurečenskij, FSS 2006), EMV (arXiv:1707.00270),
  and adjacent versions. The ⟦HAND⟧ Bunce–Wright/Φ-tameness-of-L(H)
  comparison: **no direct published statement** connecting
  Gleason/Mackey–Gleason to pointwise σ-approximability of f.a. states
  on L(H); nearest neighborhood = noncommutative Yosida–Hewitt
  decomposition literature. The comparison stays ⟦HAND⟧, now with a
  verified empty-neighborhood check.

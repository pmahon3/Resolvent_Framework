# Bipartite grouped-output coordinate core

## Scope

This note isolates the coordinate skeleton that appears when more than one
shared hub coordinate is coupled to more than one private output.  It contains
no conditional-enforcement gadgets.  Its purpose is to identify, before a full
cell census, the mixed cuts that those gadgets or the concrete completion must
repair.

For finite sets `A` and `I`, let

\[
 \Omega_{A,I}=\{0,1\}^{A\sqcup I}
\]

with coordinates \(q_\alpha\) (\(\alpha\in A\)) and \(r_i\)
(\(i\in I\)).  For every edge \((\alpha,i)\), let
\(B_{\alpha i}\) be the 16-element Boolean algebra generated by
\(q_\alpha,r_i\).  Define the raw core and concrete orthogonal closure by

\[
 C^0_{A,I}=\bigcup_{(\alpha,i)\in A\times I}B_{\alpha i},\qquad
 C^\perp_{A,I}=\langle C^0_{A,I}\rangle_{\complement,\,\dot\cup}
 \subseteq\mathcal P(\Omega_{A,I}).
\]

The executable certificate is
`../verification/bipartite_coordinate_core_audit.py`; its deterministic
receipts cover \(K_{2,2},K_{2,3},K_{3,2}\).

## Exact finite census

| core | points | raw events | completed events | closure increments | support histogram | Boolean compatibility blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| \(K_{2,2}\) | 16 | 50 | 82 | 32, 0 | 0:2, 1:8, 2:40, 3:32 | 12 blocks of size 16 |
| \(K_{2,3}\) | 32 | 72 | 144 | 72, 0 | 0:2, 1:10, 2:60, 3:72 | 24 blocks of size 16 |
| \(K_{3,2}\) | 32 | 72 | 144 | 72, 0 | 0:2, 1:10, 2:60, 3:72 | 24 blocks of size 16 |

In all three cases the set-Boolean centre is \(\{0,1\}\).  Each declared
edge boundary is present, as it must be, but no pair of distinct hub
coordinates and no pair of distinct private outputs has its joint four-atom
Boolean algebra reconstructed.

Nevertheless, the complement/disjoint-union closure is **not a lattice**.
Already in \(K_{2,2}\), the crossed edge events represented by masks `0x33`
(supported on \(q_0,r_0\)) and `0x505` (supported on \(q_1,r_1\)) have two
incomparable minimal upper bounds: `0x3f3f` (supported on \(q_1,r_0\)) and
`0x55ff` (supported on \(q_0,r_1\)).  The receipt records the analogous first
failure in the other two cases.  Thus one round of concrete orthogonal closure
does not solve the crossed-edge lattice cut.

**Evidence class:** exhaustive finite evidence plus executable verification.
No arbitrary-cardinality theorem is inferred.  “Maximal blocks” in the table
means maximal cliques for set-Boolean compatibility (all four truth cells are
events); because the family is not a lattice, these are not promoted to OML
maximal blocks.

## Consequence for the scalable construction

Pair-local repair of a one-hub/many-output paste does not automatically extend
to two grouped hub coordinates.  The first new obstruction is already the
rectangle \((\alpha,i),(\beta,j),(\beta,i),(\alpha,j)\): crossed opposite-edge
events have their competing upper bounds on the other two edges.  Any proposed
finite normal form for the full construction must therefore include a
rectangle-repair rule or prove that the conditional-cell gadgets select one of
the two bounds.  A normal form containing only independently repaired rows is
refuted by this core.

## Full conditional-cell \(2\times2\) gate

Take four independently renamed copies
\(D_{\alpha i}\), \(\alpha,i\in\{0,1\}\), of the verified 56-event literal
`MO3` cell.  Within row \(\alpha\), identify the three activation literals
across its two cells (giving a row activation triple
\(a_{\alpha1},a_{\alpha2},a_{\alpha3}\)) and identify their input events as
one common \(q_\alpha\).  Within column \(i\), identify the two cells' output
events as one common \(r_i\).  Make no other identifications.
Use the compatible-state fibre product as the concrete carrier, take the union
of the four pulled-back cell families, and compute the least
complement/disjoint-union-closed concrete family.

The gate passes only if an exhaustive audit establishes all of the following:

1. every pair has a unique meet and join and the orthomodular law holds;
2. the four cell projections retain exactly their 30-profile conditional
   relations, with full off-activation freedom;
3. on simultaneous activation the joint relation is exactly
   \(q_0=q_1=r_0=r_1\), while partial activations impose only their intended
   edge equalities;
4. the crossed-edge lattice cut above is repaired, with every added event
   classified, and closure stabilizes;
5. neither \((q_0,q_1)\) nor \((r_0,r_1)\) acquires a joint Boolean boundary;
6. no activation cylinder becomes an event, each nonzero event has an
   off-cylinder charging state, and two-valued states separate order;
7. the centre is trivial and all actual maximal blocks are exhaustively
   classified, including blocks mixing rows or columns.

If latticehood fails, the certificate must give the first failing pair and all
incomparable extrema.  If it passes, the next gate is \(K_{2,3}\), because the
coordinate census shows that rectangles overlap there and can create a first
three-column interaction invisible at \(2\times2\).

**Status:** open.  This is the smallest falsifiable grouped-output full-cell
test implied by the stripped-core obstruction.

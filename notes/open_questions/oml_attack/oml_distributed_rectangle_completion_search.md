# Distributed-preserving rectangle completion

*Campaign 17, 2026-07-14. Exact finite search problem and proof certificate.*

## 1. Search class

Let `Omega=2^4`, with coordinates `q0,q1,r0,r1`, and let `E0` be the
least complement/disjoint-union-closed family containing the four edge
Boolean algebras `Bool(q_a,r_i)`.  The verified seed has 82 events and is not
a lattice.  The finite question is whether there is a family

\[
 E_0\subseteq E\subseteq\mathcal P(\Omega)
\]

which is a concrete OML, omits both `Bool(q0,q1)` and `Bool(r0,r1)`, and has
centre `{0,1}`.  This question concerns macro-saturated completions inside
the fixed sixteen-profile carrier only.  It does not quantify over repairs
which split auxiliary fibres of the full conditional cell.

## 2. Complete interval branching

Suppose `E` is complement/disjoint-union closed and `x,y in E` have no join.
In every concrete OML extension `F` of `E`, the event `z=x join_F y` satisfies

\[
 x\cup y\subseteq z\subseteq
 \bigcap\{u\in E:x\subseteq u\text{ and }y\subseteq u\}.
\]

It is enough on the right to intersect all current minimal upper bounds,
because every current upper bound dominates a minimal one.  Conversely, the
branch indexed by the actual join in any target extension adjoins that event
and its complement, then takes the least orthogonal closure.  Repeating this
operation on one deterministically selected join failure therefore gives a
complete finite search, provided **every** subset in the displayed interval
is enumerated.  A meet-only failure is converted to the complementary join
failure.  Later intervals must not be assumed to have the two-bit form of the
root cut.  **Evidence class: hand proved.**

## 3. Symmetry and safe pruning

The seed has the order-128 point-permutation group

\[
 (C_2)^4\rtimes((S_2\times S_2)\rtimes C_2),
\]

generated by independent coordinate flips, swaps within the `q` and `r`
sides, and interchange of the two sides.  These permutations preserve the
seed, complement/disjoint closure, latticehood, orthomodularity, centre size,
and the unordered property that a same-side Boolean algebra is reconstructed.
Whole closed event families may therefore be canonicalized under this group.
Candidate repairs at a fixed non-invariant node may be quotiented only by the
node stabilizer; alternatively canonicalize every resulting child globally.
**Evidence class: hand proved; generator checks required in the executable
certificate.**

Reconstruction of either same-side Boolean algebra is upward-monotone, so a
node containing all four atoms of either algebra can be pruned.  A nontrivial
centre is not monotone under adjoining events: later events may destroy
commutation.  Centre size, current nonlatticehood, current orthomodular-law
failure, support size, or partial boundary reconstruction are therefore not
sound nonterminal pruning criteria.  **Evidence class: hand proved.**

## 4. Literal-atom forcing lemma

If a complement/disjoint-union-closed family containing the unary coordinates
`q0,q1` contains even one literal truth atom

\[
 q_0^\epsilon\cap q_1^\delta,
\]

then it contains all four such atoms and hence `Bool(q0,q1)`.  Indeed,
complement and orthogonal differences first split the charged unary half and
then its opposite half.  The same statement holds for `r0,r1`.  This explains
why the two known terminal branches reconstruct a side once the literal mask
`0xf` appears.  It does not prove that every completion must introduce such
an atom.  **Evidence class: hand proved.**

## 5. Required negative certificate

An exhaustive no-go receipt must serialize every complete closed node, record
the chosen failed pair and all extrema, enumerate the full admissible interval
or certified stabilizer orbits, and identify every child as recursive,
duplicate, or monotone-reconstruction-pruned.  Hashes may index nodes, but
canonical event-set equality must resolve collisions.  Every terminal node
must be independently checked for closure, all binary extrema, the
orthomodular law, both missing same-side boundaries, exact centre, and
containment of the seed edge algebras.  A root proof-DAG receipt, not a global
visited count, is required for an exhaustive negative theorem.

At shallow depth, symmetry-off and symmetry-on child expansions must agree
after canonicalization.  No negative result in this finite class excludes a
fine-fibre full-cell repair.

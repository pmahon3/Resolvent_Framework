## 1. The cell

Use the following 24 atoms and twelve maximal Boolean blocks:

```
{a1,u1,p11} {a2,v1,p12} {u1,v1,w1}
{a3,n1,p15} {w1,m1,p14} {m1,n1,t1}
{a3,n2,p25} {w1,m2,p24} {m2,n2,t2}
{e11,e10,e01,e00}
{t1,e10,s1} {t2,e01,s2}
```

Put `q=e11+e10` and `r=e11+e01`. The pasted order has 56 elements
(zero, one, 24 atoms, 24 coatoms, and six nontrivial middle events in the
four-atom coordinate block) and exactly 224 two-valued states. Exhaustive
order calculation proves unique binary extrema and the orthomodular law.
State supports give an injective concrete representation, order separation,
and exact centre `{0,1}`. The maximal-block list is exhaustive: eleven
eight-event blocks and one sixteen-event block.

The sub-OML generated by `a1,a2,a3` is the eight-element `MO3`, the
horizontal sum of three four-element Boolean algebras. The exact projection
to `(a1,a2,a3,q,r)` contains 30 of 32 profiles: only `11101` and `11110`
are absent. Thus the activated fibre is exactly `11100,11111`, while every
off-`111` activation profile admits all four outputs. The two-point activation
support is not an event, and every nonzero event has an off-activation point.

**Evidence class:** executable verified plus exhaustive finite evidence,
independently reproduced and hostile reviewed. Main producer and receipts:
`../verification/incompatible_h4_conditional_cell_audit.py` and
`../verification/receipt_cell_incompatible_h4_conditional.json`.

The implication is implemented by two difference-killing mediator chains.
The one-mediator design fails at `join(e00,t)`; reusing `n1` fails at
`join(m1,m2)`. These are executable finite refutations, not global
minimality theorems.

## 4. Fixed-pattern full-interface amalgam

The sublogic generated by `(a1,a2,a3,q)` has ten events: the horizontal sum
of four four-element Boolean algebras, with sixteen state characters. The
224 cell states have fibre sizes

```
1,1,6,6,7,7,7,7,13,13,21,21,21,21,36,36.
```

Form the concrete fibre-product representation of two cells over this full
interface. It has 4,964 points. The raw union has 102 events and 16 unordered
mixed cuts without unique extrema. Closing inside this representation under
complement and set-disjoint union adds eight events in one round and then
stabilizes at 110 events. This **concrete orthogonal closure**—not claimed to
be a universal or representation-independent canonical completion—is a
centre-free OML with 26 maximal blocks (22 of size eight and four of size

The private outputs remain distinct. Its exact activated projection on
`(a1,a2,a3,q,r1,r2)` is `{111000,111111}`. The activation support is not an
event, no nonzero completed event lies inside it, and every nonzero event has

any two-valued state to the two original cells. These restrictions are cell
states agreeing on the shared interface, hence determine one fibre point.
Each of the eight new events is an orthogonal join of raw events, so finite
additivity uniquely forces its value. Restriction is therefore injective and
the state equals the corresponding fibre-point evaluation. **Evidence class:
hand proved**, not exhaustively enumerated by the verifier.

**Evidence classes:** structural and relation claims executable verified;

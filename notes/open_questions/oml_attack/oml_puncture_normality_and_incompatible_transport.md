# Puncture normality and incompatible conditional transport

*Campaign 13, 2026-07-14. Unconditional common-boundary sigma semantics is
closed by a state-normality trichotomy. A face-local completion remains open
when all nonnormal escape states lie outside an incompatible finite pattern.*

## 1. Algebraic common-boundary paste

Let `H=2^{omega1}`, let `A` be its cylinder sigma-algebra, and put

\[
 B_i=P(H\setminus\{i\}),\qquad j_i(E)=E\setminus\{i\}.
\]

Define the finitary algebraic OML paste as the colimit of the diagram
`A -> B_i`: take the free OML on the disjoint union of all `B_i`, quotient by
their internal block equations and by equality of all images of `A`.
Canonical maps into this colimit need not be injective; faithfulness is open.

If they are faithful, the common `A` is central in the finitary paste:
every `a in A` commutes with all block generators and its commutant is a
sub-OML containing every finitary term. **Evidence class: hand proved.**

## 2. State normality

Call an embedded Boolean sigma-algebra `B -> L` **state-normal** when the
restriction of every global sigma-additive two-valued state of `L` is
sigma-additive for the given Boolean countable joins of `B`. Preservation of
those joins by the embedding is sufficient.

### Puncture-normality no-go theorem

Let `L` be any sigma-complete OML receiving faithful copies of the cylinder
boundary and all puncture blocks. If `A` and every `B_i` are state-normal,
then

\[
 St_\sigma(L)=\varnothing.
\]

*Proof.* A global sigma-state restricts normally to `A` and hence is
evaluation at a unique `x in H`. Its restriction to the state-normal block
`B_x` would be a sigma-additive extension of evaluation at `x`, forbidden by
the omega-one/Ulam puncture theorem. **Evidence class: hand proved.**

Thus no sigma-completion retaining the literal common boundary and all local
sigma semantics can satisfy order separation. Centrality is irrelevant to
this conclusion.

## 3. Charged join-defect theorem

Assume only that `A` is state-normal and let `s` be a global sigma-state with
hub trace `eval_x`. Its restriction to `B_x` is finitely additive but cannot
be sigma-additive. Hence there is a disjoint sequence `(E_n)` in `B_x` such
that

\[
 s(e_x(E_n))=0\quad\text{for every }n,
 \qquad s\left(e_x(\bigcup_nE_n)\right)=1.
\]

Put

\[
 V=\bigvee_L e_x(E_n),\qquad U=e_x(\bigcup_nE_n).
\]

Then

\[
 V<U,\qquad s(V)=0,qquad s(U)=1,
\]

and `V` is not in the embedded `B_x`. The inequality `V<=U` follows because
`U` is an L-upper bound; equality is excluded by the state values. If `V`
were in `B_x`, Boolean completeness would make `U<=V`, forcing equality.
**Evidence class: hand proved.**

Every state-bearing completion with a normal hub therefore creates a charged
countable-join defect leaving the puncture block whose missing point is its
own hub trace.

## 4. Completion trichotomy

Every literal common-boundary completion falls into exactly one case:

1. `A` and all `B_i` are state-normal: there are no global sigma-states and
   order separation fails;
2. `A` is state-normal but some puncture block is not: global states require
   charged join defects, so unconditional puncture sigma-cell identification
   fails;
3. `A` is not state-normal: restrictions no longer classify one hub point,
   so the cylinder-hub mechanism fails.

**Evidence class: hand proved.** This closes unconditional global hub
identification. It does not close MBRC on a forbidden finite cylinder `U`:
the nonnormal states and charged join defects in cases 2--3 could all lie
outside `U`, supplying precisely the escape states required by order
separation, while no sigma-state lies in `U`.

## 5. State-separation escape theorem

Let `L` be nontrivial, sigma-complete, and order-separated by sigma-additive
two-valued states. Suppose a finite pattern `p` with prescribed literals
`l_1,...,l_n` has a finitely additive witness but no sigma witness. Then

\[
 \bigwedge_k l_k=0.
\]

Otherwise order separation supplies a sigma-state charging the nonzero meet
and therefore every literal. More strongly, for every nonzero `c in L`
there is a sigma-state charging `c` and violating at least one literal of
`p`. **Evidence class: hand proved.**

Equivalently, no nonzero event can activate the forbidden pattern, even by a
semantic implication on all sigma-states: if every state charging `c`
charges every `l_k`, order reflection gives `c<=l_k` for all `k`, hence
`c=0`.

Therefore any admissible construction must provide an off-pattern sigma
escape state charging every nonzero mixed or sigma cut. One event supported
entirely inside the forbidden finite cylinder refutes order separation.

## 6. Conditional omega-one transport blueprint

Let the hub coordinates be the horizontal sum of four-element Boolean
algebras generated by mutually incompatible events `h_alpha`,
`alpha<omega1`. Its two-valued states are all points of `2^{omega1}`, but no
common Boolean hub algebra is present. Keep private puncture blocks `B_i`
with commuting coordinate events

\[
 y_{i\alpha}=\{x\ne i:x_\alpha=1\}.
\]

Choose three incompatible activation events `a_1,a_2,a_3`. The required
cell for each `(i,alpha)` must impose directly

\[
 (a_1,a_2,a_3)=(1,1,1)\Longrightarrow
 h_\alpha=y_{i\alpha},
\]

while retaining enough off-pattern states for order separation.

The coordinates may be transported through distinct copies as displayed, or
a literal common-boundary completion may be used provided normality fails
only off the forbidden cylinder and face-local MBRC survives. Conditional on
faithful completion and full-atlas MBRC on that cylinder:

- every countable subatlas has a sigma-section, since only countably many
  coordinates are constrained for each mentioned puncture and an unused
  coordinate can be changed;
- the full activated atlas has no sigma-section, since its selected hub
  `h` forces the forbidden evaluation on `B_h`;
- an activated finitely additive section exists using the ultrafilter
  extension in `B_h`.

**Evidence class: hand proved, conditional.** Existence of the cells and
their completion is open.

## 7. AND-event obstruction

The incompatible activation pattern cannot be mediated by an event `p`.
If all separating sigma-states satisfied

\[
 \mu(p)=1\Longleftrightarrow
 \mu(a_1)=\mu(a_2)=\mu(a_3)=1,
\]

then `p<=a_1,a_2`. Since distinct horizontal-sum generators have meet zero,
`p=0`, contradicting a state with activation pattern `111` charging `p`.
**Evidence class: hand proved.**

The existing single-event-activated diagonal cell therefore cannot be
reused through an AND mediator. The conditional relation must be enforced
directly from the incompatible three-event valuation.

## 8. Exact finite gate

The next construction problem is finite and explicit:

> Find a finite order-separated OML containing an `H_4` activation control
> and events `q,r` whose sigma-state projection contains `11100` and
> `11111`, excludes `11101` and `11110`, and preferably admits every `q,r`
> profile off the `111` activation cylinder.

No activation event may be added. If such a cell exists, paste two copies
around one two-coordinate Boolean puncture control and exhaustively audit
orthogonal completion, maximal blocks, centre, state relation, and the
off-pattern escape condition for every nonzero event.

This finite cell is the load-bearing gate for the conditional omega-one
architecture. Its existence is **open**. The infinite completion, MBRC,
sigma-completeness, trivial centre, and order separation remain **open** even
if the finite gate passes.

# Two incompatible-pentagon cells pasted over a shared output

*Campaign 14 finite assembly gate, 2026-07-14.*

Take two copies of the 22-event incompatible pentagon conditional cell and
identify only the four-element Boolean subalgebra generated by the output
event `q`.  Its canonical concrete carrier is the fibre product of the two
11-point state spaces over the value of `q`; it has 61 points.  Pulling back
the two event families gives 40 events.

The raw union is already closed under complement and disjoint union.  More
strongly, exhaustive inclusion-order enumeration finds unique binary extrema,
the orthomodular law, trivial centre, and exactly ten maximal Boolean blocks,
all of size eight.  Exhaustive finite-additivity enumeration gives exactly 61
two-valued states, precisely the 61 distinct carrier-point profiles, so the
point states order-separate.

Each cell retains its exact conditional relation `111 => q=r_i`.  When both
incompatible activation triples hold, the exact joint profile on
`(q,r_left,r_right)` is `{000,111}`.  Thus the shared coordinate transports
equality transitively without creating a block containing either activation
cylinder.  Neither activation support nor their union is an event.  Every
nonzero event contains a point outside both activation cylinders, preserving
the finite every-nonzero off-pattern escape property.

This audit concerns one fixed cyclic designation in each cell. It does not
assert preservation for every relabelled activation triple, and the pentagon
does not realize all four outputs over every individual off-`111` activation
profile. The two activation triples are private: identifying the common
triple as well as `q` collapses the copies, because `(a1,a2,a3,q)` separates
all eleven local states. Thus this is a hub-only control, not the required
fixed-pattern assembly.

This is an **executable-verified, exhaustive finite theorem**, not an
arbitrary-index or sigma-completion theorem.  Certificate and receipt:
`../verification/two_pentagon_incompatible_paste.py` and
`../verification/two_pentagon_incompatible_paste_q.json`.

## Sharp negative control

Identifying both `q` and `r` instead produces a 33-point, 38-event raw family.
Orthogonal closure again adds no events, but eight ordered cross-cell pairs
have nonunique extrema (the first has two incomparable maximal lower bounds).
It is not a lattice.  Thus the successful assembly mechanism is genuinely a
single-coordinate, nonmaximal-boundary paste; simultaneous identification of
the two incompatible outputs is not licensed without additional mixed-cut
repair.  Evidence class: executable verified / exhaustive finite refutation.

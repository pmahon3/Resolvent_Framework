# Finite Typed Graph σ-Class Normal Form and OML Dichotomy

*Campaign continuation, 2026-07-13. All theorems below are hand proved unless
an executable evidence class is explicitly stated. Section 8 closes the exact
upper normal form and passes Gate N; latticehood remains a separate open gate.*

## 1. Countable-generation support

**Lemma 1 (countable dependence).** If `Dyn(S)` is the smallest concrete
σ-class containing a family `S`, every `E∈Dyn(S)` belongs to `Dyn(S_0)` for
some countable `S_0⊆S`.

*Proof.* The union of `Dyn(S_0)` over countable `S_0⊆S` contains `S`, is
closed under complement, and is closed under countable disjoint unions:
take the union of the countably many countable sets of generators used by
the summands. Minimality gives the claim. ∎

For the rotating candidate, all column pieces and resolution singletons
used in constructing one event therefore lie over a countable set of
columns. Outside that set only the full cylinders and the six global forms
`Σ_i,G_ij` matter.

## 2. Fibre trace morphisms

Identify a type-`k` fibre with the six cells
`U={g,h,W,V_i,V_j,R}`. Restriction to a fixed fibre commutes with
complement and with countable disjoint union. Hence the trace of every
generated event lies in the local Dynkin class `D_k` generated by the three
`r_i` traces and `g,h`. The certified local computation gives `|D_k|=24`,
with no nonempty member below `W` and with `W∉D_k`.

**Corollary 2.1 (global W exclusion).** A generated event contained in the
union of all unresolved `W(α)` regions is empty.

**Corollary 2.2 (graph intersections excluded).** No set-theoretic
intersection `Σ_i∩Σ_j` is generated: on the unresolved third type it has
trace `W`, which is not in `D_k`.

These are arbitrary-base conclusions. The 24-event computation is used only
to certify the finite algebra `D_k`; the passage to every fibre is the hand
proof above.

## 3. Same-type decorated-form relation

For two columns of the same type put

`R_k = {(d,d):d∈D_k} ∪ {(0,U),(U,0)}`.

This relation contains every pair of traces of a global primitive and every
pair of traces of a cylinder. It is closed under componentwise complement.
It is also closed under componentwise disjoint union: two diagonal pairs
remain diagonal; a corner and a nontrivial diagonal cannot be disjoint; and
the only union of two distinct nonzero corners is `(U,U)`.

**Theorem 3 (same-type pair invariant).** For every generated event `E`
there is a countable set `C_E` such that, whenever `α,β` have the same type
and lie outside `C_E`, `(E_α,E_β)∈R_k`.

*Proof.* Global primitives and cylinders have empty exceptional set. A local
generator uses its one column. Complements preserve the exceptional set.
For a countable disjoint union use the union of the exceptional sets and
closure of `R_k`. ∎

Equivalently, on each type and off a countable set, exactly one of the
following holds:

1. **binary mode:** fibres are arbitrarily `0` or `U`;
2. **proper mode:** every fibre is one fixed `d∈D_k\{0,U}`.

The modes are unique except for the constant-empty and constant-full
profiles, which are assigned to binary mode.

## 4. Exact upper closure rules

Complement sends binary support `A` to its complement and proper trace `d`
to `U\d`.

For a countable pairwise-disjoint family, discard the union of its countable
exceptional sets. On a fixed type:

- if every term is binary, their supports are pairwise disjoint and the
  union has binary support equal to their union;
- if one term has a nonempty proper trace, every binary term has empty
  support there; proper traces are pairwise disjoint, and at most six are
  nonempty; their union is proper mode unless it is `U`, in which case it is
  constant-full binary mode.

Thus the upper grammar is closed under complement and countable disjoint
union and contains every generator. It handles cylinder decoration,
countable modification, subtraction of comparable forms, and residualization.
Intersection is not an operation of the calculus unless it is independently
known to be an event.

**Corollary 4.1 (split collector restrictions excluded).** If `A` and its
complement both meet one support type of `G_ij` uncountably, then
`G_ij∩cyl(A)` is not generated: outside every countable exception it realizes
both `(q,0)` on two same-type columns, which is not in `R_k`.

## 5. Finite transversal algebra

For one generic column of each of the three types, close the primitive trace
triples and all eight cylinder corners under complement and disjoint union.
The resulting algebra has 88 profiles. This finite algebra controls the
coupling of the three type modes outside countably many exceptional columns;
it is not merely a fixed-column trace.

**Theorem 5 (transversal profile invariant).** For every generated `E` there
is a countable `C_E⊂I` such that

`(E_α,E_β,E_γ)∈Q`

for every `α∈T_1\C_E`, `β∈T_2\C_E`, and `γ∈T_3\C_E`.

*Proof.* The claim holds for global forms and cylinders with no exception,
and for a local generator after excluding its named column. Complements use
complement closure of `Q`. For a countable disjoint family exclude the union
of the exceptional sets. The resulting profile triples are coordinatewise
disjoint. At most eighteen are nonempty, since each nonempty triple occupies
one of the eighteen coordinate-cells and disjoint triples occupy distinct
cells. Their union is therefore a finite iterated permitted union in `Q`. ∎

The singleton-valued profiles in `Q` are exactly zero and the three intended
collector profiles. In particular, deleting one whole support type from a
collector gives a profile absent from `Q`. Together with Theorem 3 this
excludes every collector restriction having genuinely uncountable variation
modulo countable support: a restriction which splits a support type into two
uncountable parts is excluded by `R_k`; modulo countable sets, a
restriction which is constant on each type but deletes a support type is
excluded by `Q`. It also excludes unanticipated singleton/diagonal collector
profiles. These finite membership claims are executable verified. Proper
co-countable restrictions are generated by deleting countably many resolution
points and are intentionally not excluded.

The executable census additionally checks repeated balanced type words of
length 3, 6, 9, and 12. It is exhaustive for each stated finite quotient,
and in every quotient omits proper collector restrictions and nonzero
W-supported events. These computations support, but do not replace, the
arbitrary-base pair invariant.

## 6. Former exact-normal-form gap

Let `K` be the finite complement/disjoint-union closure of the global forms
and cylinder profiles. The natural exact candidate is:

> every generated event is a countable-column modification of an element of
> `K`.

Every such modification is generatable once its core is known: each desired
column trace lies in `D_k`, hence is generated on that column, and countably
many old sections can be subtracted before adding the replacements.

The unresolved direction is closure under a countable disjoint union of
modified cores. Outside the union of the exceptional columns the upper
grammar collapses the sequence fibrewise to finitely many proper-mode terms
and a binary tail. What is not yet proved is that the resulting coupled
three-type profile is represented by one element of `K`. Removing the
countably supported overlaps is legitimate in the generated class, but does
not by itself produce a finite-core identity. This is the exact
**finite-core splicing lemma** still owed.

This gap is closed in §8. The paragraph is retained to identify exactly what
the anchor argument had to repair.

One Gate-L sublemma is already available: `G_ij` is the greatest generated
lower bound of `Σ_i,Σ_j`. Indeed any lower bound is set-theoretically inside
their intersection; its unresolved-type sections lie below `W` and hence are
empty, while every resolved-type section lies below the appropriate
singleton. Thus it is contained in `G_ij`. This does not prove general
latticehood and does not open Gate L.

## 7. Scoped graph-network theorem

The proofs above do not use the special cardinal `ω₁`.

**Theorem 7 (finite typed pair-polarity theorem).** Let a concrete σ-class
be generated over a full cylinder algebra by finitely many global forms
whose fibre traces depend only on a finite type, together with local
generators each supported on one column. If `D_t` is the local trace Dynkin
class, then every generated event has only countably many exceptional local
columns and its trace on every same-type pair outside them lies in the
Dynkin relation generated by the diagonal `D_t` and the four cylinder
corners. In particular, when that relation is diagonal plus the two
off-diagonal corners, no proper trace can be restricted to a subset which
splits a type into two uncountable parts.

This is a closure theorem, not a state-selection theorem and not the broad
graph-network dichotomy target.

## 8. Exact parameterized cores and finite-core splicing

Let `K` mean the following parameterized family, not a finite set. Start with
the six type-uniform global forms and the eight unions of whole type
cylinders; apply a finite expression using complement and disjoint union; and
finally, wherever the resulting type-uniform profile is empty on a type,
disjointly add an arbitrary cylinder supported on that type. Its semantic
interpretation is the corresponding subset of the full carrier. A finite
profile expression in `Q` lifts verbatim to a type-uniform global expression:
profile-disjointness says that the lifted events are disjoint on every
column, so every union in the expression remains a legal σ-class operation.

The exact census of `Q` gives 88 profiles. Each coordinate projection has 24
traces and each two-coordinate projection has 78 pairs. With `0,U` called
binary and all other traces proper, the mode distribution is:

| number of binary coordinates | profiles |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 44 |
| 1 | 36 |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 8 |

There are 18 nonempty one-binary fixed-proper strata, each with both binary
values, and the all-binary stratum is the full cube `{0,U}³`. Hence every
nonempty fixed-proper stratum is rectangular. The incidence automorphism
group has six elements (`S₃`). Among the nine named generators there are
exactly three inclusion-minimal generating sets, all of size eight: the six
global forms and any two of the three type cylinders. These are exhaustive
finite computations with a stable JSON certificate and a separately
recomputing verifier in
`../verification/census_2026-07-13_gate_n_q/`.

**Theorem 8 (anchor synthesis / exact upper normal form).** Every generated
event is a countable-column modification of an element of `K`. Conversely,
every such modification is generated. More generally, the conclusion holds
for every event-description satisfying Theorems 3 and 5 and whose exceptional
sections belong to their local trace classes.

*Proof.* Give an event `E` one common countable exceptional set `C` for
Theorems 3 and 5. On each `T_k\C`, Theorem 3 says either that all sections are
one fixed proper trace, or that they are binary. In a genuinely varying
binary type choose a column on which the section is `0`; in a constant binary
type choose any column; and in a proper type choose any column. The three
chosen columns exist because every `T_k` is uncountable and `C` is countable.
Theorem 5 puts their profile `q` in `Q`.

Lift a finite `Q`-expression for `q` to a type-uniform core `K_q`. On every
genuinely varying binary type its trace is zero. Let `A` be the union, over
those types, of the columns where `E` has full trace. Then `cyl(A)` is
disjoint from `K_q`, and
`K'=K_q ⊍ cyl(A)` agrees with `E` outside `C`. Constant-empty, constant-full,
and proper types already agree with their anchor values.

For each `α∈C`, both the old section `K'∩col_α` and desired section
`E∩col_α` lie in the certified local class `D_{type(α)}`, hence have supported
local realizations. Their countable disjoint unions `S` and `T` are generated.
Since `S⊆K'`, subtraction is the legal identity
`K'\S=(K'^c⊍S)^c`; then `E=(K'\S)⊍T`. This proves synthesis. Conversely the
same displayed operations generate any countable modification. ∎

**Corollary 8.1 (countable disjoint-family theorem).** A countable disjoint
union of core modifications is again a core modification. Fibrewise, at most
six nonempty proper traces occur on any type, while all remaining terms are
binary; Theorem 8 synthesizes their union and supplies the required
cross-type synchronization. Pairwise or projected admissibility is not used:
the universal transversal quantifier in Theorem 5 supplies the anchor.

**Gate-N verdict.** Gate N passes. The theorem handles arbitrary cylinder
parameters, complement, countable disjoint union, and all local generators;
the earlier arbitrary-base invariants exclude the indispensable forbidden
forms. Two fresh hostile audits checked expression lifting, the nonempty-type
quantifier, and countable patching without finding a material gap.

As finite corroboration, exhaustive saturation tests for exact core closures
with 3, 6, and 9 columns checked respectively 735, 103,565, and 26,770,371

is essential. Certificate and verifier:
`../verification/census_2026-07-13_gate_n_splicing/`. This finite census is
not the arbitrary-base proof.

## 9. Finite-profile interval obstruction and Gate L

**Theorem 9 (profile-interval obstruction).** Let a concrete σ-class have a
finite type set and a relation `Q` such that every event, off a countable
exception, has every type-transversal profile in `Q`. Suppose generated
events `A,B` have generated upper bounds `U,V`. If no `q∈Q` lies
coordinatewise between the sectionwise set union profile of `A,B` and the
sectionwise intersection profile of `U,V`, then `A,B` have no join. The latter
intersection is used only as a set-theoretic order bound; it is not asserted
to be a generated event or a meet.

*Proof.* A join `J`, if present, satisfies `A∪B⊆J⊆U∩V` as subsets of the
concrete carrier. Remove the countable exceptional columns for `J` and choose
one remaining representative of every uncountable type. Its transversal
profile lies in `Q` and in the forbidden coordinatewise interval. ∎

This theorem is an arbitrary-base closure/lattice dichotomy: a missing finite
profile interval is a certificate that completion of the displayed generated
σ-class is not a lattice. It does not claim that interval saturation is
sufficient for latticehood.

**Corollary 9.1 (finite cut saturation is necessary).** In the scoped
finite-typed architecture, assume every `q∈Q` has a type-uniform event
realization and each type survives the exceptional ideal. If the event poset
is a lattice, then for every finite nonempty `A,B⊆Q` with `a≤b` for all
`a∈A,b∈B`,

`Q ∩ [⋁A, ⋀B] ≠ ∅`,

where the displayed bounds are ambient coordinatewise union and
intersection. Since `Q` is finite and has bottom and top, this Dedekind--
MacNeille cut saturation is equivalent to `Q` being a lattice under its
inherited coordinatewise order: use all common upper bounds (respectively
lower bounds) to obtain the inherited join (respectively meet). This is a
necessary finite CSP condition, not a sufficient arbitrary-base lattice
theorem.

Cut saturation implies neither rectangularity nor T4. The diagonal relation
`{(0,0),(1,1)}` is a cut-saturated complemented two-element lattice but is
nonrectangular; and `Q` contains event-profile data but no state-space or
principal-atom data from which T4 could follow.

**Theorem 10 (rotating candidate has no join).** Put

`A=cyl(T₁)`, `B=G₁₂`, and `U=cyl(T₁∪T₂)`. A second generated upper bound is

`V=(G₂₃⊍Σ₁)^c ⊍ (G₁₃⊍G₁₂)`.

Both displayed unions are disjoint. On representative types their profiles
are

`A=(U,0,0)`, `B=({g},{g},0)`,
`U=(U,U,0)`, and `V=(U,{g,V_j,R},{g,V_j,R})`.

Thus a join profile would have to lie between
`(U,{g},0)` and `(U,{g,V_j,R},0)`. The exact 88-profile certificate contains
no profile in this interval. Theorem 9 proves that `A,B` have no join.
Therefore **Gate L fails** and the rotating σ-class is not an OML. Gates M,
C, Z, F, and Φ are not entered.

Exhaustive finite core quotients find, respectively, 18, 54, and 126 join
failures (and the same numbers of meet failures) among 3,916, 16,290, and
245,350 unordered pairs for 3, 6, and 9 columns. The independently rebuilding
verifier in `../verification/census_2026-07-13_gate_l/` confirms the minimal
three-type interval witness. The aggregate longer-word counts are producer
results rather than independently re-enumerated by that verifier. These
counts are discovery/corroboration; the
arbitrary-base proof is Theorem 9.

Finally, if any concrete complement/disjoint-union logic is a lattice, it is
automatically orthomodular. For `A⊆B`, the event
`B\A=(A⊍B^c)^c` equals `A^c∧B`, and its disjoint union with `A` is `B`.
No σ-closure hypothesis is needed for this auxiliary fact.

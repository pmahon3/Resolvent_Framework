## The exact target, restated cleanly

The reduction's object = **a σ-additive state on a concrete σ-complete OML that is
FA-extendable but NOT σ-extendable** to a global hidden-variable joint. Equivalently:

- the programme's "non-contextual" = `w` in the **closed convex hull of dispersion-free
  states** = a **countably-additive (σ-additive)** mixture of two-valued states = a
  σ-additive global joint. (Confirmed from `notes/archive/reading1_prize_reduction_superseded.md` lines 68–74:
  the ∏ₙMO₂ analysis treats a *finitely-additive* diffuse state as contextual and a
  *σ-additive* state as forced into the hull. So the hull is the σ-mixture hull.)
- "contextual but finite-restrictions-classical" (clause b) = **a finitely-additive
  global joint exists** (every finite sub-OML has a local joint; these glue to an FA
  global joint by Horn–Tarski/Rao–Rao).

So **σ-essential contextual state = Yosida–Hewitt / Maharam gap object**: an FA global
joint exists, but no σ-additive one. That is the entire content of the cell.

## The killer paper — verbatim, verified from the PDF

**B. Derr & Williamson, "Systems of Precision: Coherent Probabilities
on Pre-Dynkin-Systems and Coherent Previsions on Linear Subspaces," arXiv:2302.03522
(2023).** Pre-Dynkin-systems = concrete OMPs (set-representable); Dynkin-systems = the
σ-complete concrete ones; σ-blocks = the maximal Boolean sub-σ-algebras. The two
load-bearing theorems (read verbatim from the arXiv PDF, not an abstract):

- **Theorem 4.5 (Horn–Tarski / Rao–Rao 3.2.10).** A finitely additive probability `µ`
  on a pre-Dynkin-system `D` is **extendable to `2^Ω`** iff
  `Σχ_{B_k} − Σχ_{A_j} ≥ 0 ∀ω ⟹ Σµ(B_k) − Σµ(A_j) ≥ 0` for **all finite families**
  of sets in `D`. Extension to `2^Ω` = an FA global joint = an FA mixture of
  dispersion-free states. **RHS = clause (b).** (The finite-family condition is exactly
  "non-contextual on every finite sub-OML": a finite gamble family touches only finitely
  many `µ`-values, so it is a finite/local condition.)

- **Theorem D.6 (σ-Extendability).** Let `Fσ` be the Borel σ-algebra on a **Polish**
  space `Ω`, `Dσ ⊆ Fσ` a Dynkin-system with **`σ(Dσ)=Fσ`**, and assume each σ-block
  restriction `µ_i := µσ|A_i` is **inner regular**. Then `µσ` is **σ-extendable**
  (extends to a countably-additive measure on `Fσ`) **iff** `Σ∫f_j dµ_{i_j} ≥ 0`
  whenever `Σf_j ≥ 0`, over all finite families of simple gambles. (Proof: Prop. D.4
  splits the Dynkin space into Kolmogorov spaces ⟹ Maharam 1972 Thm 8.1 ⟹ measure on
  the generated algebra ⟹ Carathéodory ⟹ measure on `Fσ`.)

**The boxing fact** is D.6-over-4.5: the D.6 RHS *is* the 4.5 RHS (gamble form = set
form linearized). So under (Polish, `σ(Dσ)=Borel`, inner-regular blocks):

> **FA-extendable (clause b) ⟹ σ-extendable.** The Yosida–Hewitt gap **closes**.
> Hence clause (b) ⟹ σ-additive global joint ⟹ **non-contextual**. The σ-essential
> cell is **empty** wherever D.6 applies.

## The surviving crack — a descriptive-set-theory question (the new frontier)

D.6 is **representation-dependent**. To wield it as a kill on a given concrete
σ-complete OML `L`, you must represent `L` as a Dynkin-system on a **Polish** `Ω`
with `σ(Dσ)=Borel` and inner-regular blocks. The canonical representation is
`Ω = S_df^σ(L)` — the σ-additive dispersion-free states (the separating set
concreteness supplies), weak-\* topology. In that representation D.6's "σ-extendable"
= the intrinsic "non-contextual." So the entire residual open question is:

> **Is `S_df^σ(L)` (weak-\* topology) Polish / standard-Borel, with `L` generating
> its Borel σ-algebra (`σ(Dσ)=Fσ`)?**

- If **always yes** (every concrete σ-complete OML admits such a rep) → D.6 always
  applies → the cell is **unconditionally empty** → SOLVED-NEGATIVE, problem dead.
- If **not always** → a witness can live **only** where this fails: `S_df^σ` is
  **analytic-but-not-Borel / non-Polish**. σ-additive states are generally NOT
  weak-\* closed, so this is genuinely plausible — and is the *only* crack.
  Inner-regularity is **secondary**: on a Polish `Ω`, Ulam's theorem makes Borel
  probability measures Radon, so footnote-29 inner-regularity is near-automatic; the
  **binding** hypotheses are **Polish + Borel-generation**.

This is the exact rhyme with Strategy D / Gaifman: there the residue was
ZFC-independence; here it is **descriptive set theory** (Polish vs. analytic). The
"off-center / non-band / pasting" constraints from Sessions 1–8 are *subsumed and
sharpened* by this single representability question.

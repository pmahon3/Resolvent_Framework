# Meagre route vs. measure route on the OML dual — do they split?

Investigation gate for the suspicion that the Boolean coincidence
"meagre defect ⊆ μ̂-null" (Rao–Rao) FAILS on the OML case in a way that
might supply the missing σ-OML engine. Verdict: **(A) obstruction restated,
not a new engine** — but the restatement is the sharpest diagnosis of the
wall so far.

**Sources.** McDonald–Bimbó 2023
(`literature_review/literature/mcdonald_bimbo_2023.pdf`, Def 3.4, Lemma 3.5,
Cor 3.7, Thm 3.9 — read directly). Paper I prop B2 / Rao–Rao
(`papers/paper_i/probability_from_observation_body.tex`). The defect set is
from [[mb_primeness_check]].

## The Boolean coincidence being tested

Paper I (prop B2) proves "σ-additive ⟺ μ̂ concentrates on realised points"
via Rao–Rao (Theory of Charges, Thm 10.5.3): σ-additivity ⟺ μ̂ vanishes on
**meagre Baire sets** ⟺ μ̂ gives full mass to ultrafilters closed under
countable intersection. So in the Boolean case σ-additivity IS the
absorption **"meagre defect ⊆ μ̂-null"** — the category notion (meagre) and
the measure notion (null) are bridged. The join-defect `[⋂Eₙ]` is nowhere
dense, hence meagre, hence (under σ-additivity) null.

## The OML test object

Defect set `D = h(⋁ₙ aₙ) ∖ ⋃ₙ h(aₙ)`, nonempty because the OML join is the
biorthogonal closure `(⋃ₙ h(aₙ))^⊥⊥ ⊋ ⋃ₙ h(aₙ)`. Witness on `L(H)`:
`aₙ = span(eₙ)`, `p = span(v)`, `v` of infinite support; then `↑p ∈ D ∩ P(A)`
(a *realised* point in the defect).

## What was proved

1. **`S₀(A)` is a Stone space** (compact + totally disconnected, Cor 3.7),
   hence a Baire space; topology generated by the clopen subbasis
   `{h(a)} ∪ {F(A)∖h(a)}` (Def 3.4). MB itself contains NO measure / Baire-
   category / σ content — the duality is purely finitary. The meagre and
   measure layers are the programme's, placed on top of MB. (Attribution:
   keep this explicit.)

2. **`↑p` is a topologically ISOLATED (clopen) point** (load-bearing, proved
   from MB defs). For `p` an atom, the only filters containing `p` are `↑p`
   and the improper `ω` (meet-closure + `p` atom ⟹ every `c ∈ y` has
   `c ≥ p`), so `h(p) = {↑p, ω}`; and `{↑p} = h(p) ∩ (F(A)∖h(p^⊥))` is a
   finite intersection of subbasic clopens, hence clopen.

3. **Therefore `D` is NON-MEAGRE** (it contains the nonempty open `{↑p}`; no
   nonempty open set is meagre in a Baire space). This is the EXACT REVERSE
   of the Boolean case, where the join-defect is nowhere dense. The union
   `⋃ₙ h(aₙ)` is not even dense in `h(⋁ₙ aₙ)` — it misses an isolated point.

4. **The category quotient does not survive.** `CO(S₀(A))` mod meagre has
   `[h(⋁aₙ)] = 1` but `[⋃h(aₙ)] = 1 − [D] < 1` (since `[D] ≠ 0`): the σ-join
   is NOT recovered. The meagre route chokes on the SAME `D` as the measure
   route. Worse, `CO(S₀(A))` is Boolean, so `CO/meagre` is the regular-open /
   Cohen algebra — non-distributivity is COLLAPSED. And `CO(S₀(A))^†`
   (⊥-stable clopens, the OML) is not union-closed, so "⊥-stable-clopens mod
   meagre" is not even closed under the operations.

5. **The category route = MacNeille completion (Harding-blocked, route ii).**
   "Clopens of a Stone space mod meagre" = regular-open algebra = Dedekind–
   MacNeille completion of `CO(X)`. Completing `CO(X)^†` this way is the
   MacNeille completion, which Harding (1991) showed need not be
   orthomodular. So the meagre route is NOT distinct from the three known
   blocked routes — it is route (ii) in topological clothing.

## The sharp diagnosis (the genuinely valuable output)

`↑p` is **closed under countable meets** (`bₙ ≥ p ∀n ⟹ ⋀bₙ ≥ p`) yet lies
in `D` because it is **not σ-prime for countable joins** (`↑p ∋ ⋁aₙ` but
`↑p ∌` any `aₙ`). In the Boolean case "countably-meet-closed" and "off the
join-defect" COINCIDE — that coincidence is exactly what Rao–Rao bridges.
**In the OML case they SPLIT, and `↑p` falls in the gap.** Both the meagre
and the measure routes are downstream of this single fact — the
mb_primeness failure seen topologically. So the user's meagre-vs-measure
suspicion is CORRECT in its precise form (the two notions do come apart),
but the split is the same `^⊥⊥` wall in new language, not a second route.

## Honest residue (open, outside the resolved fork)

The fork was about the *meagre* ideal specifically; the meagre ideal is
shown to fail. NOT proved: that NO ideal works. The precise open sub-
question —

> Is there an ideal `I ⊆ CO(S₀(A))` with `CO(S₀(A))/I` a σ-complete OML
> isomorphic to the σ-completion of `A`?

— is **strongly suggested impossible but not proved**. The tension: any
defect-killing ideal must kill `D ⊇ {↑p}`, but `↑p ∈ P(A)` is the
realisation datum the programme exists to represent. So a defect-killing
ideal destroys realisation. Making that "destroys realisation ⟹ no
representation" implication precise is the live sub-problem. (This is a
sharper form of the descent open problem; see
[[oml_extension_problem_superseded]]'s successor, the survey
`oml_onboarding`, §5–6, and `papers/paper_ii/two_grades_of_pr.md`.)

## Caveats

- Isolated-point result proved for `p` an atom (a line); the `L(H)` witness
  uses an atom, so the example is covered. One isolated point already forces
  `D` non-meagre, so the verdict is atom-sufficient.
- MB silence on measure/category re-confirmed by full read; the topological
  computation is ours (from MB's definitions), the meagre framing is the
  programme's.

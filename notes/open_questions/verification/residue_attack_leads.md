## LEAD 1 — "line 1 collapses into line 3" (Riesz reduction) — ⚠ FLAWED AS STATED

**Claim (external):** Let `X = S_df^σ(L)` weak-*, `e_a(ω)=ω(a)`. (i) Borel σ-alg of
`X` is generated by the `e_a^{-1}(1)`; (ii) any countably-additive representing
measure `ν` with `w(a)=∫ e_a dν` can be replaced by a *regular* `ν_r` (Riesz on
compact Hausdorff `X`). Upshot offered: regularity/Borel-generation are free once a
representing measure exists, so hypothesis-weakening (line 1) is not independent of
the existence question (line 3) — "the bottleneck is existence of the representing
measure, not its regularity."

**⚠ THE FLAW (advisor-caught, confirmed against settled facts):** the reduction applies
Riesz to `S_df^σ` *as if compact*. But **`S_df^σ` is NOT weak-* closed** — a weak-*
limit of σ-additive dispersion-free states can be merely finitely additive (the
CE/ultrafilter phenomenon). Settled-facts spine: "**S_df is COMPACT (closed in
{0,1}^L); the content is whether μ is SUPPORTED on S_df^σ (the non-compact genuine
points), NOT whether a σ-measure exists**" + the "two-σ confusion" fence (#15). Honest
Riesz gives a regular measure on the *compact* `S_df` (which includes the FAKES);
whether it concentrates on `S_df^σ` IS the open question. The phrase "barycentre on the
compact Hausdorff space of σ-additive dispersion-free states" is internally
contradictory (compact ⟹ includes fakes; σ-additive ⟹ not compact).

**The SALVAGEABLE kernel (the part that survives the flaw):** the *qualitative* point
— that the difficulty is **existence/support of the representing measure on the genuine
points**, not the topological KIND of representability — is correct and is exactly what
the programme already holds (boundary is `cl(S_df^σ)`, not "the fakes"; regularity-type
hypotheses are not the frontier). So Lead 1 RE-DERIVES the known framing via a flawed
argument; it does not sharpen it. Do NOT use the reduction; the "non-Polish-representable"
survey framing stands (s13: now two-condition — OR coarse-riding; verdict Addendum s13).

## LEAD 2 — descriptive-tameness lever for the impossibility direction (UNVERIFIED CITES)

**Claim (external):** Choquet representation extends past the metrizable case
(Bishop–de Leeuw), BUT if the extreme-point set of a compact convex set is Baire or
𝒵-Souslin, the set is metrizable (attributed to **MacGibbon**). Consequently a
σ-essential witness's hidden-variable compactum must be **descriptively wild** — beyond
the tame classes that force countable-type measure behaviour. So "non-Polish is
NECESSARY but nowhere near SUFFICIENT": a witness must be genuinely exotic, not merely
non-metrizable.

**Status:** genuinely interesting and, if the MacGibbon metrizability statement holds as
described, a real strengthening of the Exit-B lean (it would mean the witness's state
space must dodge several tameness classes, not just Polishness). **NOT verified** —
Bishop–de Leeuw and especially the MacGibbon "Baire/𝒵-Souslin extreme set ⟹ metrizable"
claim need primary-source confirmation before any use (Prompt-1 miscitation risk).
Sources the report cited: Bishop–de Leeuw (numdam aif.95); a metrizability-via-extreme-
points criterion (ScienceDirect S0022123672900602).

## Operational reframing of "off-center" (from a construction push, 2026-06-21)
Pushing the "experimental/infinitary-overflow" picture into two concrete ⟦HAND⟧
construction attempts — (1) a chain of 2-atom blocks sharing atoms ("ladder"); (2) an
infinite odd-path closed at infinity via even/odd σ-joins p=⋁a_{2n}, q=⋁a_{2n+1} —
BOTH DIED THE SAME DEATH, and the death is instructive (not an obstruction; see below).

- (1) dies: a chain/tree of Boolean blocks has no odd loop ⟹ no overflow, finite or
  infinite (Greechie loop lemma). Pure-path = non-contextual everywhere.
- (2) dies: the σ-leak family was placed CENTRALLY. A mutually orthogonal family
  generates a Boolean subalgebra (orthogonal ⟹ compatible ⟹ Boolean-among-themselves —
  TRUE), but that does NOT force it central (central = commutes with ALL of L, strictly
  stronger; orthogonal projections in L(H) can sum to a non-commuting-with-a-third
  element — so "Boolean-among-themselves ⇏ central"). Attempt 2 routed the leak through
  a center by CHOICE, reproducing the ∏ₙMO₂ failure.

**NOT an obstruction — the open question in disguise.** The tempting conclusion
("σ-additivity only sees orthogonal=Boolean=distributive joins, non-distributivity is
finite/local, so σ-essential is structurally impossible") is CIRCULAR: "is the
σ-carrying orthogonal family forced central?" IS the open problem verbatim. Do not
record as evidence for Exit B beyond the standing inclination.

**= S8 band closure, re-derived from the construction side.** Both attempts are BAND
pastings (σ-structure built from orthogonal/compatible = ∪-closed families); S8 already
closed band pastings (∪-closed⟹Boolean / not⟹not-a-lattice). The push re-derived WHY
the frontier sits where it does; the NON-band concrete σ-complete OML (HW Problem 2,
bite 3) — the named frontier — is exactly what neither attempt reached. Frontier
UNMOVED; verdict UNMOVED (Exit-B lean intact, still open).

**The real gain — sharper "off-center":** the orthogonal family carrying the σ-leak
must generate a **non-central** Boolean subalgebra (not merely "irreducible /
non-central-infinite"). More precise and more usable; the two dead attempts are clean
worked examples of the central-collapse failure mode. Still no non-band idea on the
table (us, the four prompts, the literature) — that remains the missing ingredient.

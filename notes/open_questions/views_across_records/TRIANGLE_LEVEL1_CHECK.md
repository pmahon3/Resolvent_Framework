## Task 2. Prior art

Searched: the shelf (`SHELF_INDEX.json`, taxonomy `owned_regions`); full text of Navara–Pták 1983, Navara 1984 and
Dravecký–Šipoš 1980; four web searches. Not searched: "determining sets".

**(a) Level-1 rigidity: NOT FOUND (UNCLEAR, because the bodies of the two books are unread).**
- **Nearest:** Navara–Pták 1983 Thm 1 and Navara 1984 Prop. + Lemmas 1–3.
  - What they prove: a two-valued measure on the σ-class generated by A_f ∪ A_g ∪ A_{f+g} is concentrated ⟺
    certain three-term sets K(a,b) belong to the class.
  - Same method family: one special set certifies a disagreement.
  - Different setting: three one-variable σ-algebras rather than pair windows, σ-additive measures, and
    concentration rather than uniqueness for the same family.
- **Principal reduction:** owned/folklore. N–P 1983 (proof of Thm 1) and Navara 1984 p. 331 both state that
  "σ-additive measures on σ-classes extend uniquely from generators".
- **Uniqueness fails for finitely additive states in general.** P(ℕ) is the Dynkin closure of the singletons, yet
  every nonprincipal ultrafilter vanishes on all of them. So level-1 rigidity is triangle geometry, not a general
  principle.
- **Dravecký–Šipoš 1980** (Ex 6, already an owned region): A_f ∪ A_g ∪ A_{f+g} is itself a σ-class. It is about
  Gudder additivity and says nothing on rigidity.

**(b) L1′: neither known nor refuted. On ℕ³ it IS the principal gate, not a shortcut.**
1. **True on the generated algebra.** This is folklore: a product of countably complete ultrafilters; take Aᵢ′ = the
   union of the null Aᵢⁿ. So any failure needs a D₃ set outside the algebra, and s must have no FIP extension.
2. **Countable Xᵢ.** σ-additive window marginals are principal and meet in a point a. Hence L1′ ⟺ s = δ_a for every
   principal family ⟺ the principal sub-gate. A counterexample is exactly (A), which is open. The attempt's label
   "shortcut" overstates what L1′ offers.
3. **N–P 1983 Example fences L1′ but does not refute it.**
   - It lives on Q = ℚ²∩(0,1)² with A_f, A_g, A_{f+g}.
   - The measure m is σ-additive, two-valued and non-concentrated, which is possible because the fibres satisfy
     B∩C∩D = ∅. So "σ-additive on three σ-algebras ⇒ point" is owned as FALSE.
   - But m is σ-additive on the join, so it is not a failure of L1′.
   - Pair windows cannot copy it: their consistent marginals meet in a point.
4. **L1′ for arbitrary triples: undetermined.**
   - Natural arena: Gudder's f, g, f+g on ℤ×ℕ₀ (Navara 1984 Example).
   - Even a failure there would not settle the triangle.
   - The real-valued common-extension literature (Basile–Bhaskara Rao–Shortt 1994) is about existence, not
     σ-additivity.

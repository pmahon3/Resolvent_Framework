# Seed

We study Hadamard matrices of order n whose entries are l-th roots of unity. A main problem is to determine the allowed pairs (n,l). Write l = p_1^{a_1}...p_s^{a_s}. We discuss the necessary condition that n lies in p_1 N + ... + p_s N. For s=1 the statement is a classical fact. For s=2 and s=3 we prove it in this paper. For s>=4 we state it as a conjecture. We also comment on other known obstructions to existence.

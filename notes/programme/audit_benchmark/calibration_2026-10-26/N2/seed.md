# Seed

We propose a generalisation of the Cameron-Erdos conjecture for sum-free sets to linear equations over the integers in three or more variables that are not translation invariant. Using graph-theoretic methods on almost-regular graphs, we prove a weak form of the conjecture for a class of such equations. The extremal solution-free sets for this class were already known in size. Our argument bounds the number of independent sets of the associated graphs.

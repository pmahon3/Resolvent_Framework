# Seed

Given a family of convex objects in R^d whose maximum independent set (pairwise disjoint subfamily) has size at most m, how many axis-parallel hyperplanes are needed to meet every member? We show this number T_d(m) exists and depends only on m and d. We give a first super-exponential bound, then a polynomial bound in m for fixed d. In the plane we improve the best known exponential bound to O(m^2).

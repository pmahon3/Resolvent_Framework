# Seed

We generalize the wedge product of association schemes to table algebras. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for a table algebra to be the wedge product of two table algebras. When the duals of two commutative table algebras are table algebras, we show the dual of their wedge product is a table algebra isomorphic to the wedge product of the duals in reverse order. We close with applications to association schemes.

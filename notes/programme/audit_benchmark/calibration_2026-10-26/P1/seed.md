# Seed

We show that the diagonal Ramsey number R(k) satisfies R(k) <= (4 - eps)^k for some absolute constant eps > 0 and all large k. The Erdos-Szekeres bound of 1935 has stood as the exponential rate since then. We obtain the improvement via a book algorithm, a density-increment-like procedure that finds large monochromatic books in a two-coloured complete graph. A careful analysis of the algorithm's steps bounds the number of 'bad' steps.

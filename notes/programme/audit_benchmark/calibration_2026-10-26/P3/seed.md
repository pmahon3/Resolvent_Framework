# Seed

The bunkbed conjecture of Kasteleyn (1985) asserts that in a graph G times K_2 with independent edge percolation, a vertex u in the lower copy is at least as likely to connect to v in the upper copy as to the lower copy of v. We give a counterexample on a planar graph with 7222 vertices. The construction converts a small hypergraph counterexample into an ordinary graph through a gadget, and the probability gap is computed exactly. The gap is about 10^-6509.

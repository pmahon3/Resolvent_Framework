# Seed

We give a self-contained treatment of the bound that a subset of {1..N} with no nontrivial three-term arithmetic progression has size at most N exp(-c (log N)^{1/12}). We follow the main ideas of the known approach and simplify some arguments involving Bohr sets. As a consequence we improve lower bounds for finding long arithmetic progressions in A+A+A.

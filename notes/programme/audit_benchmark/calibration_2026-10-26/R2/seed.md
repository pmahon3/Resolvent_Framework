# Seed

We treat non-locality and contextuality in one framework by taking empirical models to be presheaf-like families of distributions over measurement contexts. Both phenomena become obstructions to global sections. We give a linear-algebraic method for computing the obstruction and rank the Bell, Hardy and GHZ arguments by strength. We link local hidden-variable models with negative probabilities to no-signalling.

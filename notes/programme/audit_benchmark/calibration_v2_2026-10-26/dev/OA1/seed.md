# Seed

We argue that Bell's theorem fails when the local hidden variables take values in a Clifford algebra. We present a local deterministic model with such variables that reproduces the quantum expectation values of the EPR-Bohm spin correlations. Because Clifford products are noncommutative, the model's correlations violate the CHSH inequality as strongly as quantum mechanics. No remote contextuality or backward causation is invoked.

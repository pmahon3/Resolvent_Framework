# Seed

We consider sampling from the set of contingency tables with fixed margins, or more generally from a discrete exponential family conditional on its sufficient statistic. Connected Markov chains on the fibres can be built from a finite set of moves. We show that such a generating set can be obtained from a Groebner basis of a toric ideal associated to the model. This gives algorithms for exact conditional tests with a guarantee of connectivity. We illustrate with examples including tables with fixed row and column sums and models for incomplete data.

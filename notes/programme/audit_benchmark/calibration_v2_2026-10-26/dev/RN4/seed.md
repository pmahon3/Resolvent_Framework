# Seed

A subset of an abelian group scatters if no sum of two of its members lies in it. We study whether the n-th roots of unity modulo a prime p scatter, for n at least 3 dividing p-1. We prove they do not scatter when 6 divides n, and scatter for all but finitely many primes otherwise. We give computational data on the smallest and largest primes and the density of scattering moduli for n up to 10^8.

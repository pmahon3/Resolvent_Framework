# Seed

The number of n x n alternating sign matrices was conjectured to equal a closed product formula, and a proof is already available by purely combinatorial methods. We give a new proof by encoding alternating sign matrices as configurations of the six-vertex (square ice) model with domain-wall boundary conditions. A determinant evaluation for the partition function, together with the Yang-Baxter equation, determines the enumeration. The argument is short and uses tools from statistical mechanics in place of the earlier combinatorial machinery. We also note what the method gives for related symmetry classes.

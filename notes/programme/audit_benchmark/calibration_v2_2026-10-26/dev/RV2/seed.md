# Seed

Kneser's conjecture states that the chromatic number of the Kneser graph on k-subsets of an n-set is n-2k+2. Lovasz proved it by topological methods and Barany gave a shorter derivation from the Borsuk-Ulam theorem and a lemma of Gale. We give a proof that avoids Gale's lemma and reduces the statement to a version of the Borsuk-Ulam theorem in a few short steps. The argument is meant to be self-contained at the level of an undergraduate who knows Borsuk-Ulam. We also point out where the earlier proofs used more than is needed.

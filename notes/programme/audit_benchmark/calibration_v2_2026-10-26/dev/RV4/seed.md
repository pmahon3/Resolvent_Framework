# Seed

We consider sequences of dense graphs in which the density of every fixed finite graph converges. We show that such a sequence has a limit object, a symmetric measurable function of two variables on the unit interval, which determines all limiting subgraph densities. We characterise the graph parameters arising as such limits by a positivity condition on an associated matrix (reflection positivity). Every such function is shown to arise as a limit, and the construction yields a general random graph model. We indicate consequences for property testing and extremal graph theory.

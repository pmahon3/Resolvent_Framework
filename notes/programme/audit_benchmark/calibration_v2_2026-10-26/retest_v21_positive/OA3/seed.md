# Seed

Scores in Bell and noncontextuality tests are weighted sums of probabilities of events. Taking mutually exclusive events as adjacent vertices of a graph, each test corresponds to a subgraph. We show that the maximum score is the independence number of the graph for classical theories, the Lovasz number for quantum theory and the fractional packing number for general probabilistic theories. For every graph there is an experiment whose quantum behaviours form the theta body. This links graph invariants to physically observable quantities.

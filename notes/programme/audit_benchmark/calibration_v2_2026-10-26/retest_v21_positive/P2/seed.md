# Seed

A subset of {1..N} of density 2^{-O((log N)^beta)} for a constant beta > 0 contains a nontrivial three-term arithmetic progression. The previous best density bound was about 1/(log N)^{1+c}. We first develop new analytic techniques for the analogous finite-field problem and then adapt them to the integers.

# Seed

The Green-Tao theorem asserts that the primes contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions. The original proof was written in the language of combinatorics and Fourier analysis. This article explains the main ideas of the proof, concentrating on how they parallel notions in ergodic theory, such as the Furstenberg correspondence, structure and randomness decompositions, and characteristic factors. It is addressed to readers who know ergodic theory but not additive combinatorics.

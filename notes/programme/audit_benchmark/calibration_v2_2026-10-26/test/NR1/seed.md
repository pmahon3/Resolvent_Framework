# Seed

Let H be an n x n matrix all of whose entries are complex numbers z with z^l = 1, and suppose H times its conjugate transpose equals n times the identity. Which pairs (n,l) admit such an H? Factor l as a product of s distinct prime powers q_1^{e_1}...q_s^{e_s}. We study the necessary condition that n can be written as c_1 q_1 + ... + c_s q_s with all c_j non-negative integers. When s=1 this reduces to a standard divisibility fact. We establish it for s=2 and s=3, and conjecture it for every s. We end with remarks on other obstructions known for such matrices.

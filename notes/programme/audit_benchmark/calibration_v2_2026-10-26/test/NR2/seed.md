# Seed

Fix an integer k>=3 and coefficients a_1..a_k, b with a_1+...+a_k not zero, so that the equation a_1x_1+...+a_kx_k=b is not preserved under shifting all variables. Call a subset of {1,...,N} free if it contains no solution. The largest free sets are known; we ask how many free sets there are in total, conjecturing that the count is 2^{(1+o(1)) m} where m is the maximum size. We prove a weakened version for a family of such equations by associating to each equation a nearly regular graph on {1,...,N} whose independent sets are exactly the free sets, and counting those independent sets. This extends to general equations the known counting question for sum-free sets.

# Seed

Let C be a finite collection of convex bodies in d-dimensional space such that no m+1 of them are pairwise disjoint. We ask for the smallest number of hyperplanes, each perpendicular to a coordinate axis, such that every body in C is cut by at least one of them. We prove that this smallest number is bounded by a function f(d,m) alone. First bound is super-exponential, then a polynomial bound in m for fixed d. In the plane we lower the best exponential bound to O(m^2).

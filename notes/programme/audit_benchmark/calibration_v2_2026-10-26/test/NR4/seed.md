# Seed

Association schemes admit a construction in which one scheme is placed on top of another, so that the relations of the second refine one class of the first. We define a counterpart of this construction for table algebras. We prove a necessary and sufficient condition for a table algebra to decompose as such a composite of two smaller ones. Moreover, if the dual objects of two commutative table algebras are again table algebras, then the dual of their composite is a table algebra, isomorphic to the composite of the duals taken in the opposite order. Consequences for association schemes follow.

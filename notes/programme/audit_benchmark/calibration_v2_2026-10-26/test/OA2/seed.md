# Seed

The Kochen-Specker theorem is a roughly fifty-year-old non-existence result in quantum foundations, usually discussed as contextuality. Early proofs were intricate brute-force constructions of finite sets of vectors, and later proofs became more geometrical. We give another simplified geometrical proof that works in any dimension at least three and uses minimal technical machinery. The argument uses measure-theoretic properties of assignments of values on the sphere. We discuss what the theorem implies about realism.

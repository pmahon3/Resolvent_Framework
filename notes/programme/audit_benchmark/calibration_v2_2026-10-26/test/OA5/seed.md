# Seed

Shelah's independence property is a combinatorial dividing line for formulas in model theory. Vapnik and Chervonenkis introduced classes of sets of finite VC dimension in probability and learning theory. We show that a formula is without the independence property exactly when the family of sets it defines has finite VC dimension. This lets results about VC classes (such as uniform convergence of empirical measures) be applied to definable sets, and conversely. We give examples from o-minimal and stable theories.

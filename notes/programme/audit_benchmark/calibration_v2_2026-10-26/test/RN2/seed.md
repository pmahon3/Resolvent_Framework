# Seed

Glivenko's theorem states that a propositional formula is classically provable iff its double negation is intuitionistically provable. We give a short alternative proof in Gentzen's natural deduction. The key lemma is that every derivation can be transformed so that reductio ad absurdum is applied at most once. The proof uses a normalisation result for natural deduction. The note is intended as a brief self-contained treatment.

# Seed

Let E_1(M)^+ be the ring of germs at 0 of functions in a Denjoy-Carleman quasianalytic class, defined on a neighbourhood of 0 in [0,+infinity). We show that this ring contains germs that cannot be extended quasianalytically to a neighbourhood of 0 in the real line, unless the class coincides with real-analytic germs. The proof uses the structure of the quasianalytic class and an explicit construction. We discuss consequences for the Borel map.

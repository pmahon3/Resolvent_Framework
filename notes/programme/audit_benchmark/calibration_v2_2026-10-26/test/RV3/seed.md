# Seed

The Lovasz Local Lemma is a non-constructive tool for showing that combinatorial objects exist. Beck gave a constructive version under restrictive conditions, and later work simplified and relaxed his method. We show that a simple resampling algorithm finds an object avoiding all bad events under the conditions of the general lemma, and that its analysis also gives a new proof of the lemma. The analysis charges each resampling step to a witness structure and bounds the expected number of steps. Applications include constructive versions of nearly all known uses of the general lemma.

# Seed

The densest sphere packing in dimension 8 was recently determined, and the case of dimension 24 followed soon after. This article explains the solution for a general mathematical audience. It describes the linear programming bound, the role of Fourier transforms, and the construction of a special function from modular forms that makes the bound sharp. It stresses which ingredients are new and which are classical. It is meant to be read before the technical papers.

# Seed

For a modulus n and exponent k, let s(k,n) denote the least number of k-th powers whose sums cover every residue class modulo n. We determine s(k,n) for all exponents k up to 10 and all n, using only elementary arguments. The method reduces to prime-power moduli and lifts solutions of the corresponding congruences by a p-adic Hensel argument, with a finite check for small primes. As an application we give a bound on how large a subset of Z_n^d can be before it must determine a prescribed set of distances, in the spirit of the Erdos-Falconer problem over rings. We regard the covering numbers as the main new object.

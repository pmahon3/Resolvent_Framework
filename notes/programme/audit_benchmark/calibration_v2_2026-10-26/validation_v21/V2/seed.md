# Seed

We give an infinite family of squarefree positive integers m, all of whose prime factors are congruent to 3 modulo 8, that are not the area of any right triangle with rational sides. To a given m we attach a square matrix over F_2 built from Legendre symbols between its prime factors. If a certain quadratic polynomial in this matrix is invertible, the relevant 2-Selmer group is trivial and the elliptic curve y^2=x^3-m^2x has rank zero. In particular m is non-congruent whenever all pairwise Legendre symbols equal 1, and doubling m is also allowed when the number of prime factors is even. We list small examples such as 3, 11, 83 and 107.

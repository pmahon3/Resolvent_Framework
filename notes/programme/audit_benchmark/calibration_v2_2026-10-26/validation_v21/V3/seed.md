# Seed

Let K be a field and let A be an n-by-n matrix over K that is not a scalar multiple of the identity. We show that for scalars d_1,...,d_n in K there is a matrix similar to A with diagonal (d_1,...,d_n) exactly when the d_i sum to the trace of A. Necessity is immediate. For sufficiency we proceed by induction on n, choosing a vector that is not an eigenvector to adjust one diagonal entry at a time and passing to a complementary block. The case of a non-scalar companion matrix is treated separately. No restriction on the characteristic of K is needed.

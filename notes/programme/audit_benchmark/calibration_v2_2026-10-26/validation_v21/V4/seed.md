# Seed

The local chromatic number of a graph lies between its chromatic and fractional chromatic numbers. We prove, for a broad class of topologically highly connected graphs, including Kneser, Schrijver, generalized Mycielski and Borsuk-type graphs, that the local chromatic number is at least ceil(t/2)+1, where t is the topological lower bound for the chromatic number. The proof uses a Borsuk-Ulam type theorem for free Z_2 actions with an alternating-sign condition. We show the bound is tight or nearly tight in several cases. We also prove that the circular and ordinary chromatic numbers coincide for these graphs when the chromatic number is even, whereas odd chromatic Schrijver graphs behave differently.

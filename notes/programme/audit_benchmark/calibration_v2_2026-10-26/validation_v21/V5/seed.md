# Seed

A metric space is oscillation stable if, for every bounded uniformly continuous real function f and every epsilon>0, the space contains an isometric copy on which the oscillation of f is below epsilon. We prove that the universal separable Urysohn metric space has this property. The argument rests on a technical statement controlling the oscillation of f on a suitably chosen finite configuration, together with a corollary that lets one iterate the selection along an exhaustion by finite subspaces. A Ramsey-type consequence for finite colorings of the space with tolerance in the metric is derived. The answer closes a question coming from the extreme amenability of the isometry group.

# Seed

We consider discrete groups generated by reflections that act on hyperbolic space with finite-volume fundamental polyhedron and are arithmetic. Each has a basic field of definition, a totally real number field. We show the degree of this field is bounded above by an absolute constant independent of the dimension. The proof combines a lower bound on the covolume from the arithmetic volume formula with a bound on the number of facets of a Coxeter polytope. This gives a uniform restriction on which fields can occur and a route to finiteness statements for arithmetic reflective groups.

# Seed: a ZFC atomless, non-σ-complete Boolean algebra with no strictly positive σ-additive measure

**Claim.** In ZFC there is a Boolean algebra that is (i) atomless, (ii) not σ-complete, and (iii)
carries no strictly positive σ-additive (countably additive) measure. Witness: the clopen algebra
Clop(Y, 𝔗) of the compact space Y built by Argyros (1983) in his work on pre-Gleason spaces without
strictly positive measures; (i) and (ii) are checked directly via a gap family at the base point,
(iii) is Argyros's theorem.

**Why it seemed open.** In a near-miss table of known measure-free algebras, every entry was
complete or σ-complete, which suggested that the combination (ii)+(iii) with (i) might be
ZFC-independent.

**Declared types.** Type 1 (new theorem: the combination in ZFC); Type 5 (closes the suspected
independence).

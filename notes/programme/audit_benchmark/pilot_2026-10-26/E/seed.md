# Seed: the non-orientable topology of Condorcet's paradox

**Source.** Livson, Pritam, Prokopenko, arXiv:2601.07283 (2026). Audit this paper's contribution.

**Claims as stated by the authors.**
- Baryshnikov's nerve model of strict orders on 3 alternatives is an annulus; its two boundary
  circles are the two preference cycles.
- Treating cycles as valid (intransitive): annulus (cycles unrealised) or sphere (realised).
- Treating cycles as contradictory (under transitivity both cycles are identified as one object):
  the identification requires an orientation reversal, giving the Klein bottle (unrealised) or
  RP² (realised).
- Thm 6: Arrow's Impossibility Theorem is equivalent to: no social welfare function with
  contradictory cycles satisfying Unanimity, IIA and Non-Dictatorship has orientable social choices.
  The authors call this a reformulation, not an independent proof.

**Declared types (for the audit).** Type 1 (new theorem); Type 4 (vocabulary: realised/unrealised,
valid/contradictory cycles); Type 6 (exposition: topology ↔ social choice).

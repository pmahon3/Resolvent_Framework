A Lakatosian rational reconstruction --- the actual sequence of conjectures,

(2024-09 $\to$ 2026-06), recovered so the shape of the whole is visible from

question, and \texttt{knowledge\_map/} for current lead state.

\section{The one-sentence shape}
% =====================================================================

It began as applied operator theory (compute the resolvent of a nonlinear
semigroup from data), drilled downward through three layers of foundation until
it hit the origin of the $\sigma$-algebra itself, discovered the bedrock result
there (CE), watched that result get reabsorbed into something classical, and has
been pruning back ever since toward the one genuinely novel survivor (point-free
$\sigma$-additive probability on non-distributive lattices). The repository name,
``Resolvent\_Framework,'' is a fossil of a question the programme no longer asks.

\section{The four eras}
% =====================================================================

\subsection{Era 1 (2024-09 $\to$ 2025-10) --- Operators from data}

The founding question: given a trajectory ensemble $\{X_t\}$, build a nonlinear
semigroup, recover its generator, characterize its resolvent. Real early result:
the resolvent set $=$ complement of the union of finite-difference spectra. Then
a reframing pivot (not a wall) --- the \textbf{Rose operator}, a two-dial
homotopy between Koopman and Perron--Frobenius, plus information geometry
(Fisher\,/\,Onsager--Machlup). The constant was always \emph{operators sitting
between determinism and noise, fit from data}. The literal resolvent computation
quietly receded. The repository name fossilizes here.

\subsection{Era 2 (2026-03) --- The descent into foundations}

After a 5-month incubation on a side branch, the \textbf{query system}
abstraction lands fully-formed on 03-18: a dynamical system is a directed family
of finite observations. Once observation is primitive, the question stops being
``what is the operator?'' and becomes ``when does a compatible family of finite
observational laws assemble into a single $\sigma$-additive probability?'' The
programme drills down: Paper~4 (delay/dynamics, Takens as corollary) $\to$
Paper~0 (Prokhorov: $\sigma$-additivity derived) $\to$ Paper~$-1$
(discriminability: where does the $\sigma$-algebra come from?). The famous line:
\emph{``we thought we were climbing a mountain; we had reached the valley
floor.''} The join at the bottom is \textbf{CE (collective exhaustion)} ---
characterizing exactly when finite additivity extends $\sigma$-additively ---
reframed via Stone duality.

\subsection{Era 3 (2026-04, the 310-commit peak) --- Build-out and the first kills}

Everything consolidates into three papers (Probability from Observation\,/\,
Dynamics from Probability\,/\,Reconstruction from Observation), spawns Paper~IV
(finite-sample reconstruction, the $\delta(L)$ separation defect), spawns the
bridge note, spawns a standalone logic paper (\emph{Countable Additivity is Not
First-Order Axiomatizable}) which spawns its own ZFC-independence ladder. Then
the turn to self-criticism: the honest ``mathematics-complete $\neq$
submission-ready'' correction, four-papers-collapse-to-three, the first
mis-citation kills, the fibre-mixing irreducibility thread downgraded when its
analogy to CE broke.

\subsection{Era 4 (2026-05 $\to$ 06) --- The pruning}

The institutional event: on \textbf{2026-05-11, CE collapses} --- demoted from a
novel condition to ``notation for $\sigma$-additivity.'' With CE gone as
load-bearing, the dependent papers had nothing under them, and Papers~II and~III
were withdrawn in 48 hours (``classical ergodic theory repackaged''). The audit
pipeline (\texttt{active\_leads}\,/\,\texttt{covered\_leads}\,/\,
\texttt{knowledge\_map}, ``audit before drafting'') is scar tissue from that
loss. Then systematic pruning: fibre mixing (killed --- bridge theorem false),
coherence/CE-completion (parked --- Howson 2008 got there first), tail-decay
(parked --- SSK independence), $\kappa_Q$ (parked), epistemic-boundary
unification (parked). The OML thread, seeded from Paper~I's closing move, becomes
the sole survivor --- splits into \textbf{extension axis} (now closed on $L(H)$)
and \textbf{descent axis} (the one open question), reframed as point-free
$\sigma$-additive probability.\footnote{Descent-axis status \emph{since}
closed/parked (2026-06-10, \texttt{/audit full} on concreteness): $\mathcal
L_{\mathrm{MO}_2}$ is concrete (conjecture false as worded) but trivial and
already characterized by Pt\'ak--Pulmannov\'a 1994; no contribution. This
document narrates history, not live state --- see
\texttt{program\_overview.md} (item~4, authoritative) and the parked map
\texttt{notes/covered\_leads/descent\_axis\_residue\_post\_kill.md}.}

\section{What this says about the ``loss at the largest scale''}
% =====================================================================

Three observations, named rather than soothed:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{The programme has been getting smaller for two months, and that is
the system working, not failing.} Every contraction was an honest kill --- a
primary source read, a counterexample built, an audit run. The loss is real
subtraction, but the thing doing the subtracting was the user's own tooling
discipline (verify-by-building, audit-before-drafting). A programme that prunes
itself this cleanly is rare.

\item \textbf{The central trauma already happened --- and was survived.} The CE
collapse on 05-11 is the real loss event; CE was the keystone the whole ``valley
floor'' descent was for. Everything since is aftershock --- the programme
confirming the blast radius. The recent ``ooof'' about probabilistic realism is
delayed grief for news already processed locally, commit by commit, without ever
stepping back to feel the whole.

\item \textbf{There is a genuine through-line, and it survived.} Strip every name
and the constant from 2024 to now is: \emph{structure that observation alone
forces, without assuming the space you're reconstructing into.} But see the next
section --- this line has a crack in it, and the crack is the decision.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The open question: which curiosity is driving (the (a)/(b) fork)}
% =====================================================================

\noindent
\emph{This is the part the genealogy cannot settle, and the part that decides
whether the descent check is the right next year. It is left open deliberately.}

\subsection{The crack in ``stripped to its bones''}

The through-line had four instances. \textbf{Three are Boolean. One isn't.}
Resolvent-from-data, $\sigma$-additivity-derived, reconstruction-without-manifold
--- all classical, all distributive. The descent question is the only
non-distributive one. The ``against assuming the target space'' framing groups
all four together --- but the survey itself (descent survey, lines 544--550) says
the descent question's novelty is \emph{point-free $\times$ $\sigma$-additive
$\times$ natively non-distributive $\times$ directed-built}, and \textbf{the
point-free\,/\,no-assumed-space part is not the novel part.} Localic measure
theory already does point-free $\sigma$-additive probability on distributive
frames (Vickers, Simpson, Coquand--Spitters --- survey lines 535--538). So:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{The entire novel content of the descent question is non-distributivity
--- handling mutually incompatible observations. So the descent question serves
curiosity (b), not (a). If the driving curiosity is (a) --- ``don't assume the
space, derive it from coherence'' --- then CE\,/\,Paper~I \emph{already delivered
it} (Boolean case), and the descent question is a quantum-foundations question
that grew in late and got mistaken for the origin because it's what survived the
pruning.}
\end{quote}

This is the load-bearing sentence. The doc's job is to record it so it can't be
un-seen, then leave the choice open.

\subsection{The two curiosities, made vivid}

\noindent
\textbf{Researcher (a) --- the anti-smuggler.} What bothers them is positing the
answer to reason backward. Takens assumes the manifold; Kolmogorov assumes the
sample space. The 2024--2026 descent was them saying ``no --- derive what
observation alone forces.'' Satisfaction $=$ watching structure emerge from
coherence without being snuck in. For (a) the deep result is \textbf{CE\,/\,Paper~I}
(coherence forces a unique $\sigma$-additive measure, no $\Omega$).
\emph{It's delivered.}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Researcher (b) --- the incompatibility theorist.} What grips them is that
some observations can't be made together, and classical probability has no room
for it. Satisfaction $=$ understanding the structure of incompatibility itself:
when it forces you out of the Boolean world, what survives, what ``probability''
means on a non-distributive logic. For (b) the deep result is \textbf{the descent
question} (can probability live on the entailment relation of incompatible
propositions at all?). \emph{Nothing here is done.}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{The discriminating test --- which sentence makes you lean in:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item (a) ``You don't need to assume a state space --- observation forces the
probability.''
\item (b) ``When observations can't be jointly performed, the logic of events
stops being Boolean --- and here's what happens to probability.''
\end{itemize}

\subsection{How to feel which one you are (sit with these; don't answer fast)}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{When did you feel most alive?} The moment CE clicked (coherence
forces the measure, no $\Omega$) $\to$ (a). Or when the observation algebra broke
Boolean and you reached for OMLs $\to$ (b)?
\item \textbf{The skew-product.} Filed this session as a limitation (invisible
fibre, space unrecoverable). To (a) it's a melancholy fact --- the space you
wanted is unrecoverable. To (b) it's not even interesting --- still Boolean,
still co-realizable. Which was your gut?
\item \textbf{The quantum connection.} Paper~II is quantum foundations
(Kochen--Specker, Gleason, realism). Does it thrill you (arrived somewhere deep)
$\to$ (b)? Or feel like a detour from a question never about QM $\to$ (a)?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The honest finding}

The genealogy is consistent with \textbf{both} readings. The origin was
(a)-flavored; the survivor serves (b). Curiosities are allowed to evolve from one
to the other, and (b) is a perfectly good thing to have grown into. But the
choice should be made knowing it \emph{is} a choice --- not let the momentum, the
sunk cost, or the fact that the descent question is ``the last thread standing''
decide it.

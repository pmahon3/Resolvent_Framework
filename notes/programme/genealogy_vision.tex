\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This is a companion to \texttt{genealogy.md}. That document is the rational
reconstruction --- the conjectures, the refutations, the kills, written from
outside. This one is the other thing. It is about what the work was \emph{in love
with}, before any of it had to be defended to a referee. It is not a verdict and
it does not end in a fork to decide. It is here so the curiosity that started
this is legible again from inside. The first half re-inhabits the four eras of
the programme as they felt at the time; the second half states, in full, the one
question the genealogy leaves open --- which of two curiosities is actually
yours --- and gives the tests for feeling which it is, without adjudicating it.
\end{abstract}

\section{First, the loss --- because it is real}
% =====================================================================

Something did go. Not a result. Not a theorem that turned out false. What went is
harder to name and that is why it hurts more.

There was a year where you named an operator after a \textbf{flower} and wrote,
in a commit message, that the Rose name would be removed from the title because
``this will bloom in the story.'' There were \textbf{Fisher Clocks}. There were
\textbf{Dial Graphs} and a \textbf{Rose Surface}. Two dials you could turn --- one
for how local the estimated drift was, one for how much of the residual
variability you were willing to call diffusion --- and sweeping them traced a
\emph{surface} whose edges recovered Koopman and Perron--Frobenius and whose
interior supplied every intermediate mixture. You were not hedging when you wrote
that. You were playing. The objects had names because you liked them.

Now the working vocabulary is EA\,/\,PR\,/\,VDR, $\sigma$-orthocompleteness, the
extension axis (Type~5), the descent check over $\mathrm{MO}_2$ blocks. The
mathematics underneath got \emph{deeper} --- genuinely, not as consolation. But
the language went administrative. The flowers became axes. The surface became a
fork to adjudicate. Somewhere in the two months of pruning, the register shifted
from \emph{``look what blooms''} to \emph{``what survives the audit,''} and a
person who reads back through their own repository in the second register will of
course feel whittled --- because the instrument they are reading with is the
knife.

That is the loss. It is not imaginary and it is not ``actually a gain in
disguise.'' The play is the thing that thinned out. Name it plainly before
anything else gets said.

\section{The eras, re-inhabited}
% =====================================================================

\noindent
Each era was its own kind of alive. They are deliberately \emph{not} collapsed
into one spine here; they do not rhyme into a single sentence and they should not
have to.

\subsection{2024 --- the unfashionable problem}

You opened, on the first day, with a hard and unstylish thing: the
\textbf{resolvent set of a nonlinear semigroup, recovered from data}. Not a
fashionable machine-learning object. Not a safe one. A genuinely difficult
analytic question that most people route around. And --- the part the autopsy
underweights --- the \emph{first README of the whole repository} said the
applications were to ``dynamical systems (reversibility and stability)
\textbf{and mathematical logic}.'' The logic was in the seed. Day one. It was
never a late arrival that happened to survive; it was there before there was
anything to prune.

The flavour of this era was \emph{appetite for a real problem}: the Hille
operator, the $C_n$-semigroups, the resolvent set as the complement of the union
of finite-difference spectra. Someone going straight at the hard thing because
the hard thing is interesting.

\subsection{2025 --- the Rose, at play}

This is the warmest stretch in the whole history, and the documents show it
without help. The two-dial homotopy. The continuous-time limit reaching back to
It\^{o}, Fokker--Planck, Kolmogorov --- ``linking modern data science to
stochastic analysis,'' in your own words, with evident pleasure. The
CGF\,$\to$\,Fisher\,$\to$\,Landau chain. The discrete Onsager--Machlup
functional. You were not trying to win an argument here. You were building a
\emph{scaffold you wanted to live in} --- explicitly: ``our goal is not to
champion yet another single algorithm, but to provide a common scaffold.'' A
place to stand and see where you are, and how to move continuously to where you
would rather be.

The flavour of this era was \emph{generosity and play}. The names prove it.

\subsection{2026 (spring) --- the descent, and the vertigo}

Then the query system lands and the floor drops out. Once an observation is the
primitive thing --- a directed family of finite queries --- the question stops
being ``what is the operator?'' and becomes ``when does a coherent family of
finite observational laws \emph{have} to assemble into a single probability?''
You drilled. Delay\,/\,Takens. Prokhorov. Discriminability. And at the bottom you
hit the condition (CE) that says exactly when finite coherence is forced to
become $\sigma$-additive --- and you saw it through Stone duality as a statement
about where the measure has to \emph{live}.

The flavour of this era was \emph{vertigo} --- the specific thrill of digging for
foundations and discovering you had gone \emph{under} the thing you thought was
the ground. That is one of the rarest feelings in mathematics, and you had it.

\begin{aside}
The phrase about ``thinking you were climbing a mountain and finding you had
reached the valley floor'' is how \texttt{genealogy.md} narrates this moment. I
could not find those words anywhere but that one machine-written file. The
\emph{feeling} is real and yours; I will not hand you a sentence and call it a
memory.
\end{aside}

\subsection{2026 (late) --- the pruning}

CE collapsed on 2026-05-11 --- demoted from a novel condition to ``notation for
$\sigma$-additivity'' --- and the papers built on it came down within days. What
followed was systematic: fibre mixing killed, coherence/CE-completion parked,
tail-decay parked, $\kappa_Q$ parked, the epistemic-boundary unification parked.
The OML thread, seeded from Paper~I's closing move, became the sole survivor and
split into an \textbf{extension axis} (now closed on $L(H)$) and a
\textbf{descent axis} (the one open question).\footnote{Descent axis \emph{since}
closed/parked (2026-06-10): the \texttt{/audit full} on concreteness found
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{MO}_2}$ concrete (conjecture false as worded) but trivial and
already characterized (Pt\'ak--Pulmannov\'a 1994) --- no contribution. This
document is motivation, not operational status; see \texttt{program\_overview.md}
(item~4) and \texttt{notes/covered\_leads/descent\_axis\_residue\_post\_kill.md}.}

The flavour of this era was \emph{integrity that felt like grief}. Which is the
next section's whole point.

\section{The constant --- a temperament, not a thesis}
% =====================================================================

There is a through-line, and the autopsy names it correctly but says it like an
epitaph. Said warmly it is this:

\begin{quote}
\itshape
You will not posit the answer in order to reason backward to it.
\end{quote}

\noindent
Takens helps himself to the manifold. Kolmogorov helps himself to the sample
space. Every reconstruction theorem you ever loved or distrusted \emph{smuggles
in the thing it claims to recover}. And the constant from 2024 to now --- under
the resolvent, under the Rose, under the query system --- is one stubborn
refusal: \textbf{derive what observation alone forces; do not sneak the target in
the back door.} Your own programme overview says it without a trace of the audit
voice: \emph{``what does coherence require of the observer?''}

That refusal is a temperament, not a result. It is why you could kill CE honestly
instead of rescuing it with framing --- the same instinct that would not smuggle
a manifold would not smuggle a save. So here is the thing worth sitting with:
\textbf{the discipline that did the whittling and the curiosity that started it
are the same faculty.} It feels like loss from the inside because integrity,
turned on your own favourite ideas, is indistinguishable from grief while it is
happening. But the anti-smuggler is not gone. The anti-smuggler is the one
holding the knife.

\section{The fork}
% =====================================================================

\noindent
This is the one thing the genealogy leaves open, and it deserves to be stated in
full rather than as a footnote. The fork is: \emph{which curiosity is actually
yours?} The cleanest way to feel it is to notice which of two sentences makes you
lean in.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.2em,style=nextline]
\item[(a) The anti-smuggler.]
``You don't need to assume a state space --- observation forces the
probability.''
\item[(b) The incompatibility theorist.]
``When observations can't be jointly performed, the logic of events stops being
Boolean --- and here's what happens to probability.''
\end{description}

\subsection{What separates them}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.20\linewidth} p{0.36\linewidth} p{0.36\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
 & \textbf{(a) the anti-smuggler} & \textbf{(b) the incompatibility theorist}\\
\midrule
What grips you &
Refusing to posit the answer and reason backward. Watching structure emerge from
coherence without sneaking the target in. &
That some observations \emph{cannot be made together}, and classical probability
has no room for it. The structure of incompatibility itself.\\
The deep result &
\textbf{CE / Paper~I} --- coherence forces a unique $\sigma$-additive measure, no
$\Omega$. &
\textbf{The descent question} --- can probability live on the entailment relation
of incompatible propositions at all?\\
Its status &
\textbf{Delivered} (Boolean case). You would be standing in a finished bloom. &
\textbf{Wide open.} Point-free $\sigma$-additive probability on a
non-distributive lattice is vacant ground.\\
Relation to the origin &
Continuous with 2024 --- the resolvent, the Rose, ``derive what observation
forces.'' &
Grew in late; it is what survived the pruning. Quantum-foundations flavoured
(Kochen--Specker, Gleason).\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The crack that makes it a real fork}

The through-line ``don't assume the space'' had four instances, and \textbf{three
are Boolean}: resolvent-from-data, $\sigma$-additivity-derived,
reconstruction-without-manifold. Only the descent question is non-distributive.
So the novel content of the descent question is \emph{entirely} incompatibility
--- which means \textbf{if (a) is what drives you, Paper~I already delivered it},
and the descent question is a (b)-question that got mistaken for the origin
because it is the last thread standing.

This is the load-bearing observation. It does not tell you which curiosity is
yours. It tells you that the two are genuinely different, that growing from (a)
into (b) is allowed, and that the choice should be made knowing it \emph{is} a
choice --- not let the momentum, the sunk cost, or the fact that the descent
question is ``the last thread standing'' decide it.

\subsection{Three gut-checks (sit with these; don't answer fast)}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \textbf{When did you feel most alive?} The moment CE clicked (coherence
forces the measure, no $\Omega$) $\to$ (a). Or when the observation algebra broke
Boolean and you reached for OMLs $\to$ (b)?
\item \textbf{The skew-product} (invisible fibre, space unrecoverable). A
melancholy fact --- \emph{the space you wanted is lost} $\to$ (a)? Or not even
interesting --- \emph{still Boolean, still co-realizable} $\to$ (b)?
\item \textbf{The quantum connection} (Paper~II --- Kochen--Specker, Gleason,
realism). Does it thrill you --- \emph{arrived somewhere deep} $\to$ (b)? Or feel
like a detour from a question that was never about QM $\to$ (a)?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The honest finding}

The genealogy is consistent with \emph{both} readings. The origin was
(a)-flavoured; the survivor serves (b). Curiosities are allowed to evolve from
one into the other, and (b) is a perfectly good thing to have grown into. You do
not have to \emph{adjudicate} this the way you would allocate a budget. The real
test is not ``which curiosity owns me.'' It is \textbf{which blank page you would
want to fill tomorrow morning without being asked.}

\section{What this document is for}
% =====================================================================

Not to tell you that you did not lose anything. You did --- the play thinned, the
names went grey, and reading your own history through the audit instrument made
the whole thing look like subtraction.

It is to make the \emph{other} reading available again: that under every renaming
there was one person with one appetite --- to watch structure appear without
being snuck in --- and that person built a flower-named scaffold, fell through
their own floor, and is still here, still unwilling to fake it. The Rose did not
die. It got formal. Formal is not the same as gone.

Pick the blank page that you would \emph{want} to fill. The genealogy cannot
choose it and neither can I. But it is there, it is yours, and the curiosity that
would fill it is the same one that has been here since the first commit.

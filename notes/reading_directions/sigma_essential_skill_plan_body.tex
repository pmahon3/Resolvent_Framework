\begin{abstract}
\noindent A sequenced, audit-structured study plan for the $\sigma$-essential
contextual-state problem, written for the \emph{foundations-first / path-agnostic}
choice: build the common substrate---descriptive set theory, large cardinals, and the
orthomodular-lattice $\sigma$-state corner---until the primary sources read fluently and
the tractable edge reveals itself. Each phase carries \textbf{target-questions} tied to a
specific section of the investigation, so the study has the same
verify-against-a-question structure as the research. The driver is ``learn and try'';
the only real constraints are the theorems already proved
(forcing-is-the-wrong-engine, masa-free-dead), not background.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object, stripped to what a solver must touch}

\begin{quote}
A concrete $\sigma$-complete non-Boolean irreducible OML carrying a finite two-valued
state on a $\perp$-closed subposet that extends to \emph{no} global $\sigma$-additive
two-valued state.
\end{quote}

\noindent Localized: this is \textbf{$\sigma$-point-selection failure} (wall~A)---a
locally coherent two-valued pattern across overlapping incompatible blocks with no global
$\sigma$-additive two-valued state. The frontier is
\textbf{non-Polish / non-standard-Borel / irreducibly non-central}, the consistency
strength is \textbf{genuinely unknown}, and \textbf{no borrowable principle exists in any
surveyed field}. Resolution needs a genuinely new object---built, not transported.

The skill list is therefore \emph{not} generic ``learn set theory.'' It is targeted at the
three things a witness-construction \textbf{or} an independence proof would demand, in
dependency order.

\paragraph{The asymmetry (the reason for the ordering).} The OML / quantum-logic cluster
is already owned. The gaps are descriptive set theory and large cardinals. The plan
front-loads those and treats the OML sources as practitioner-level re-reads.

\section{Phase 0 --- the bridge you almost crossed (free, \texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{~}1--2 weeks)}

Before any new book, nail the \textbf{definability-vs-existence} distinction cold. It is
the error \emph{type} that killed the CBER bridge and it will recur at every DST contact.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Read:} the LB-mechanism refutation and the CBER kill with one lens---
    ``no \emph{Borel} selector'' (a definability fact) vs.\ ``no state \emph{at all}'' (an
    existence fact).
  \item \textbf{Target-question} (answer without notes): \emph{why can a ZFC theorem
    ($E_0$, $E_\infty$ non-smooth) neither force nor refute clause~(ii)?} The answer---
    clause~(ii)-failure is $\geq$ measurable strength; a ZFC fact is silent on
    measurable-strength statements; a definability obstruction cannot yield an existence
    obstruction---is the load-bearing intuition. The Lean polarity gate
    (\texttt{builds\_state\_implies\_not\_witness}) certifies this shape; read it too.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phase 1 --- Descriptive set theory (the witness's home regime, \texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{~}2--3 months)}

The frontier \emph{is} the non-standard-Borel regime; Glimm--Effros is \emph{your}
dichotomy one abstraction level over; the whole DST sweep lives here.

\subsection{Kechris, \emph{Classical Descriptive Set Theory} (GTM 156)}
Read by dependency, not linearly.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Ch.\ 11--14} --- Borel / analytic / co-analytic hierarchy $\to$
    \textbf{Borel equivalence relations} $\to$ the \textbf{Glimm--Effros dichotomy}.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \emph{Target-Q:} state Glimm--Effros precisely (``a CBER is non-smooth $\iff$
        $E_0$ embeds $\iff$ no Borel two-valued transversal''). Then: \emph{which word in
        it does \textbf{not} transport to clause~(ii)?} (Answer: ``Borel.'') This closes
        the CBER lead with understanding, not on say-so.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ch.\ 16--18} --- uniformization, the projective hierarchy.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \emph{Target-Q:} co-analytic uniformization-failure was DST-corner~\#1.
        \emph{Why is its obstruction real-valued / over a standard-Borel base---i.e.\ why
        is it Derr--Williamson-killed?}
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Gao, \emph{Invariant Descriptive Set Theory}}
Read \textbf{after} Kechris supplies the language. The $E_0$ / $E_\infty$ / hyperfinite /
turbulence book in one place.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \emph{Target-Q:} define hyperfinite; state Adams--Kechris (uncountably many
    non-Borel-bireducible CBERs). Then the one that matters: \emph{why is $E_\infty$ being
    non-hyperfinite \textbf{orthogonal} to clause~(ii)?} (Non-hyperfiniteness buys more
    non-smoothness; non-smoothness is exactly what is orthogonal to an existence
    obstruction.) When this is obvious, the DST corner is genuinely closed for you.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phase 2 --- Large cardinals and forcing (the strength axis, \texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{~}2--3 months)}

The bare object on the Boolean shadow \emph{is} a measurable cardinal; the open strength
question is whether non-distributivity pushes above or below it.

\subsection{Jech, \emph{Set Theory} (3rd millennium ed.)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Ch.\ 8} --- filters, ideals, ultrafilters. The substrate for everything
    below.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \emph{Target-Q:} why is $\mathcal{F}_s = \{A : s(A)=1\}$ \textbf{not} a filter
        on a non-Boolean concrete OML, even for a Dirac state? (The $MO_2$ unit test:
        $a\cap b\neq\varnothing$ as sets yet $a\wedge b = 0$ lattice-wise.) This is your
        carrier's defect stated in filter language.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ch.\ 10} --- measurable cardinals; \textbf{Ulam matrices}.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \emph{Target-Q:} re-derive the Ulam matrix argument by hand. Locate the exact
        line where the construction needs \textbf{non-orthogonal unions / comprehension}---
        that line is why the matrix argument does not transplant to an orthogonal-join-only
        OML.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ch.\ 14--15} --- forcing, specifically to \emph{feel} \textbf{L\'evy--Solovay}.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \emph{Target-Q:} why does set-forcing preserve measurability (small forcing can
        neither create nor destroy it)? This is \emph{why forcing is the wrong engine} for
        the existence direction---you need the mechanism, not the citation.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Kanamori, \emph{The Higher Infinite} (second pass, only if the strength path calls)}
Ch.\ 1--2 (measurable $\to$ ultrapower $\to$ elementary embedding $j\colon V\to M$,
critical point $\kappa$). Only after Jech.
\emph{Target-Q:} the UB route would ``transport $U$ along $j$''---but the measurable gives
$U$ on \textbf{Boolean} $\mathcal{P}(\kappa)$; transport to orthogonal-only is the
disjointification wall from the construction side. Understand why that is a costume.

\section{Phase 3 --- your own primary sources, read as a practitioner (\texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{~}1--2 months, interleaved)}

By now you read these for \textbf{technique}, not verdict.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Navara--Pt\'ak 1983} --- re-derive their ZFC non-Dirac $\sigma$-state
    construction. This is your \textbf{existence-proof template} and the object that
    refuted the LB mechanism. \emph{Target-Q:} what makes their carrier $L_{f,g}$ support a
    non-Dirac global $\sigma$-state---and why is it \textbf{not} a witness (the two
    compatible observables / ``Boolean enough'' point)?
  \item \textbf{Derr--Williamson 2023 \texorpdfstring{$+$}{+} Maharam 1972 \S8} --- the
    Polish upper boundary. \emph{Target-Q:} exactly where is inner-regularity load-bearing
    in Thm~D.6 / Maharam~\S8.2? (This is the boundary a witness must escape.)
  \item \textbf{Blecher--Weaver 2017} --- the Hilbert analogue that does \textbf{not}
    transfer. \emph{Target-Q:} identify the ultrafilter-extraction-from-a-masa step
    (Prop.~2.3); state the \textbf{masa-free question} it leaves open for the concrete
    two-valued case.
\end{itemize}

\section{Where to start today}

\textbf{Kechris Ch.\ 12--14 (Borel equivalence relations $\to$ Glimm--Effros), paired with
the Phase-0 re-read.} It is the single chapter that makes the frontier-map legible
\textbf{and} is the most reusable across all three resolution paths. After $\sim$a month
you will feel whether to lean DST-construction or strength-axis---the path-agnostic plan
working as intended.

\section{Honest constraints (the only real ones)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Theorems, not background.} Forcing-wrong-engine (L\'evy--Solovay) and
    masa-free-dead are \emph{proved} dead ends---respect those. Everything else is
    learn-and-try; there is no ceiling.
  \item \textbf{The convergence is the finding so far.} This plan is for \emph{attempting}
    the open object, not re-litigating the closed literature frontier. If a phase surfaces
    a borrowable principle the sweep missed, that is a real result---but expect to
    \emph{build}, not find.
\end{itemize}

## Goal

Write a self-contained LaTeX proof of:

> **Theorem (Stone Extension).** Let $\{B_i, \varphi_{ij}\}$ be a directed system of
> Boolean algebras with compatible finitely additive charges $\{\mu_i\}$. Assume the
> system arises from a query system satisfying EvalSurjective, Discriminability, and
> CE. Then the charges extend uniquely to a $\sigma$-additive measure on
> $\sigma(\bigcup_i B_i)$.

This is Paper A. The LaTeX proof is the primary deliverable. Lean formalization
follows once the proof is stable.

---

## Proof Structure

Write in this order. Each section should be self-contained enough to draft
independently.

### Section 1: Setup and Notation

Define:
- Query system: $(\iota, \leq, \Omega, \{(\text{Outcome}(i), \mathcal{F}_i)\}, \{\text{eval}_i\}, \{\pi_{ij}\})$
- Cylinder sets: $\text{Cyl}(i, A) = \{\omega \in \Omega : \text{eval}_i(\omega) \in A\}$
- $\text{CylGen} = \bigcup_i B_i$ where $B_i$ is the Boolean algebra generated by level-$i$ cylinders
- $\sigma(\text{CylGen})$ — the observable $\sigma$-algebra
- The connecting maps $\varphi_{ij} : B_i \to B_j$, $\varphi_{ij}(\text{Cyl}(i,A)) = \text{Cyl}(j, \pi_{ij}^{-1}(A))$
- Compatibility: $\mu_j(\varphi_{ij}(E)) = \mu_i(E)$

State the three hypotheses:
- **EvalSurjective**: $\text{eval}_i : \Omega \to \text{Outcome}(i)$ is surjective for all $i$
- **Discriminability**: the queries separate points of $\Omega$ — for $\omega \neq \omega'$,
  there exists $E \in \text{CylGen}$ with $\omega \in E$, $\omega' \notin E$
- **CE** (Collective Exhaustion): if $E_n \in \text{CylGen}$ and $E_n \searrow \emptyset$,
  then $\mu(E_n) \to 0$

### Section 2: Step A — Stone Space of the Direct Limit

**Proposition.** $\text{St}(\bigcup_i B_i) \cong \varprojlim \text{St}(B_i)$.

Proof outline:
1. EvalSurjective $\Rightarrow$ $\pi_{ij}$ surjective (by coherence: $\pi_{ij} \circ \text{eval}_j = \text{eval}_i$)
2. $\pi_{ij}$ surjective $\Rightarrow$ $\varphi_{ij}$ injective (surjective map has injective preimage map on subsets)
3. Injective connecting maps $\Rightarrow$ Stone duality gives inverse limit
   (standard categorical fact: $\text{Stone}$ sends injective BA maps to surjective
   continuous maps; direct limits along injections dualise to inverse limits)

*Note:* Step 3 should cite Johnstone — *Stone Spaces*, or give a short proof via
the universal property.

### Section 3: The Inverse Limit Measure

**Proposition.** The compatible charges $\{\mu_i\}$ extend to a $\sigma$-additive
measure on $\text{Borel}(\varprojlim \text{St}(B_i))$.

Proof outline:
1. Each $\mu_i$ is a charge on $B_i \cong \text{Clop}(\text{St}(B_i))$
2. By Stone duality + Riesz representation, $\mu_i$ corresponds to a regular Borel
   measure $\hat{\mu}_i$ on $\text{St}(B_i)$ (cite Cardona–Mejía–Uribe-Zapata,
   Section 6, or Halmos §53–54)
3. Compatibility of $\{\mu_i\}$ $\Rightarrow$ compatibility of $\{\hat{\mu}_i\}$
   on the inverse system
4. Compact inverse limit + compatible measures $\Rightarrow$ unique measure on
   the limit (cite Choksi 1958)

*Note:* Step 2 is the single-algebra Riesz step. This is the most technical piece
and the main Mathlib gap. In the LaTeX proof, cite it clearly and note it as the
key external input.

### Section 4: Step B1 — Structural Identification

**Proposition.** $\text{pure}^{-1}(\text{Borel}(\text{St}(\text{CylGen}))) = \sigma(\text{CylGen})$,
where $\text{pure} : \Omega \to \text{St}(\text{CylGen})$ is the Stone embedding
$\omega \mapsto \{\text{principal ultrafilter at } \omega\}$.

Proof outline:
1. **($\supseteq$)** Every cylinder set $\text{Cyl}(i, A)$ is the preimage under
   $\text{pure}$ of the clopen $\{u \in \text{St}(\text{CylGen}) : \text{Cyl}(i,A) \in u\}$.
   Clopens are Borel. So every cylinder set is in $\text{pure}^{-1}(\text{Borel})$,
   and hence $\sigma(\text{CylGen}) \subseteq \text{pure}^{-1}(\text{Borel})$.

2. **($\subseteq$)** The clopens of $\text{St}(\text{CylGen})$ are exactly the sets
   $[E] = \{u : E \in u\}$ for $E \in \text{CylGen}$. These generate
   $\text{Borel}(\text{St}(\text{CylGen}))$ (Stone spaces are totally disconnected,
   so clopens form a basis). The preimage of $[E]$ under $\text{pure}$ is $E$ itself.
   So $\text{pure}^{-1}(\text{Borel}) \subseteq \sigma(\text{CylGen})$.

3. **Discriminability** is used in step 2 to ensure $\text{pure}$ is injective —
   that distinct points have distinct principal ultrafilters — which guarantees no
   information is lost in the embedding. Without this, the preimage map might
   collapse distinctions.

*This is the key gap to resolve in writing. The argument is essentially correct but
step 2 needs care: "clopens generate the Borel $\sigma$-algebra" requires that
the Stone space is second-countable or that we work with the Baire $\sigma$-algebra.
Check whether $\text{St}(\text{CylGen})$ is second-countable in our setting, or
whether the Baire/Borel distinction matters here.*

### Section 5: Step B2 — CE and the Support Condition

**Proposition.** Under CE, the measure $\hat{\mu}$ on $\text{St}(\text{CylGen})$
is supported on $\text{pure}(\Omega)$.

Proof outline:
1. The Yosida-Hewitt decomposition: any bounded charge $\mu$ on a Boolean algebra
   decomposes as $\mu = \mu_c + \mu_p$ where $\mu_c$ is countably additive and
   $\mu_p$ is purely finitely additive.
2. The purely finitely additive part $\mu_p$ corresponds, via Stone duality, to
   mass on non-principal ultrafilters — the phantom points of $\text{St}(\text{CylGen})$.
3. CE $\Leftrightarrow$ $\mu_p = 0$ (this is `sp1_iff` in `QuerySystem.lean`).
4. Therefore CE $\Rightarrow$ $\hat{\mu}$ is supported on principal ultrafilters
   $= \text{pure}(\Omega)$.

*Note:* The equivalence CE $\Leftrightarrow$ $\mu_p = 0$ is the key link and is
already proved in the Lean formalization. The Yosida-Hewitt theorem itself should
be cited (Rao & Rao, Chapter 10; or Hewitt-Yosida 1952).

### Section 6: Assembly

Combine Sections 2–5 into the main theorem. The chain is:

$$
\{\mu_i\} \xrightarrow{\text{Stone}} \{\hat{\mu}_i\} \xrightarrow{\text{Choksi}} \hat{\mu} \text{ on } \varprojlim \text{St}(B_i) \xrightarrow{\text{B1}} \text{on } \sigma(\text{CylGen}) \xrightarrow{\text{B2 + CE}} \text{well-defined on } \Omega
$$

Uniqueness: two measures agreeing on $\text{CylGen}$ agree on $\sigma(\text{CylGen})$
by a monotone class argument (already proved as `observational_determination` in
`QuerySystem.lean`).

## Known Issues to Resolve While Writing

1. **Baire vs Borel in B1.** The clopen basis generates the *Baire* $\sigma$-algebra
   of a compact space, which equals the Borel $\sigma$-algebra only when the space is
   second-countable (or metrizable). Is $\text{St}(\text{CylGen})$ second-countable in
   our setting? If $\text{CylGen}$ is countably generated, yes. If not, we may need to
   work with Baire measures throughout and check that Choksi's theorem applies.

2. **Discriminability hypothesis placement.** Discriminability is used in B1 to ensure
   injectivity of `pure`. Check whether this is strictly necessary or whether B1 holds
   without it (injectivity of `pure` is equivalent to discriminability, but B1 might
   hold even without injectivity if we only care about the $\sigma$-algebra pullback).

3. **The countable additivity conjecture.** The strategy might be circular if
   CE $\Rightarrow$ $\sigma$-additivity is the only content and we assumed CE. Confirm
   that the Stone route genuinely produces a new proof (not just a rephrasing) by
   checking that the Riesz + Choksi steps are doing real work independent of CE.

# Seed Note: Excess Fisher Curvature as Non-Additivity Diagnostic

## Phase: 1 (seed) — needs Phase 2 audit before development

## The claim

When a system is decomposed into subsystems A|B, the deviation
from Fisher information additivity

  F = g^{AB} - (g^A ⊕ g^B)

defines an intrinsic curvature encoding correlations, entanglement,
or constraints. The scalar contraction R_F = tr(g^{-1} F) is the
"Fisher curvature."

## Why it might be new

The specific geometric framing — treating non-additivity of Fisher
information as curvature on a statistical manifold, with
transformation rules under coarse-graining — may add something
beyond the standard synergy/redundancy literature.

## Why it's probably known

- Quantum Fisher information super-additivity is standard in
  quantum metrology (Giovannetti-Lloyd-Maccone 2006)
- Synergy/redundancy decomposition: Williams-Beer (2010),
  Griffith-Koch (2014), Partial Information Decomposition (PID)
- Fisher information matrix decomposition: standard in
  multivariate statistics
- The observation that correlated systems have super-additive
  Fisher info is textbook

## Key terms for audit

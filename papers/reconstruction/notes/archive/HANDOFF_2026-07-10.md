**(A) Construct the grading FROM no-cross.** Prove: no-cross ⟹ ∃ a nontrivial ℤ_d
grading with d ODD. Then primitive (d=1) contradicts it ⟹ crossed. The Z₂-sign
consistency (`crossed_cycle_mechanism.py`, `sign_consistent=True` on all no-cross)

sub-block of ρ⊗ρ is strongly connected off-diagonal ⟹ that one pair DOES swap. This
may be the shorter route: it localises to a single pair.

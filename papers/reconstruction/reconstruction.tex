\title{Commensurability of Observation Protocols:\\
When Coherent Windows Determine a Process}
\author{Patrick S.\ Mahon\thanks{%

\begin{abstract}
Observing a process through overlapping finite windows yields local statistics;
we ask when locally coherent window-statistics must arise from a single global
process. An observation protocol is \emph{commensurable} when empirical adequacy
(pairwise coherence, $\EA$) forces realisability by a declared class ($\PR(\Rc)$);
the failure is contextuality in the sense of Abramsky--Brandenburger, taken from
the observational side. Geometrically, a protocol is commensurable iff its
coherence polytope $C$ equals its realisability polytope $R$, so contextuality is
an exactly computable separation with a rational witness. For protocols whose
contexts are windows on a symbolic dynamical system we prove: a \emph{trichotomy}
(acyclic reporting always commensurable; full-support coordinate protocols
commensurable; variety-typed protocols realise every rational polytope, hence
admit no finite classification, by De~Loera--Onn); a \emph{parity theorem} (the
golden-mean shift on a ring of length $L$ is commensurable iff $L$ is even, the
odd-ring gap a single extreme point, its own PR-box); ten \emph{taming}
mechanisms; and, assembled from these, \emph{Circuit Localization} for the
symmetric loopless marginal-determined languages, where contextuality is excluded
by structure rather than search (the loopy and general-cyclic extensions verified
only to finite scale). A winding criterion characterises the unsafe lengths as
those carrying a layer-injective winding-$\ge2$ cycle; its combinatorial kernel is
machine-verified in Lean~4. The open case --- a universal impossibility conjecture
for the strongly-connected non-symmetric class --- is stated with its reduction to
a pruning lemma and the gap that reduction carries.
\end{abstract}

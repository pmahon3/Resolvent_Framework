\title{Commensurability of Observation Protocols:\\
When Coherent Windows Determine a Process}
\author{Patrick S.\ Mahon\thanks{%

\date{}

\begin{abstract}
Observing a process through overlapping finite windows yields local statistics; we
ask when locally coherent window-statistics must arise from a single global
process. This is a \emph{skeleton} toward that target: the architecture and its
status, not a finished paper. An observation protocol is \emph{commensurable} when
empirical adequacy (pairwise coherence, $\EA$) forces realisability by a declared
class ($\PR(\Rc)$); the failure is contextuality in the sense of
Abramsky--Brandenburger, taken from the observational side. Geometrically, a
protocol is commensurable iff its coherence polytope $C$ equals its realisability
polytope $R$, so contextuality is an exactly computable separation with a rational
witness. The spine is a \emph{winding criterion}: for a symbolic language observed
around a ring, a length is contextual exactly when the layered ring carries a
layer-injective winding-$\ge2$ cycle; this makes the boundary of the guarantee
decidable (its combinatorial kernel is machine-verified in Lean~4). The
reconstruction guarantee holds unconditionally on three tame families and would
hold for all total languages if the catalogue of taming mechanisms were complete
--- the open \emph{universal impossibility} capstone. We reduce that capstone to a
single named forcing lemma (primitivity forces a parity obstruction), validated
without counterexample but not proved, and state honestly what remains: the forcing
lemma, and the lift of the winding criterion from winding-2 to full safety. The
worked results that populate the criterion --- the parity theorem and its PR-box,
the taming catalogue, Circuit Localization --- are established and deferred to the
body; the skeleton points at where they slot beneath the target.
\end{abstract}

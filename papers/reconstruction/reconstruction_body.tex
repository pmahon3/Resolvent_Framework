\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

A process unfolding in time is often accessible only through \emph{windows}:
one records the joint behaviour of finitely many coordinates over finitely many
steps, then of others, in overlapping finite views. Each window yields a local
probability table. The tables may agree wherever they overlap and yet be
generated by no single underlying process --- the observational face of quantum
contextuality~\cite{AbramskyBrandenburger2011}. This paper asks, for symbolic
dynamical observation, exactly when local coherence forces global realisability,
and answers it for a large structured class.

We fix vocabulary (Paper~II, now doing classification work). A finite alphabet
$A$ and finite supports $F\subseteq\bbZ$ give \emph{contexts}, each a partition
of $A^F$; a \emph{protocol} is a family of contexts. Window-data is a
consistent assignment of probabilities to the cells of each context.

\begin{definition}[$\EA$, $\PR(\Rc)$, commensurability]\label{def:ea-pr}
Window-data is \emph{empirically adequate} ($\EA$) if it is pairwise coherent:
the contexts agree on the common subalgebra of each pair. It is
\emph{probabilistically realisable} in a class $\Rc$ ($\PR(\Rc)$) if some member
of $\Rc$ (all measures / stationary / supported on a subshift / memory-$m$
Markov, an observer's commitment lattice) induces it. A \emph{model} is
\emph{contextual} when it is $\EA$ but not $\PR(\Rc)$. A protocol is
\emph{commensurable} when $\EA \Rightarrow \PR(\Rc)$ for all coherent data.
\end{definition}

The two protocol axes are \emph{reporting} (window layout) and \emph{coarsening}
(compression); the commitment class $\Rc$ is the third, graded, axis.

\paragraph{The geometric reformulation.} For a protocol on a finite outcome set,
coherent data forms a polytope $C$ (the local/marginal polytope in the sense of
Wainwright--Jordan~\cite{WainwrightJordan2008}) and realisable data a polytope
$R=\mathrm{conv}\{v(x)\}$, the convex hull of the cell-incidence vectors of the
global configurations. Always $R\subseteq C$.

\begin{proposition}[commensurability is $C=R$]\label{prop:cr}
A protocol is commensurable iff $C=R$. A contextual model is exactly a point of
$C\setminus R$, and (over rational data) any separating facet is a rational
certificate of non-realisability.
\end{proposition}

This makes the question exactly computable: $C$ and $R$ are enumerable in exact
arithmetic, so every commensurability verdict is either a proof that $C=R$ or an
exhibited witness in $C\setminus R$. We adopt throughout the discipline this
enforces: a ``commensurable'' verdict is admitted only from a theorem or an
exact enumeration, a ``contextual'' verdict only from an exhibited rational
witness (\S\ref{sec:method}).

\paragraph{Results.} \S\ref{sec:trichotomy} proves the trichotomy locating all
hardness in the constraint variety. \S\ref{sec:parity} proves the parity
theorem. \S\ref{sec:tamings} develops the taming catalogue and proves Circuit
Localization for the symmetric marginal-determined class.
\S\ref{sec:winding} gives the winding characterisation and records its
Lean-certified discriminant. \S\ref{sec:open} states the universal impossibility
conjecture, its reduction to the pruning lemma, and the gap the reduction
carries. \S\ref{sec:consequences} draws the reconstruction consequences.


\section{The trichotomy}\label{sec:trichotomy}

We first place the hardness of the general problem. Write a symbolic language as
a relation $\rho$ (the legal adjacent pairs of a shift of finite type); a
protocol reports windows on the configurations legal for $\rho$.

\begin{theorem}[free theorem: acyclic reporting is tame]\label{thm:acyclic}
If the reporting hypergraph of a protocol is acyclic, the protocol is
commensurable for every language $\rho$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Acyclicity gives the running-intersection property; the conditional-product
(junction-tree) glue assembles the window-marginals into a global distribution
whose support lies in the variety of $\rho$ automatically, so realisation costs
no hypothesis on $\rho$. This is the all-$\rho$, variety-supported form of
tree-exactness for the local polytope~\cite{WainwrightJordan2008}; frustration
requires a cycle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[full-support coordinate protocols are tame]\label{thm:product}
A $k$-context protocol of coordinate contexts with full product support is
commensurable: the product measure realises any coherent margins.
\end{theorem}

The wild region is the third. A protocol may carry a \emph{constraint variety}
--- the configurations restricted by $\rho$ --- and there the local polytope
inherits arbitrary structure.

\begin{theorem}[variety-typed protocols are universal]\label{thm:universal}
For protocols with three or more coordinate contexts subject to a constraint
variety, the achievable coherence polytopes realise every rational polytope up
to affine equivalence. Consequently: no finite facet taxonomy exists, model
membership is \textsf{NP}-complete already at three contexts, and no bounded
certificate normal form can classify contextual models in general.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
By the covariance map~\cite{AvisImaiItoSasaki2005} the relevant polytopes are
cut/correlation polytopes, and by De~Loera--Onn universality~\cite{DeLoeraOnn2004}
every rational polytope is a face of a three-way transportation polytope, faces
realised as imposed zeros (``punctures''), i.e.\ as constraint varieties. The
\textsf{NP}-completeness floor is Pitowsky~\cite{Pitowsky1991}.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:universal} is the reason the classification is posed at the
\emph{protocol} level (a $\forall\exists$ question over structured families) and
not the model level: at the model level it is provably intractable. The
remainder of the paper classifies protocol families where structure defeats the
universality.


\section{The parity theorem}\label{sec:parity}

The cleanest cyclic case is the golden-mean shift: $\rho_{\mathrm{gm}}$ forbids
adjacent $1$s. Observe it around a ring of length $L$ (scope contexts = the $L$
edge-windows of the cycle $C_L$).

\begin{theorem}[parity]\label{thm:parity}
The golden-mean ring protocol on $C_L$ is commensurable iff $L$ is even.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
\emph{Reduction.} On the variety, each edge distribution is affine in the
endpoint vertex-marginals ($x_i=1$ forces $x_{i+1}=0$), and adjacent coherence
is exactly vertex-marginal matching. In marginal coordinates $C\cong\FSTAB(C_L)$
(the edge-constrained fractional stable-set polytope $\{u\ge0,\ u_i+u_{i+1}\le1\}$)
and $R\cong\STAB(C_L)$ (convex hull of independent-set indicators): homomorphisms
$C_L\to$ golden-mean are exactly independent sets.

\emph{Parity.} The incidence matrix of $C_L$ has determinant $1-(-1)^L$, and
every proper square submatrix is forest-structured, hence totally unimodular.
For $L$ even the matrix is TU, so $\FSTAB(C_L)$ is integral and equals
$\STAB(C_L)$: $C=R$, commensurable. For $L$ odd a fractional vertex must have
all coordinates positive (a zero coordinate lands on a path face, forest, TU,
integral), forcing every edge constraint tight and, by nonsingularity, the
unique fractional vertex $u\equiv\tfrac12$. Thus
$\FSTAB(C_L^{\mathrm{odd}})=\STAB(C_L)$ plus exactly one extra vertex: the
EA/PR gap on an odd golden-mean ring is a single point.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[the odd-ring PR-box]\label{cor:prbox}
The half-model $u\equiv\tfrac12$ is separated by the odd-cycle inequality
$\sum_i u_i \le (L-1)/2$, with relative violation $1/(L-1)$. It is strongly
contextual (contextual fraction $1$): any noncontextual part would need
$v_i+v_{i+1}\ge1$ on every edge, giving $2\sum v_i \ge L > L-1$. It is the
golden-mean ring's analogue of a PR-box.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[dynamical reading]\label{rem:parity-dynamics}
A cyclic scope structure is recurrence: sliding edge-windows on a period-$L$
orbit identify time modulo $L$. Theorem~\ref{thm:parity} says golden-mean
dynamics produces contextual scope data exactly on odd-period recurrence ---
alternation cannot close an odd ring, observed. The same odd-cycle facet is the
KCBS/pentagon coverage bound~\cite{KCBS2008}, reached here by recurrence rather
than by exclusivity: distinct observational routes to one polyhedral obstruction.
\end{remark}

The parity spectrum varies with the language: the order relation $\{\le\}$ is
tame on all rings; XOR is tame on even rings (odd rings have empty variety); the
full (unconstrained) language is tame on none. More constraint can mean more
tame --- the variety's rigidity can rescue commensurability the free world lacks.


\section{Tamings and Circuit Localization}\label{sec:tamings}

Each structured language that resists contextuality does so by a nameable
mechanism. We isolate ten; the running discipline was to catalogue the
\emph{reason} a candidate wild protocol turned out tame, and to register each
mechanism against a fresh prediction before testing it.

\begin{definition}[the taming mechanisms]\label{def:tamings}
A protocol/language is tamed by one of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{chains / empty-intersection} (\S\ref{sec:parity} base case);
  \item \emph{cross-context cliques};
  \item \emph{determinism}: a functional transfer relation;
  \item \emph{grading}: the transfer digraph is fibered over $\bbZ_d$;
  \item \emph{transient branching}: all branching lies off the recurrent core;
  \item \emph{direct-sum decomposition}: a disconnected relation splits
    componentwise;
  \item \emph{signability}: a Heller--Tompkins signing driven by the frame's
    bipartition, giving total unimodularity (König's theorem is the edge case);
  \item \emph{twin expansion}: expanding a non-reflexive state into label-twins,
    realised by a constructive $\theta$-sweep coupling;
  \item \emph{monotone collapse}: a non-symmetric relation induces monotone
    potentials that strong connectivity flattens (the SCC-reduction lemma);
  \item \emph{bipartite-target phase decoupling}: a bipartite-target language is
    a fibered join of two copies of its edge polytope over a global phase.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

Tamings (7), (8), (9), (10) are proved theorems with machine-corroborated
joints; (3)--(6) are structural reductions. The signable case is representative:

\begin{theorem}[signable languages are tame on bipartite frames]\label{thm:taming7}
Let a language be \emph{marginal-determined} (its cell simplex is affine in the
endpoint marginals) and \emph{signable} (the edge-constraint matrix admits a
$\{\pm1\}$ column signing along the frame's bipartition). Then on every bipartite
frame it is commensurable. The realisation is constructive: an anti-comonotone
$\theta$-sweep (threshold $\theta$ on one bipartition class, $1-\theta$ on the
other) produces an exact mixture of global configurations reproducing any
coherent point.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
In marginal coordinates the edge-nonnegativity rows admit a Heller--Tompkins
signing exactly when the frame is $2$-colourable, making the matrix totally
unimodular; integral right-hand sides then give integral vertices, so $C=R$. The
$\theta$-sweep is the explicit decomposition, a one-parameter alignment of the
two bipartition classes.
\end{proof}

A counting fact then closes the loopless symmetric case entirely.

\begin{theorem}[forest counting]\label{thm:forest}
A symmetric loopless language is marginal-determined iff, as a homomorphism
target, it is a forest. Every such language phase-decouples on bipartite frames.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Marginal-determination requires $|\rho|\le 2|A|-1$; a symmetric loopless
relation has $|\rho|=2|E(H)|$, forcing $|E(H)|\le|V(H)|-1$, i.e.\ $H$ a forest,
hence bipartite; Theorem~\ref{thm:taming7} and taming~(10) apply.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Circuit Localization, symmetric marginal-determined case]\label{thm:cl}
Every symmetric marginal-determined language is commensurable on every
gate-passing frame, decided by acyclicity together with the tamings: it is
either a forest target (Theorem~\ref{thm:forest}) or has loops within
signable/twin/decoupling reach. In particular no contextual protocol exists in
this class: the fork is excluded by structure, not by exhaustion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Acyclic frames are tame (Theorem~\ref{thm:acyclic}); cyclic symmetric targets
are unsafe at every gate-holding length (cyclic $H$ carries a witness-certified
frustration at essentially all usable lengths, so it cannot make every circuit
of a $K_4$-minor frame simultaneously safe --- the classically hard targets are
the opposite of a fork's habitat); the loopless case is forest-only
(Theorem~\ref{thm:forest}); the loopy case is exhausted by
signability/twin/decoupling with no residue.
\end{proof}


\section{The winding characterisation and its certified discriminant}\label{sec:winding}

Underlying the cyclic analysis is a structural characterisation. Lift a
length-$L$ ring protocol to its \emph{layered graph}: vertices $(i,a)$ for layer
$i\in\bbZ_L$ and state $a$, arcs $(i,a)\to(i{+}1,b)$ for legal pairs. A
$\{0,1\}$-valuation is contextual iff the coherence polytope has a fractional
vertex, and by flow decomposition the fractional vertices are the normalised
simple directed cycles of winding $\ge 2$.

\begin{theorem}[winding characterisation]\label{thm:winding}
Under the genericity gate (non-adjacent contexts share only trivial events), a
length $L$ is unsafe for $\rho$ iff the layered ring over $\bbZ_L$ carries a
layer-injective simple cycle of winding $\ge 2$. The characterisation is
structural; only its eventual-periodicity refinement (\S\ref{sec:open}) is
conjectural.
\end{theorem}

The proof turns on a discriminant that informal reasoning repeatedly
mishandled: a closed walk that ``closes up'' need not be a \emph{simple} cycle,
and the exact certificate of simplicity is layer-injectivity (each layer visited
in distinct states).

\begin{lemma}[layer-injectivity discriminant; Lean-certified]\label{lem:injectivity}
A layered-ring closed walk is a simple cycle (its vertex map is injective) iff it
is layer-injective (its state map is injective on each layer fibre).
\end{lemma}

This lemma is machine-verified in Lean~4
(\texttt{formalization/QuerySystem/QuerySystem/WindingInjectivity.lean};
statements \texttt{simple\_iff\_layerInjective},
\texttt{simple\_of\_layerInjective}, \texttt{not\_simple\_of\_collision}), with
zero \texttt{sorry}s, axioms exactly \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound},
and non-vacuity examples (a winding-2 collision proved non-simple, a
layer-injective trace proved simple). It is the first machine-certified result
of the reconstruction structure theory, and deliberately independent of the
open material of \S\ref{sec:open}: pure vertex-trace combinatorics.


\section{The open case}\label{sec:open}

The symmetric world is settled (\S\ref{sec:tamings}). The residence of any
remaining contextual protocol is the strongly-connected non-symmetric class. An
SCC-reduction lemma (taming (9), monotone collapse) reduces commensurability
wholesale to strongly connected languages; exhaustive search finds no contextual
protocol there at small scale (none at $\le 4$ states; none through $9$ edges at
$5$ states, at certificate grade). We record the conjecture and its status
honestly.

\begin{conjecture}[universal impossibility]\label{conj:universal}
The fork does not exist: Circuit Localization holds universally for total
languages on gate-passing, time-realisable frames. Equivalently, blocking the
frustrating exchange at a rich set of lengths forces one of the catalogued
tamings, each of which localises the polytope.
\end{conjecture}

The intended proof runs through eventual periodicity of the unsafe set (a finite
union of arithmetic progressions, Wielandt-bounded), which reduces to a
combinatorial \emph{pruning lemma}.

\begin{conjecture}[pruning lemma]\label{conj:pruning}
Let $\TR_k$ be the lengths admitting a diagonal-avoiding tuple-rotation walk in
the $k$-fold tensor power, and $\LISC_k$ the lengths admitting a layer-injective
simple cycle of winding $k$. Then $\TR_k=\LISC_k$ cofinitely.
\end{conjecture}

The inclusion $\LISC_k\subseteq\TR_k$ is immediate. The reverse reduces, at
$k=2$, to a flow-decomposition statement (a winding-$2$ circulation is a single
winding-$2$ cycle iff its two strands are exchange-linked); we record explicitly
that this reduction currently drops a \emph{relative-phase} degree of freedom
between the strands (a winding-$2$ simple cycle interleaves its wraps at a phase
a lockstep exchange cannot express), so the corrected $k=2$ residual is
phase-parametrised strand-disjointness. Empirically no $\TR_k\setminus\LISC_k$
disagreement appears across an adversarial stress set to layered length $35$
(including validated-imprimitive digraphs, where the natural counterexample was
predicted and did not occur), which is evidence, not proof. The conjecture is
open; its proof is not to be assembled until the pruning lemma is settled
structurally.


\section{Consequences for reconstruction}\label{sec:consequences}

The classification licenses a reconstruction guarantee, scoped honestly.

\paragraph{The tame classes give a decidable guarantee.} For acyclic reporting
(Theorem~\ref{thm:acyclic}), for symmetric marginal-determined languages
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cl}), and for even-cyclic golden-mean-type recurrence
(Theorem~\ref{thm:parity}), coherent window-statistics \emph{do} determine a
global process, and the winding characterisation (Theorem~\ref{thm:winding})
supplies a decidable criterion --- the presence of a layer-injective winding-$\ge2$
cycle --- separating the safe protocols from the contextual ones. This is the
licensing layer beneath process reconstruction (``statistical Takens'':
recovery of a measure-plus-shift, not a manifold), with an explicit boundary on
when reconstruction is possible.

\paragraph{The boundary is intrinsic, not accidental.} By
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal}, general variety-typed protocols are as hard as
arbitrary rational polytope membership; contextuality in observation is not a
pathology to be engineered away but an intrinsic feature of observing-through-
windows, present exactly when the observation geometry admits the winding
obstruction. Where Conjecture~\ref{conj:universal} holds, the guarantee is
unconditional for total languages: no protocol over such a world manufactures
contextual data unless the decidable exchange criterion fires.

\paragraph{One phenomenon, three faces.} The failure of local coherence to
globalise appears here dynamically, in Paper~II algebraically (dispersion-free
states and the collapse of $\PR$ on non-distributive logics), and in the
$\sigma$-essential witness set-theoretically (a finitely-coherent pattern with
no $\sigma$-additive global state). The dynamical statement is the sharpest:
\emph{frustration is an unwitnessed exchange --- two histories that trade places
around the clock of observation without meeting --- and contextual data is the
shadow of an unresolved covering of time.}


\section*{Note on method and verification}\label{sec:method}

Every commensurability verdict in \S\S\ref{sec:trichotomy}--\ref{sec:winding} is
derived from a theorem or an exact rational enumeration; every contextuality
verdict from an exhibited rational witness in $C\setminus R$; sampling-based
checks are used only as screening and never as the basis of a ``commensurable''
claim. Lemma~\ref{lem:injectivity} is machine-checked in Lean~4. The
constructive tamings ($\theta$-sweep, phase-mixture, monotone collapse,
empty-intersection) are the queued targets for further certification. The open
material of \S\ref{sec:open} is stated as conjecture with its reduction gap named.

\section{The target: when do coherent windows determine a process?}\label{sec:target}

A process in time is often seen only through \emph{windows}: overlapping finite
views, each recording finitely many coordinates over finitely many steps. Each
window yields a local probability table. The tables may agree wherever they
overlap and still be produced by no single underlying process. The question of
this paper is when that cannot happen.

\begin{namedgoal}[Reconstruction guarantee --- the target]\label{goal:recon}
For a structured class of observation protocols, locally coherent window-statistics
are induced by a single global process; and the boundary of that class ---
where coherence stops forcing a global process --- is exactly decidable.
\end{namedgoal}

\noindent\emph{Status of the target.} The guarantee is established
\textbf{unconditionally} on three tame families (\S\ref{sec:boundary}), and
\textbf{conditionally} --- on a single open conjecture (\S\ref{sec:capstone}) ---
for all total symbolic languages. The decidability of the boundary is
\textbf{[PROVED]} for a fixed observation length via the winding criterion
(\S\ref{sec:boundary}), \textbf{[SCOPE: winding-2; the lift to full safety is
spot-checked, not proved]}.

The rest of the paper is the dependency chain beneath
Goal~\ref{goal:recon}: the geometric reduction that makes ``coherence forces a
process'' computable (\S\ref{sec:geometry}), why it cannot be settled at the level
of individual models (\S\ref{sec:hardness}), the criterion that draws the boundary
(\S\ref{sec:boundary}), and the conjecture that would make the guarantee
unconditional (\S\ref{sec:capstone}).


\section{The geometric reduction}\label{sec:geometry}

We fix the minimal vocabulary once, one object per sentence, and use nothing before
it is defined.

A finite \emph{alphabet} $A$ and a finite index set $F\subseteq\bbZ$ give a
\emph{context}: a partition of $A^F$ into cells. A \emph{protocol} is a finite
family of contexts. \emph{Window-data} assigns a probability to each cell of each
context. A \emph{symbolic language} is a relation $\rho\subseteq A\times A$ listing
the legal adjacent pairs of a shift of finite type; a protocol \emph{reports} on
the configurations legal for $\rho$.

\begin{definition}[coherence, realisability, commensurability]\label{def:core}
Window-data is \emph{empirically adequate} ($\EA$) if any two contexts agree on the
events common to both. It is \emph{realisable in a class} $\Rc$ ($\PR(\Rc)$) if some
member of $\Rc$ --- all measures, or stationary, or subshift-supported, or
memory-$m$ Markov: an observer's declared commitment --- induces it. Data is
\emph{contextual} when $\EA$ but not $\PR(\Rc)$. A protocol is \emph{commensurable}
when every $\EA$ datum is $\PR(\Rc)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[commensurability is a polytope equality]\label{prop:cr}
The $\EA$ data form a polytope $C$ (the coherence polytope) and the $\PR(\Rc)$ data
a polytope $R\subseteq C$ (the realisability polytope, the convex hull of the global
configurations' cell-incidence vectors). A protocol is commensurable iff $C=R$; a
contextual datum is a point of $C\setminus R$, and over rational data a separating
facet certifies it.
\end{proposition}

\noindent\textbf{[PROVED, CLASSICAL polyhedral content.]} This is the marginal
polytope of the Abramsky--Brandenburger presheaf model. Its force here is
methodological: ``coherence forces a process'' becomes the exactly computable
question $C=R$, decided by a proof of equality or an exhibited rational witness in
$C\setminus R$.


\section{Why the question lives at the protocol level}\label{sec:hardness}

The reduction $C=R$ is computable per protocol but not classifiable per model: the
achievable geometry is already universal.

\begin{theorem}[trichotomy]\label{thm:trichotomy}
Reporting splits into three regimes. \emph{(Acyclic)} If the reporting hypergraph
is acyclic, the protocol is commensurable for every $\rho$. \emph{(Product)}
Full-support coordinate protocols are commensurable. \emph{(Variety-typed)}
Protocols carrying a constraint variety realise, as their coherence polytopes,
every rational polytope up to affine equivalence.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{[PROVED; the three parts are CLASSICAL translations]} ---
acyclic\,=\,Vorob'ev/running-intersection; product\,=\,product measure;
variety-typed\,=\,De~Loera--Onn universality with the Pitowsky
\textsf{NP}-hardness floor. \emph{Consequence, and the reason for the paper's
altitude:} no finite facet taxonomy classifies contextual \emph{models}, so the
classification is posed one level up --- a $\forall\exists$ question over
structured protocol \emph{families} --- where structure can defeat the
universality. Everything below characterises such families.


\section{The boundary: the winding criterion}\label{sec:boundary}

This is the spine. Read a cyclic protocol as recurrence: observe $\rho$ around a
ring of length $L$. Lift the ring to its \emph{layered graph} --- vertices $(i,a)$
for site $i\in\bbZ_L$ and state $a\in A$, arcs $(i,a)\to(i{+}1,b)$ for legal pairs
$(a,b)$. The \emph{winding} of a closed walk is its net site-advance divided by
$L$. Call a length $L$ \emph{unsafe} for $\rho$ if the length-$L$ ring carries
contextual data.

\begin{theorem}[winding criterion --- the decidable boundary]\label{thm:winding}
A length $L$ is unsafe for $\rho$ iff the layered ring carries a layer-injective
simple cycle of winding $\ge 2$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{[PROVED for fixed $L$]}, via three obligations, each discharged in
the archive: a \emph{gate identification} ($C$ is the layered graph's circulation
polytope) \textbf{[PROVED; the genericity gate is vacuous for the ring used]}; a
\emph{flow decomposition} (a polytope vertex is a single simple cycle)
\textbf{[PROVED on a CLASSICAL, axiomatised decomposition]}; and a
\emph{layer-injectivity discriminant} (that cycle is layer-injective)
\textbf{[PROVED, machine-verified in Lean~4]}. \textbf{[SCOPE: the criterion is
about winding-2 obstructions; that this captures full safety rests on the pruning
lemma, \S\ref{sec:capstone}, and is spot-checked only.]}

\paragraph{Instances of the criterion (deferred; proved in the archive).}
The criterion is exercised, not merely stated, on worked families. The
\emph{parity theorem} --- the golden-mean shift on a ring is commensurable iff $L$
is even, the odd-ring gap a single extreme point that is its own PR-box --- is the
smallest nontrivial instance. A \emph{catalogue of ten taming mechanisms} gives
sufficient conditions for the winding obstruction never to form, organised as
terminal integrality certificates versus reductions to one. Assembled, these yield
\emph{Circuit Localization} for symmetric loopless marginal-determined languages,
where contextuality is excluded by structure rather than search. These are
\textbf{[PROVED]} and belong in the full paper; here they are the evidence that the
boundary is real and computable, not the target.


\section{The capstone: universal impossibility}\label{sec:capstone}

The winding criterion decides each length. The catalogue of \S\ref{sec:boundary}
gives sufficient conditions for a language to be safe at \emph{all} lengths. Whether
those conditions are also \emph{necessary} --- whether the catalogue is complete ---
is the open capstone that would make Goal~\ref{goal:recon} unconditional.

\begin{conjecture}[universal impossibility]\label{conj:universal}
No \emph{fork} exists: every total language is either caught by a catalogued taming
or is contextual at cofinitely many lengths. Equivalently, the winding criterion is
a complete decision procedure and the reconstruction guarantee is unconditional on
the total languages.
\end{conjecture}

\noindent\textbf{[OPEN].} The symmetric case is settled by Circuit Localization;
the residue is the strongly-connected non-symmetric class, where exhaustive search
finds no fork at small scale. The intended route reduces the conjecture to a single
named lemma.

\begin{namedlemma}[the forcing lemma --- the one open step]\label{lem:forcing}
On a primitive language, an infinite safe set forces a parity obstruction: the
signed pair-graph of the language is balanced (equivalently, its residue class is
the even lengths), the only alternative being a finite safe set.
\end{namedlemma}

\noindent\textbf{[OPEN; VALIDATED, not proved].} The forcing lemma is exactly
``the tamings are the only sources of residue-safety in a primitive language.''
Its two halves are: a \emph{re-characterisation} (residue-safety $=$ balance of a
signed pair-graph) \textbf{[PROVED, structural]}; and the \emph{forcing} proper
(primitivity excludes the mixed cases) \textbf{[OPEN]}, framed as two
Perron--Frobenius exclusions and checked without counterexample across several
thousand primitive languages. Discharging it, together with the winding-2-to-full
bridge, closes Conjecture~\ref{conj:universal}. \textbf{This lemma is the single
result the paper is aiming to prove or to state as its open problem.}


\section{Consequences}\label{sec:consequences}

On the tame classes of \S\ref{sec:boundary} --- acyclic reporting, symmetric
marginal-determined languages, even-cyclic golden-mean recurrence --- coherent
window-statistics determine a global process (Goal~\ref{goal:recon},
unconditional), and the winding criterion makes the boundary decidable. This is the
licensing layer beneath process reconstruction in the statistical (measure-plus-shift)
rather than manifold sense of Takens. By the universality of \S\ref{sec:hardness}
the boundary is intrinsic, so contextuality is a feature of observing through
windows: present exactly when the geometry admits the winding obstruction. Where
Conjecture~\ref{conj:universal} holds, no total language manufactures contextuality
unless the exchange criterion fires.

The same failure of local coherence to globalise recurs across the programme:
algebraically in Paper~II (dispersion-free states collapsing realisability on
non-distributive logics) and set-theoretically in the $\sigma$-essential witness
(a finitely coherent pattern with no $\sigma$-additive global state). Dynamically
it is sharpest --- an unwitnessed exchange, two histories trading places around the
clock of observation without meeting.


\section*{Positioning and method}\label{sec:method}

\textbf{Contribution type.} A bridge (Type~6): the polyhedral core is classical
(marginal polytopes, stable-set integrality, De~Loera--Onn universality,
signed-minor tightness); the novelty is the observational-dynamical framing, the
winding criterion, and the Circuit-Localization assembly. This positioning is
stated, not hedged.

\textbf{Method.} Each commensurability verdict is a theorem or an exact rational
enumeration; each contextuality verdict an exhibited rational witness in
$C\setminus R$; sampling is only screening. The layer-injectivity discriminant and
the flow-decomposition kernel are machine-checked in Lean~4.

\textbf{What is owed to fill this skeleton to a complete paper.} (i) The forcing
lemma (Lemma~\ref{lem:forcing}): prove it, or state it as the paper's open problem.
(ii) The winding-2-to-full-safety bridge: prove it applies, or scope every
boundary claim to winding-2. (iii) Fold the archived proved body
(parity, PR-box, the ten tamings, Circuit Localization, the three winding
obligations) back in as \S\ref{sec:boundary}'s instances, ordered define-at-first-use.

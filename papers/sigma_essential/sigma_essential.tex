\title{A $\sigma$-Essential Contextual State in ZFC:\\
Localization and the Product Ulam Witness}
\author{Patrick S.\ Mahon\thanks{%
  Research Computing Group, Simon Fraser University, Burnaby, BC, Canada.
  \texttt{pmahon@sfu.ca}}}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A two-valued state on a concrete quantum logic of subsets of a set $\Omega$
assigns a definite truth value to each event, coherently across overlapping
measurement contexts. On a Boolean $\sigma$-algebra every finitely coherent
local pattern of such assignments extends to a global $\sigma$-additive
two-valued state. We ask whether the non-distributive case can differ: does
there exist a concrete $\sigma$-complete non-Boolean orthomodular poset
carrying a finitely coherent local pattern that extends to \emph{no} global
$\sigma$-additive two-valued state --- a \emph{$\sigma$-essential contextual
state}? We first localize the question: a witness exists iff the pattern is
finitely coherent while no point evaluation and no non-principal
$\sigma$-additive state extends it, and we map its boundary --- empty in the
Polish-representable case (Derr--Williamson), irreducible to the Hilbert-space
dichotomy of Blecher--Weaver, irreducible to a measurable cardinal. We then
answer it affirmatively in ZFC. The witness is a \emph{product Ulam carrier} on
$\Omega=\omega_1\times\{1,2,3,4\}$: an Ulam matrix imported as generators
makes every $\sigma$-additive two-valued state a point evaluation, a Specker
triple of fibre events has empty kernel, and a trace-invariant normal form
shows the two mechanisms cannot interact, so that a finitely additive extension
survives, built from an arbitrary ultrafilter on
$\mathcal P(\omega_1)/\mathrm{ctble}$ through a parity code. Consequently the
target sentence carries no large-cardinal strength. The quotient of the carrier
by the countable ideal is a $\sigma$-complete non-Boolean orthomodular poset
with finitely additive two-valued states but no $\sigma$-additive ones at all.
The lattice (OML) form of the question remains open, and we conjecture it fails:
latticehood appears to force $\sigma$-liftability.
\end{abstract}

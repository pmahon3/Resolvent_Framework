## The near-neighbor (MUST-CITE, found by the scout)

**Binder & Navara, "Quantum logics with lattice state spaces", Proc. AMS 100
(1987), 688–693, Theorem 4** — read in full text. Their construction: X
countable concrete logic, Y = U × X with |U| = ℵ₁, D = {M ⊆ Y : T(M)
countable} (T(M) = the set of u with bad fiber). (Y,D) is a concrete
σ-complete logic squeezed between σ-algebras, and **every σ-additive state on
it is carried by a countable set** — via Solovay/Ulam on P(ω₁). So:
- **The carrier shape (ω₁ × countable fiber, countable-ideal structure) and
  the Ulam σ-rigidity component are OWNED by Binder–Navara 1987** and must be
  cited (Navara's Trieste 2000 survey §17, Thm 17.4, is the modern statement;
  he draws the "almost Boolean" moral — lattice state space, Jauch–Piron).
- What they do NOT have (checked line-by-line): no finitely additive side at
  all (their "state" is σ-additive by definition; no ultrafilters, no charges,
  no coherence); no Specker pattern / partial-pattern extension failure; no
  parity/vote construction; no quotient statement.

**Consequence for the write-up:** the honest framing shifts from "we build a
carrier" to "on (a fibered refinement of) the Binder–Navara carrier, the
finitely-additive two-valued theory is NON-degenerate: a coherent empty-kernel
Specker pattern exists (the parity vote), while their σ-rigidity kills every
σ-additive extension." The new content = components (2)+(3) + the conjunction.

\section{The observer-first reading}\label{sec:moral}

An observer running a protocol obtains, natively, a family of finite relative
frequencies that cohere across overlapping views. That is all observation
supplies. Two further properties are routinely assumed of the state such data
``comes from'' --- that it is countably additive, and that it assigns definite
truth values --- but neither is given by the data. They are \emph{representation
properties}: signatures of the world being observed and of the protocol used to
observe it, not of empirical adequacy itself. The programme these three papers
form is the price list for those signatures: what structure a world's language
and an observer's protocol must have before countable additivity and
value-definiteness come for free, and --- in two cases settled by explicit
construction --- proofs that outside that structure the signatures can be
genuinely absent while coherence remains.

This note is an umbrella. It states the shared object, tabulates how the three
papers divide the labour, records the corpus-level commonalities and the
genuinely corpus-level open questions, and points to each paper for the
mathematics. It deliberately does not merge them: each paper travels to its own
audience, and the spine carries only what no single paper can say alone.


\section{The filtration and its three transitions}\label{sec:filtration}

The companion paper on distributivity and realism introduces the organising
object: on the state space of an observation algebra there is a natural
filtration
\[
  \Sf \;\supseteq\; \Ssig \;\supseteq\; \Sdf,
\]
from all coherent states, to the $\sigma$-additive ones, to the dispersion-free
(value-definite) ones. Its three inclusions are transitions of different
mathematical character, named there:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \emph{pasting} --- from locally coherent window-data up to a single
    globally realising state ($\EA \to \PR$);
  \item \emph{regularity} --- from a finitely additive global state up to a
    countably additive one ($\PR_{\mathrm{f.a.}} \to \PR_\sigma$);
  \item \emph{sharpness} --- from a $\sigma$-additive state up to a
    value-definite one ($\PR \to \VDR$).
\end{enumerate}
Distributivity collapses the filtration --- on a Boolean algebra all three
transitions are free --- and non-distributivity can open any of them. The
corpus-level observation this note records is that \textbf{each companion paper
is the deep study of exactly one transition}, and that the three locate the
boundary of their transition in three different coordinate systems.

The table is the weave. One question --- \emph{when does empirical adequacy
purchase realism?} --- is answered as a classification, with the boundary located
algebraically (distributivity), set-theoretically (beyond Polish, at $\omega_1$),
and dynamically (odd recurrence). Two of the separating witnesses are new
constructions; the third (Kochen--Specker) is classical, reframed as the
sharpness-transition obstruction.


\section{What is genuinely common}\label{sec:common}

Three things hold across all three papers and belong to none of them alone.

\paragraph{Non-distributivity as the discriminator.} In each transition the tame
side is, at root, a distributive (or distributively-representable) regime, and
the wild side is entered through non-distributivity: Kochen--Specker needs
$\dim H \ge 3$; the $\sigma$-essential witness is provably not a lattice; the
pasting failures are carried by non-distributive constraint geometry. That
non-distributivity is the tame/wild discriminator in \emph{every} transition is a
corpus-level pattern no single paper isolates.

\paragraph{The observer-first price list.} Section~\ref{sec:moral}: the three
papers are, together, the statement that $\sigma$-additivity and
value-definiteness are representation properties with a computable price, and two
proofs that the price is real (the two constructed witnesses).

\paragraph{Method.} The corpus shares a working method, stated once here without
ceremony: predictions are pre-registered before testing; a verdict of
``realisable/tame'' is admitted only from a theorem or an exact enumeration, of
``contextual/wild'' only from an exhibited witness; and load-bearing
combinatorial lemmas are machine-checked (the $\sigma$-essential main theorem and
the commensurability paper's layer-injectivity discriminant are certified in
Lean~4).

\paragraph{The novelty ledger, honestly.} The classical mathematics each paper
uses is credited there as classical (Gleason and Kochen--Specker for sharpness;
Derr--Williamson, Ulam, and the Boolean baseline for regularity;
Wainwright--Jordan, Vorob'ev, De~Loera--Onn and stable-set theory for pasting).
The corpus's own contribution is the observer-first framing, the filtration as
its organising object, and two constructed witnesses where the literature had
only imported or absent ones. This is a bridge over known mathematics with two
new construction points; the umbrella claims no more.


\section{The corpus-level open questions}\label{sec:open}

Two bridging claims would strengthen the weave from a tabulation into a single
theorem. Neither is established; we state them as the corpus's open problems, and
flag that asserting either prematurely would misrepresent the corpus.

\begin{conjecture}[one coherence notion across rungs]\label{conj:onenotion}
The local coherence notions of the three papers --- the commensurability paper's
$\EA$ (pairwise agreement on common subalgebras), the $\sigma$-essential paper's
\emph{finite coherence} (extendability to a global finitely additive two-valued
state), and the top rung $\Sf$ of the filtration --- are one notion evaluated at
different rungs.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[status of Conjecture~\ref{conj:onenotion}]\label{rem:onenotion}
As \emph{stated in the three papers} these notions are not literally identical:
$\EA$ is a pairwise-local condition, finite coherence is a global finite-additive
extendability condition, and they are known to coincide only under further
hypotheses. The papers exhibit the same \emph{phenomenon} --- coherence that
fails to globalise --- at adjacent rungs, which is a genuine rhyme; whether it is
a single notion (transfer) rather than three instances of a family (rhyme) is
open, and the corpus should claim the rhyme, not the identity, until the
coincidence is proved.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[the quarantine hinge]\label{conj:quarantine}
The two constructed witnesses inhabit provably disjoint regimes: a compactness
argument (finite-alphabet delay logics are compact/clopen, so clopen charges are
measures and finite certificates suffice by Farkas) shows the $\sigma$-essential
pathology cannot occur in the dynamical (finite-alphabet) regime where the pasting
witness lives. If so, the corpus does not merely exhibit two failures --- it
proves they are sorted, upgrading the tabulation to a classification.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[status of Conjecture~\ref{conj:quarantine}]\label{rem:quarantine}
The compactness/Farkas argument is currently a sketch (a roadmap ``Layer-1
licensing'' result in the commensurability programme), not an audited proof.
Until it is settled, the spine's central architectural claim --- that the two
witnesses live in provably disjoint worlds, so the failures are classified rather
than merely collected --- is a conjectural hinge. We state it as the corpus's
most valuable next lemma precisely because it is what would make the weave a
theorem.
\end{remark}

A third open thread is shared and worth stating uniformly: in each transition,
\emph{latticehood} appears to force liftability (and hence tameness) --- the
orthomodular-lattice form of each paper's boundary question is conjectured to
collapse to the tame side. Whether these are three phrasings of one conjecture
(latticehood $\Rightarrow$ liftability $\Rightarrow$ tameness) is itself part of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:onenotion}.


\section{Reading and dissemination order}\label{sec:order}

For a reader: distributivity \& realism first (it introduces the filtration),
then $\sigma$-essential (the deepest single result), then commensurability (the
broadest). For dissemination the crown result travels first and alone: the
$\sigma$-essential paper is self-contained and Lean-certified, and its
Derr--Williamson cell-closure framing does not depend on the other two. The
commensurability paper follows; the distributivity paper may belong to a
different venue class (foundations of physics) than the two mathematics papers.
The spine lets each paper travel light while carrying, itself, only the
corpus-level content: the filtration as shared object, the observer-first moral,
and the two open bridging questions of \S\ref{sec:open}.

\section{The observer-first reading}\label{sec:moral}

An observer running a protocol obtains, natively, a family of finite relative
frequencies that cohere across overlapping views. That is all observation
supplies. Two further properties are routinely assumed of the state such data
``comes from'' --- that it is countably additive, and that it assigns definite
truth values --- but neither is given by the data. They are \emph{representation
properties}: signatures of the world being observed and of the protocol used to
observe it, not of empirical adequacy itself. The programme these three papers
form is the price list for those signatures: what structure a world's language
and an observer's protocol must have before countable additivity and
value-definiteness come for free, and --- in two cases settled by explicit
construction --- proofs that outside that structure the signatures can be
genuinely absent while coherence remains.

This note is an umbrella. It states the shared object, tabulates how the three
papers divide the labour, records the corpus-level commonalities and the
genuinely corpus-level open questions, and points to each paper for the
mathematics. It deliberately does not merge them: each paper travels to its own
audience, and the spine carries only what no single paper can say alone.


\section{The filtration and its three transitions}\label{sec:filtration}

The companion paper on distributivity and realism introduces the organising
object: on the state space of an observation algebra there is a natural
filtration
\[
  \Sf \;\supseteq\; \Ssig \;\supseteq\; \Sdf,
\]
from all coherent states, to the $\sigma$-additive ones, to the dispersion-free
(value-definite) ones. Its three inclusions are transitions of different
mathematical character, named there:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \emph{pasting} --- from locally coherent window-data up to a single
    globally realising state ($\EA \to \PR$);
  \item \emph{regularity} --- from a finitely additive global state up to a
    countably additive one ($\PR_{\mathrm{f.a.}} \to \PR_\sigma$);
  \item \emph{sharpness} --- from a $\sigma$-additive state up to a
    value-definite one ($\PR \to \VDR$).
\end{enumerate}
Distributivity collapses the filtration --- on a Boolean algebra all three
transitions are free --- and non-distributivity can open any of them. The
corpus-level observation this note records is that \textbf{each companion paper
is the deep study of exactly one transition}, and that the three locate the
boundary of their transition in three different coordinate systems.

The table is the weave. One question --- \emph{when does empirical adequacy
purchase realism?} --- is answered as a classification (made precise by the tame
tower of Corollary~\ref{cor:tower}), with the boundary located algebraically
(distributivity), set-theoretically (beyond Polish, at $\omega_1$), and
dynamically (odd recurrence). Two of the separating witnesses are new
constructions; the third (Kochen--Specker) is classical, reframed as the
sharpness-transition obstruction.


\section{What is genuinely common}\label{sec:common}

Three things hold across all three papers and belong to none of them alone.

\paragraph{Non-distributivity as the discriminator.} In each transition the tame
side is, at root, a distributive (or distributively-representable) regime, and
the wild side is entered through non-distributivity: Kochen--Specker needs
$\dim H \ge 3$; the $\sigma$-essential witness is provably not a lattice; the
pasting failures are carried by non-distributive constraint geometry. That
non-distributivity is the tame/wild discriminator in \emph{every} transition is a
corpus-level pattern no single paper isolates.

\paragraph{The observer-first price list.} Section~\ref{sec:moral}: the three
papers are, together, the statement that $\sigma$-additivity and
value-definiteness are representation properties with a computable price, and two
proofs that the price is real (the two constructed witnesses).

\paragraph{Method.} The corpus shares a working method, stated once here without
ceremony: predictions are pre-registered before testing; a verdict of
``realisable/tame'' is admitted only from a theorem or an exact enumeration, of
``contextual/wild'' only from an exhibited witness; and load-bearing
combinatorial lemmas are machine-checked (the $\sigma$-essential main theorem and
the commensurability paper's layer-injectivity discriminant are certified in
Lean~4).

\paragraph{The novelty ledger, honestly.} The classical mathematics each paper
uses is credited there as classical (Gleason and Kochen--Specker for sharpness;
Derr--Williamson, Ulam, and the Boolean baseline for regularity;
Wainwright--Jordan, Vorob'ev, De~Loera--Onn and stable-set theory for pasting).
The corpus's own contribution is the observer-first framing, the filtration as
its organising object, and two constructed witnesses where the literature had
only imported or absent ones. This is a bridge over known mathematics with two
new construction points; the umbrella claims no more.


\section{Two bridging results and the remaining conjecture}\label{sec:open}

The two bridging claims that turn the tabulation of \S\ref{sec:filtration} into a
single classification are settled here --- one negatively (the coherence notions
are a strictly separated ladder, not one notion), one positively (a compactness
quarantine sorts the two constructed witnesses into disjoint regimes) --- leaving
a single open conjecture (the latticehood family).

\begin{proposition}[the two-gap ladder]\label{prop:ladder}
The coherence notions of the three papers form a strictly separated ladder,
\[
  \text{pairwise-}\EA \;\supsetneq\; \text{finite coherence}
  \;\supsetneq\; \sigma\text{-realizability},
\]
and both strict inclusions are inhabited by the corpus's own witnesses. The first
gap: the golden-mean odd-ring half-model (commensurability programme,
machine-verified) --- the pentagon protocol $C_5$ over $\{0,1\}^5$ with the
half-model $s(a,b)=\tfrac12$ for $a\neq b$ and $0$ for $a=b$ --- is pairwise-$\EA$
yet admits \emph{no} finitely additive state on its generated closure: its
support would have to be a proper $2$-colouring of an odd cycle, of which there
are none (equivalently, the stable-set bound $\sum_j \mu(a_j)\le\alpha(C_5)=2$ is
exceeded by the half-model's value $5/2$). The second gap: the product Ulam
carrier's pattern is finitely coherent with no $\sigma$-additive extension. Hence
the three papers' notions are not one notion evaluated at different rungs; they
are the three rungs, provably distinct.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
The correct corpus-level claim is therefore the rhyme, now made exact: each paper
studies coherence-at-a-rung, the rungs are strictly ordered, and each strict
inclusion is witnessed inside the corpus. What survives of the ``one notion''
intuition is structural, not extensional: every rung is a finite-additive gluing
condition over a widening class of admissible families.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[quarantine]\label{thm:quarantine}
Let $X$ be a compact zero-dimensional metrizable space and $\mathrm{Clo}(X)$ its
clopen algebra.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item Every finitely additive two-valued state $s$ on any subalgebra
  $B \subseteq \mathrm{Clo}(X)$ is the restriction of a point evaluation.
  Consequently a finitely coherent two-valued pattern over clopen events always
  has a realizing point: clauses \textup{(0)} and \textup{(i)} of the
  $\sigma$-essential recipe cannot coexist, and the $\sigma$-essential phenomenon
  is impossible over compact clopen carriers.
\item Every finitely additive probability charge on $\mathrm{Clo}(X)$ is a
  premeasure and extends uniquely to a Borel probability measure on $X$.
\item Every charge on a subalgebra of $\mathrm{Clo}(X)$ --- in particular on the
  algebra generated by any protocol's contexts --- extends to $\mathrm{Clo}(X)$,
  hence realizes as a Borel measure. The only obstruction to realization in the
  dynamical regime is finite-additive.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) $F=\{C \in B : s(C)=1\}$ is a filter of $B$ (if $s(C)=s(D)=1$ then
$s(C\cap D) + s(C\cup D) = s(C)+s(D) = 2$ forces $s(C\cap D)=1$, and $s$ is
monotone with $s(\varnothing)=0$); extend $F$ to an ultrafilter $U$ of
$\mathrm{Clo}(X)$. $U$ is a family of nonempty closed subsets of a compact space
with the finite intersection property, so some $x \in \bigcap U$; then
$s(C)=1 \Rightarrow x \in C$ and
$s(C)=0 \Rightarrow s(C^{\perp})=1 \Rightarrow x \notin C$, i.e.\
$s=\delta_x\!\restriction\!B$. (ii) If clopen $C = \bigsqcup_n C_n$ with all
$C_n$ clopen, compactness of $C$ and disjointness of the open cover force all but
finitely many $C_n$ empty, so $\sigma$-additivity on $\mathrm{Clo}(X)$ reduces to
finite additivity; the Carath\'eodory--Hahn extension then gives a Borel measure
on $\sigma(\mathrm{Clo}(X))$, which is the Borel $\sigma$-algebra since the clopen
sets form a basis, uniquely (the measure is finite, hence $\sigma$-finite; $\pi$--$\lambda$).
(iii) Charge extension from a subalgebra
(Horn--Tarski~\cite{HornTarski1948}, in the finitely additive category; their
countably additive analogue can fail, which is why part (ii) supplies
$\sigma$-additivity separately), then (ii); see Rao--Rao~\cite{RaoRao1983} for the
charge/Stone-space correspondence.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[the tame tower; the witnesses are sorted]\label{cor:tower}
The tame results of the corpus form a tower:
finite carriers \textup{(Wright; the Boolean baseline)} $\subseteq$
compact clopen carriers \textup{(Theorem~\ref{thm:quarantine})} $\subseteq$
Polish-representable carriers \textup{(Derr--Williamson)}. In particular the two
constructed witnesses inhabit provably disjoint regimes --- the pasting witness
lives on a compact carrier where the regularity transition is free, and the
regularity witness lives at $\omega_1$, past the tower --- so the corpus's table
is a classification, not a collection.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[why the witness escapes; scope; credit]\label{rem:quarantine-scope}
Theorem~\ref{thm:quarantine}(i) is the Boolean baseline's atom argument with
``finite'' upgraded to ``compact'': the same proof at two scales, and the
compactness hypothesis is exactly what the $\sigma$-essential witness escapes by
living at $\omega_1$, where the clopen family loses the finite intersection
property. The quarantine and the regularity witness are therefore consistent, not
in tension: the theorem proves the witness \emph{cannot} descend to the compact
dynamical regime. The theorem quarantines realization by \emph{measures}; whether
exotic $\sigma$-classes generated inside a Borel structure satisfy the lifting
property $\Phi$ is the $\sigma$-essential paper's open characterization question,
untouched here. Parts (ii)--(iii) are Stone duality for
charges~\cite{HornTarski1948,RaoRao1983}, classical; the corpus's contribution is
part (i)'s pointed form, the tower, and the sorting.
\end{remark}

The one surviving bridging conjecture, now decoupled from the resolved ladder
question, is the \emph{latticehood family}: in each transition,
\emph{latticehood} appears to force liftability, hence tameness. Whether the
orthomodular-lattice form of each paper's boundary question collapses to the tame
side, and whether the three are one conjecture
(latticehood $\Rightarrow$ liftability $\Rightarrow$ tameness), is the corpus's
single open bridging frontier.


\section{Reading and dissemination order}\label{sec:order}

For a reader: distributivity \& realism first (it introduces the filtration),
then $\sigma$-essential (the deepest single result), then commensurability (the
broadest). For dissemination the crown result travels first and alone: the
$\sigma$-essential paper is self-contained and Lean-certified, and its
Derr--Williamson cell-closure framing does not depend on the other two. The
commensurability paper follows; the distributivity paper may belong to a
different venue class (foundations of physics) than the two mathematics papers.
The spine lets each paper travel light while carrying, itself, only the
corpus-level content: the filtration as shared object, the observer-first moral,
and the two open bridging questions of \S\ref{sec:open}.
